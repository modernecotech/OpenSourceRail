# Separate magnetic-support research

<!-- osr-system-context:start -->
OpenSourceRail v0.6 develops simple, affordable, locally maintainable station-powered rail. Six 17 m comfort cars carry 44 seats plus two wheelchair places/car and no planned standing; the 120-second target gives 8,280 passengers/hour/direction. Passive steel-wheel bogies run on conventional rails carried by precast concrete. Station LIMs act on passive onboard secondaries; there are no traction motors, traction batteries or suspension magnets onboard. Service batteries, independent brakes and the existing station-led GoA4 architecture remain. The reference design is research only; city numerical regeneration, economics and physical release remain open.

This directory contains experimental models for magnetic support and guidance.
They are separate research configurations and do not change the selected
wheel-supported, station-powered reference or inherit its candidate identity,
mass, performance or acceptance evidence.

Any future comparison must define its complete vehicle, propulsion, protection,
recovery, manufacturing and whole-system service consequences. The current
engineering front door is the [v0.6 dossier](../../docs/design-engine/current/README.md)
and [coordinated railway workflow](../design_search/README.md). Magnetic-support
models in this directory do not provide a released vehicle, operating plant or
physically qualified design.
