# Catalogue map corrections — 20 October 2026

**39 city layouts were regenerated.** Kisangani and Lubumbashi retain source-routed radial geometry instead of forced straight core chords. Garissa and the other disconnected examples now form connected proposed networks with grouped platforms at checked dry crossings. All nine Angola cities have moved to **Central Africa**, following [UN M49 Middle Africa](https://data.un.org/legacy/en/iso/ao.html).

The [independent whole-catalogue audit](../catalogue-geography/summary.json) now reports **266 connected networks**, zero missing grouped platforms at checked dry crossings and zero detected wet platforms. **31 self-intersecting lines in 29 other cities remain open.** Each of the 39 regenerated cities passes all expanded local map checks. A geometric or proposed transfer pass is not evidence of walkability or physical approval.

Qena’s rejected source route required a separate constrained Nile-bank reroute. Its final reversal is about 49 m; independent water, connectivity and terminal checks pass. The original source failure remains recorded.

The shared planner restores source corridor shapes, keeps protected land and unknown/wet transfer exclusions, preserves population-priority stops, removes only bounded local raster knots and connects components through continuous constrained routes. Native radial/ring extensions now reject self-retracing paths. Independent graph and geometry checks hold publication on new failures. Every city retains the pre-correction geometry, exact dependency hashes and final map audit.

Population demand uses hash-checked retained WorldPop city pixels and actual OSM anchors; resampled relative demand is never summed as a resident count. OSM acquisition requests relation members and geometry, retries gateway failures with the identical GET query, and rejects partial responses.

Source-bound planning finance was recalculated from the current geometry, fleet and controlled assumptions; the book uses these reconciled quantities. These calculations do not execute an operating or energy campaign.

**Dependent engineering revalidation remains pending.** Updated city pages show current geometry and quantities only. Package manifests flag stale operating, energy, clearance and delivery evidence; retained numerical reports remain linked with their source revisions. Retained numerical results are preserved; they are not rehashed as new executions. No complete engineering, manufacturing, operating or physical release is claimed.
