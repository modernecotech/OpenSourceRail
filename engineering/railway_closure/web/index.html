PROPULSION

Recovery follows the actual clock

Nominal stores start empty. Arrival braking can charge only the receiving station, at the actual capture time. A shared bank that is busy, failed or unready bypasses recovery while independent grid traction and brakes remain available.

Release substitutes for grid effort with a finite force ramp. Unspent stored energy is never reported as delivered propulsion. Route occupancy, train identity and thermal proxies carry through the schedule.
