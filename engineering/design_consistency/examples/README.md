# Linked research demonstrators

[Manifest](manifest.json) binds the generated physical contracts and reduced responses to their sources. [Correspondence](correspondence.json) reconciles dimensions/material mass, CG and inertia; detailed local stiffness and physical qualification remain open.

- [Side bay](side.json) and [roof bay](roof.json): layered parts and support hardware; the roof thermal condition fails.
- [Interface](interface.json), [joint screen](interface-joint-screen.json) and [modes](interface-modes.json): proposed geometry and bounded mechanical response.
- [Viaduct](viaduct.json), [IFC](viaduct.ifc), [IFC audit](ifc-verification.json), [civil stages](civil-stages.json) and [construction front](construction-front.json): coordinated research geometry, explicit deck analysis identity and unresolved ground/resistance/construction gates.
- [Mixed search](mixed-search.json): three seeds, random benchmarks and independent pressure holdouts; no verified Pareto candidates.
- [Native CAD](cad/README.md) and [executed solvers](solver/README.md): independent numerical geometry/response checks, without physical release.

Reproduce using the commands in the [parent README](../README.md). Historical civil/service campaigns retain their original bindings.
