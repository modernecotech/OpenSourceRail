Simple infrastructure. Comfortable, frequent trains.

A simpler wheel-supported train, launched at stations.

Version 0.6 uses six cars with four-abreast seating and wheelchair spaces. Passive wheel bogies run on conventional rails carried by concrete. Station linear motors launch and receive the train through passive aluminium fins. Services batteries power HVAC, doors and essential equipment.
