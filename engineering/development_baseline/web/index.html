Starting example: level, fully elevated double track; six 17 m cars with 276 seated/wheelchair places, no planned standing. Demand and 99% service availability are planning assumptions. Two shifts for production and construction.

Bulk scenarios use China wholesale/manufacturer observations and calculated material, processing and landed costs. The make/buy case includes factory setup and capacity. Advertised prices and engineering estimates are distinct from supplier-confirmed offers.

Headway, speed and acceleration are explored rather than fixed.

The transport goal comes first. This calculator currently compares three station-launch concepts around six 17 m cars and a 20 m box-beam module. Other propulsion, shorter or articulated vehicles, running gear, guideways, alignments and transport approaches remain open. Inspect the open approach catalogue. An approach needing its own model remains a research proposal.

    Selected-candidate exports and delivery handoffs
    
          
    
            The selected candidate exports share their physical identities
            across CAD/IFC, the engineering BOM, manufacturing processes,
            procurement tasks, assembly, civil quantities and controls. The
            Workbench links to these scoped research records and the deployment
            guides for business and supervision integrations. Incomplete
            equipment, prices and physical acceptance remain open.
          
    
          
    
            For a city-specific proposal, compile and verify the planning
            handoff, then create and verify its immutable six-car factory
            work-package draft. It uses the selected Class C cost ledger and the
            declared two-shift, eight-hour production case. The draft is not an
            ERPNext import; civil/site delivery, purchasing, staffing and
            railway operations remain held.

./osr twin factory-draft compile build/planning-handoff --output build/factory-plan
