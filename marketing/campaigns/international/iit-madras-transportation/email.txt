Dear Transportation Engineering group or Civil Engineering research office,

I am writing from Modern EcoTech about OpenSourceRail, an open-source programme with reproducible urban-rail screening concepts focused on 265 cities in 43 developing countries. The design scope relevant to IIT Madras — Transportation Engineering Group contains 52 cities in 6 countries.

The scoped catalogue links network design, GIS, service planning, locally buildable rolling stock, renewable charging, cost and finance models, deterministic simulation and Git-reviewable evidence. It represents 104,248,000 people, 7,902 route-km and 966.2 MW of station/depot PV in the screening models.

The work may support research and teaching on public transit, urban transport planning, network optimisation, simulation and intelligent transport systems. Potential collaborations include independent model validation, student projects, comparative city studies, open datasets and reproducible engineering experiments led with institutions in the Global South.

We are not presenting these outputs as feasibility studies, funding applications, procurement proposals or evidence of eligibility. Would your research group consider a technical seminar or exploratory discussion to define a publishable, independently governed research question and suitable developing-world university partners?
