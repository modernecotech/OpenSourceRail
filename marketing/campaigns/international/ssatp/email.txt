Dear Urban Transport and Mobility or programme team,

I am writing from Modern EcoTech about OpenSourceRail, an open-source programme with reproducible urban-rail screening concepts focused on 265 cities in 43 developing countries. The design scope relevant to Africa Transport Policy Program contains 124 cities in 21 countries.

The scoped catalogue links network design, GIS, service planning, locally buildable rolling stock, renewable charging, cost and finance models, deterministic simulation and Git-reviewable evidence. It represents 158,036,324 people, 11,595 route-km and 1740.2 MW of station/depot PV in the screening models.

The platform may contribute to work on african transport policy, sustainable urban mobility, institutional capacity and evidence-based reform. It offers a reproducible basis for peer review, comparative research, data-quality improvement and locally led case studies.

We are not presenting these outputs as feasibility studies, funding applications, procurement proposals or evidence of eligibility. Would a technical demonstration or methods review be useful, and could you identify an appropriate working group or regional research partner?
