I am writing from Modern EcoTech about OpenSourceRail, an open-source programme that has generated reproducible urban-rail screening concepts for 266 cities in 44 countries.

The catalogue links network design, GIS, service planning, locally buildable rolling stock, renewable charging, cost and finance models, deterministic simulation and Git-reviewable evidence.
