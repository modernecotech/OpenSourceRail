Dear Green Cities programme team,

I am writing from Modern EcoTech about OpenSourceRail, an open-source programme with reproducible urban-rail screening concepts focused on 265 cities in 43 developing countries. The design scope relevant to EBRD Green Cities contains 99 cities in 11 countries.

The scoped catalogue links network design, GIS, service planning, locally buildable rolling stock, renewable charging, cost and finance models, deterministic simulation and Git-reviewable evidence. It represents 104,261,699 people, 8,939 route-km and 1345.8 MW of station/depot PV in the screening models.

The work may align with your mandate for municipal green action plans, urban transport and renewable infrastructure. These are transparent screening concepts intended to make the next stage of public review and project preparation cheaper and faster.

We are not presenting these outputs as feasibility studies, funding applications, procurement proposals or evidence of eligibility. Could you advise whether there is an eligible project-preparation, technical-assistance, guarantee or investment route, and which public-sector counterpart should lead it?
