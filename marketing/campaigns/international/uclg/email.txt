Dear World Secretariat or relevant regional section,

I am writing from Modern EcoTech about OpenSourceRail, an open-source programme that has generated reproducible urban-rail screening concepts for 266 cities in 44 countries. The design scope relevant to United Cities and Local Governments contains 266 cities in 44 countries.

The scoped catalogue links network design, GIS, service planning, locally buildable rolling stock, renewable charging, cost and finance models, deterministic simulation and Git-reviewable evidence. It represents 368,041,477 people, 29,106 route-km and 4048.6 MW of station/depot PV in the screening models.

The work appears relevant to your work on municipal cooperation, local public services and city-to-city knowledge exchange. We would welcome independent challenge, standards guidance and a route to the appropriate regional or member-city team.

We are not presenting these outputs as feasibility studies, funding applications, procurement proposals or evidence of eligibility. Would a 30-minute technical discussion, peer review or knowledge-sharing session be appropriate, or could you direct us to the correct team?
