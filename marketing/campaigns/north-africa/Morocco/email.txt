Modern EcoTech's OpenSourceRail project has prepared open, reproducible urban-rail screening concepts for 12 cities in Morocco, representing 8,050,000 people and 860 route-km.

Across the catalogue, the current city-level models indicate $7.17 B of planning CAPEX, 76% local capital/value allocation, 146.7 MW of station/depot solar and 759.5 MWh of storage. A shared national manufacturing strategy and city-by-city evidence are set out in the national brief.

These are transparent screening models—not bids, sovereign commitments, demand forecasts or funding approvals. We are seeking a government counterpart to review assumptions, select a pilot city and define an independently governed feasibility programme.

Could your office route this to the appropriate urban-mobility, industrial-policy and climate-finance teams, and advise whether a technical introductory meeting would be useful?
