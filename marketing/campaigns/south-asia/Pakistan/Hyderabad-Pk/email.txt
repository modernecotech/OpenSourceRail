I am writing from Modern EcoTech to share OpenSourceRail's open, reproducible urban-rail planning concept for Hyderabad-Pk, Pakistan.

The current screening model covers 6 lines and 180.0 route-km, with 216 trainsets, 21.2 MW of station/depot solar and 121.0 MWh of storage. Its planning CAPEX is $1.60 B, with 77% assigned to local capital and value creation under the current assumptions.

The design, cost model, GIS inputs and deterministic simulation evidence are open for technical review. These are early planning outputs—not a tender price, demand forecast, funding approval or construction-ready design—and we would like to test them with local data and institutional priorities.
