## Currency, historical proposal and local benefits

Only the proposed Chinese loan is USD **debt**, but half of imports also require direct government USD **cash**. Combined USD capital funding is USD 1.792 billion, or 22.75% of uses. The other 77.25% uses IQD government cash, IQD bonds and IQD bank credit. The 25% direct budget contribution is not a cap on sovereign bond liabilities, possible guarantees or lifetime public cash needs. Programme monthly and annual files retain both USD loan/government flows and native IQD flows. USD equivalent reporting is distinct from funding currency.

The updated programme compares this 516.5 km / 182-station planning layout against the [July 2024 reported proposal](https://www.aljazeera.net/ebusiness/2024/7/26/العراق-يختار-شركات-أجنبية-لتنفيذ): 148 km, seven lines, 64 stations, USD 18 billion. The [NIC notice](https://investpromo.gov.iq/announcement-of-an-international-investment-opportunity-no-3-of-2024-metro-baghdad-dbomft/) requires a bidder funding plan; entirely USD foreign loans and government cash are the requested comparison scenario, not established final terms. Under that scenario, combined USD capital funding is about 90.0% lower. Different scopes, price dates, qualification maturity and schedules prevent this planning comparison from establishing a like-for-like bid saving.

Modelled average fare is IQD 1,317 per paid trip. At fixed low-case demand, OPEX-only neutrality requires IQD 485; the first complete operating year including debt and fees requires about IQD 1,457 in the full-network-only case, excluding factory debt and reserve deposits. These are cash thresholds, not recommended tariffs. Thirty paid trips use 8% of the retained income proxy and 44 commuter trips 11.7%. The 46.4% anchor score implies a 4.54 million-resident proxy, not measured walk-access coverage or a census result. The older reported 80% city-coverage ambition has no comparable denominator.

Potential local procurement is USD 6.084 billion equivalent, or 77.25% of capital, under unverified procurement-origin allocations. Iraqi body fabrication, fit-out, wiring, civil/precast production, erection, installation and maintenance can retain skills, wages, supplier income and repair capability. These budgets are not payroll, GDP added, or verified local content. The model carries 2,350 indicative operating FTE and IQD 19.50 billion annual labour cost; construction and manufacturing jobs need validated hours, wages and productivity. The third-party plan's Iraqi labour share is unknown and cannot be assumed zero.

In the full-network-only comparator, additional cash divides into USD 9,313.64 million before full-network revenue, USD 53.90 million during operations/debt tail, and USD 418.61 million for plant financing. Phasing brings receipts forward but does not close the cash gap. Currency matching alone does not establish financial sustainability.

Base costs and revenues are nominal without escalation; imported maintenance exposure and factory operating budgets need separate appraisal. The factory ledger isolates capital financing and does not represent a complete factory business case.

Existing platform boundaries remain: process-level paired control is a software reference; independent physical outputs/watchdogs/power, calibrated train and infrastructure interfaces, formal refinement, HIL and independent assessment remain open. The city simulator is not an accepted export of a complete city into the bounded two-train TACS reference. Passing software tests does not close those gaps.
