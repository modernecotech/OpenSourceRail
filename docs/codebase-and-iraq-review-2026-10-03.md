## Variable ticketing and operating-cost inflation

The requested additional sensitivity raises both fares and OPEX 5% annually, in separate explicit indices from financial close. First service still starts in month 66; no fare receipts are credited before opening. With income growing 5% too, real fare relative to income stays unchanged, so the illustrative -0.30 price elasticity does not reduce trips. Existing rents remain nominally flat unless a separate lease-indexation case is selected; the additional grant/rights/new-net-income targets retain their prior uncommitted amounts.

The paired 5% blended case peaks at **IQD 10.774 trillion supplemental debt**, incurs IQD 10.962 trillion of gross facility draws, and ends with **zero uncovered cash and zero unpaid supplemental debt**. It still needs an available early IQD facility, the conditional climate grant/development receipts and assumed nominal financing terms. Annual price growth changes repayment capacity, not whether a lender has committed. The constant-price USD 134.06m/year net-income threshold above belongs to a different sensitivity and is not also required in this indexed case.

Average nominal tickets are IQD 1,681 at first opening and IQD 5,164 at full opening in month 346. Forty-four trips stay 11.7% of the indexed income proxy with 5% income growth. If income grows only 2%, that full-opening burden rises to **26.4%**, reducing paid demand in the model. These are conditional affordability calculations, not wage or inflation forecasts.

Variable peak/off-peak pricing assumes 40% of baseline trips at a 25% premium and the rest at a 10% discount; each tier receives its own price response before revenue is combined. With the same 5% indices, peak supplemental debt becomes IQD 10.613 trillion and terminal debt clears. Household concessions, transfer integration, commuter caps, demand switching and collection costs need a separately funded tariff design. Discount-induced trips cannot exceed the unchanged practical capacity.

If OPEX instead rises 7% while fares and income rise 5%, the case again leaves **IQD 11.768 trillion uncovered cash** and **IQD 13.000 trillion terminal debt**. Inflation cannot simply be applied to revenues alone. At 5% general inflation, the 8% real discount assumption becomes 13.4% nominal; paired-case unlevered NPV is USD -4.121 billion equivalent, excluding new grant/rights/net-income targets. Repaying the conditional loan does not make discounted project value positive. Capital escalation, lifecycle cost escalation, floating rates and future FX still need full appraisal.
