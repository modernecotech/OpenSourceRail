OpenSourceRail v0.5 — ferrite PM maglev

Fewer moving parts, local manufacture and compact elevated urban routes.

The primary design uses fixed onboard ferrite magnetic modules and powered linear electromagnetic propulsion, replacing conventional wheelsets, axles, gearboxes and rotating traction motors in normal running gear. Windings, drives, cooling, sensors, protection, passenger doors and emergency systems remain necessary. Contactless running aims to reduce wheel/rail contact noise and wear; acoustic performance and whole-system maintenance still require measurement.

Simple civil works and local capability

The research target is one compact integrated concrete guidebeam per direction, with necessary ferromagnetic reaction surfaces, repeatable supports and no continuous external walkway. A vertical spiral evacuation chute is the preferred alternative under investigation. Walkway removal has not been approved. Route clearance, safe landings, assisted evacuation, stopping/recovery and fire response must work at every permitted stopping location.

The 99% elevated-mainline objective and straight tangents aim to reach constrained urban corridors with less ground occupation and disruption. Land rights, utilities, access, wildlife, waterways, curvature and foundation feasibility still govern each route. No universal clearance or zero-disruption claim is made.

Local casting, steel fabrication, cartridge assembly, winding, vehicle structures, interiors, wiring, Rust software and integration can create domestic capability. The complete local-content percentage, installed programme cost and funding requirement remain unknown until origin-traced bills, processes and supplier offers exist. Earlier LM3 factory-gate prices and portfolio percentages are retained comparison inputs, not estimates for the new system.
