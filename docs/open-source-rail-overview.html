OpenSourceRail: open railway design and delivery

OpenSourceRail aims to retain design authority, manufacturing, software, integration and maintenance capability in-country. It connects three products: a design and delivery platform, reference railway hardware and factories, and open GoA4 controls. Low whole-life cost, local value added, repairability and repeatable construction are objectives, not established savings.

The v0.6 reference

The selected research configuration is station-powered freewheeling wheel rail. It has 6 cars of 17 m, each with 44 fixed seats and 2 wheelchair places, and no planned standing. Passive bogies run on conventional rails carried by precast concrete. Station double-sided LIMs act on passive onboard aluminium secondaries; normal-service traction motors, traction batteries and suspension magnets are absent from the train.

The targets are 72 km/h, 120-second headway and 35-second dwell. Capacity arithmetic gives 8,280 passengers/hour/direction, not a demonstrated protected service. Auxiliary batteries, HVAC, doors, independent brakes, onboard ATP, station-led supervision and recovery remain necessary. Passengers transfer between fixed lines; depot switching is a separate exception to the no-mainline-switch policy.

Partial stock geometry and equipment accounts, not a complete installed or approved train.

The wider platform remains

The lifecycle is plan, design, procure, manufacture, construct, commission, operate, maintain and renew. ERPNext/Frappe HR retains business, workforce, procurement, quality and maintenance functions. FUXA and the gateway retain observations, alarms, history and review-linked lifecycle records. The planning workspace retains local city/service editing and exports over historical packets; its v0.6 compilation is unconnected. These services require configured roles, masters, device bindings and reviewed imports; the engineering Workbench does not automatically commission them.

The coordinated design experiment propagates revised bodies through inventory, interfaces, propulsion demand, braking, civil loads and manufacturing/economic coverage. CAD/IFC stock, EBOM/MBOM and assembly/procurement records share candidate identities. ERPNext exports are disabled research drafts. Complete installed equipment, available operating motor maps, force-driven journeys and whole-life prices remain open.

All 266 retained city geographies remain inputs. v0.6 performance regeneration is held; earlier fleets, budgets and winding lengths are not current results. Imported inputs retain their actual origin. No local-content percentage or economic superiority is inferred from a lightweight material subtotal.

Start and inspect

Run ./install.sh, then ./osr workbench. Use ./osr design railway plan for coordinated research, ./osr build to refresh current evidence and ./osr verify to check its sources and payloads. ./osr sim baghdad reports the city hold.
