OpenSourceRail v0.5.2 development — station-powered ferrite maglev

Fewer moving parts, local manufacture and compact elevated urban routes.

Illustrative development concept, without physical or manufacturing acceptance.

Station-mounted linear synchronous motors launch and receive the train. Interstation propulsion is unpowered; onboard ferrite PM-biased active support, guidance and passenger services remain independently powered. Removing main traction equipment from the train shifts electrical plant to stations. Support coils/drives, cooling, sensing, protection, doors, HVAC and emergency equipment remain necessary. Contactless running aims to reduce contact noise and wear; actual losses, acoustic performance and maintenance require measurement.

Body, civil works and local capability

Baghdad uses six 17 m bodies. The central tare target is 14.2 t/car, including a 5.2 t equipped structural GFRP body, 3 t ferrite, 30 kWh services storage and the full 1.8 t growth reserve. Four passenger doors per car are the body takeoff. Calculated panel stock is distinct from sized members, joints, complete equipment mass and structural qualification.

The ordinary civil candidate is a 25 m, 0.80 × 1.80 m closed concrete box with 100 mm webs, 150 mm flanges and end infills. Hollow columns/caps and alternative foundation families are compared by quantity. Necessary passive iron, anchors, bearings, connections and final survey remain in scope. Ground-specific selection, prestress/rebar detailing, loads and construction stages remain unqualified.

The research target omits a continuous external walkway. Vertical spiral chutes are the preferred alternative under investigation, without walkway-removal approval. Safe landing, assisted evacuation, deployment, stopping/recovery and fire response must work at every allowed stopping location.

The 99% elevated-mainline objective and straight tangents aim to reach constrained corridors with less surface occupation. Rights, buildings, utilities, access, wildlife, water, curvature and foundation feasibility govern each route.

Local precast/GFRP production, steel fabrication, cartridge assembly, station winding cassettes, interiors, wiring, Rust software and integration can build domestic capability. Prioritise verified British manufacture when selecting UK machinery. A UK distributor or integrator does not establish manufacturing origin. Complete local content remains unknown until the bill and input origins exist.

Operating and cost boundaries

The station operating reference is a jerk-limited 90 s flat 1 km run with 190 s headway. Grade, wind, drag, arrival speed and receiving-brake readiness remain explicit route gates. A committed coasting train keeps its corridor and receiving berth reserved until proved full-consist clearance. Receiver failure, independent stopping/holding and stationary recovery remain design obligations; a distant station motor cannot restart a train in an unpowered span.

A 45 s charge within a 60 s dwell needs 1.20 MW/train before charger losses under the hot-services assumption, including the battery energy used during the remaining dwell. Station electrical cost targets are additional to the incomplete ordinary civil subtotal. Stations, external grid work, complete evacuation, recovery, land, qualification, factory capital and financing need their own scope. No quoted complete railway price or accepted production rate is established.
