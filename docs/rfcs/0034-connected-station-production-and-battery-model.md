User-selected scenarios, calculated quantities, documented historical capability and supplier quotations are different evidence kinds. Stable input-content revisions and file hashes identify generated packages, including modified working-tree inputs, without depending on their containing commit. Unknown installed prices, reaction designs or spare capacities remain unknown; zero is reserved for a measured/declared absence.

## Station and civil contract

Elevation is independent of archetype. `major` is a physical island, not two side platforms. Each platform, face, track direction, boarding datum and door/PSD side comes from one layout. Stacked interchanges have individual platform levels and explicit concourse transfer connections. CAD, IFC, assembly datums and the EBOM consume this contract. A physical platform count never substitutes for boarding faces or train berths.

The initial elevated island study has two lifts, two escalators, two lift shafts and two separated stair routes. It is not an approved universal minimum. Equipment has stable identities, served levels, envelopes, entrances and concourse connections. The layout rejects overlapping equipment and insufficient clear widths. Bidirectional flow, one-lift-unavailable operation, independent evacuation, assisted rescue, road barriers and additional street equipment remain separate assessments. Additional equipment/entrances can be supplied by site configuration.

Access CAPEX uses installed item quantities and associated works; energy, annual maintenance and replacement have separate outputs. Net island savings deduct wider deck, changed supports and spreading approaches. Unknown structural costs prevent a net saving claim.

Station decks, widened pier caps and foundations never inherit running-viaduct width. Approach offsets use a quintic transition and a curvature screen, followed by alignment/cant/gradient qualification. Construction requires wider launcher support and clearance checks, delivery access, released supports and an erection sequence. Changed entrances have geographic coordinates and trigger a coverage review; existing centroid coverage cannot be certified for newly proposed entrances.

## Manufacturing, suppliers and erection

Pi-beam specifications record concrete, steel, prestressing and inserts; manufactured study mass and centre of gravity; lifting/storage/transport positions; strength gates; bearing/restraint interfaces; tolerances; and acceptance records. The 60/75-tonne figures are bare sections. Study suspended load includes local diaphragms, reinforcement, prestressing, embedded items and lifting gear. Equipment approval fails until the arrangement and strength/tolerance records are released. Launcher support reactions, asymmetric placement torsion and delivery-vehicle deck loads have separate construction-stage screening outputs.

The capacity register records products, prestressing capability, beds, moulds, handling dimensions/mass, demonstrated cycles, total versus contracted output, commitments, storage/dispatch, prices, terms, qualification and upgrades. Iraq already has many precast facilities and production expertise; this is the user-provided sourcing basis. No named facility or unverified spare capacity is adopted. Each factory must enter the shared register with product-specific evidence and contracted capacity before it can supply the programme. Slabs, walkways, ducts and suitable pier parts can be allocated separately; aggregate allocations cannot exceed a product's contracted capacity.

The shared [chronological supply chain](../../design/component-catalogue/src/osr_mech/civil/supply_chain.py) now feeds contracted completions, acceptance holds, manufacturing rejects, dispatch and transport directly into erection. Factory buffers count both awaiting-acceptance and accepted stock. Full buffers pause casting without banking unused whole production capacity; rejected or damaged beams create replacement demand while installed quantities remain fixed. Shared transport fleets cannot be multiplied across routes. In-transit loads reserve front storage and receive credit only after arrival; an available qualified alternative route can serve a blocked primary route.

Delivery routes constrain journey/window/loading/unloading times, trailers, payload/bridge limits, long-load/turning access, accepted/rejected components, order and buffer space, plus alternative routes/suppliers. Unknown/rejected route approval supplies zero output. Front start/end chainage, direction, launcher, delivery access, support readiness, interruptions, start/finish/relocation dates and work intervals are explicit. “Two per line” initializes the scenario; assignments may change with lengths/readiness. Shared obstructed routes and sequential erection paths cannot receive simultaneous output credit.

The simulator counts integer beams and complete paired-track bays. Daily production is productive shift time divided by a complete bay cycle, with handovers, maintenance, weather, windows, ramp-up, station interruptions and relocations. Extra launchers on one sequential front are spares, not extra independent paths. Casting, acceptance, transport and released supports cap progress. Each front reports the model days limited by each resource, and the package separately records production, acceptance, shipment, arrival and rejection totals. The conditional pool describes a capacity requirement and requires explicit study opt-in; it is not inserted into the real supplier register. The shared `precast-logistics.json` register contains actual route evidence when supplied. Fractional 1.5/1.8/2.0 bays/launcher/day are averages, implying 54/64.8/72 beams/day across 18 machines. Two shifts do not automatically double output. A conditional forecast assumes the necessary supply; the evidence-backed forecast remains unreleased when no suppliers/contracts/supports are qualified.

## Costs, equipment and people

Make/buy separates delivered purchases, project tooling, upgrades/beds, storage/dispatch and new factories. Supplier amortisation and project tooling cannot both be charged. Rolling-stock assembly and electronics factory allowances remain until their scope is reconciled; replacing civil supply is not permission to delete them.

The civil calibration is edited and the cost file regenerated. Supplied products, erection equipment, transport, temporary works, labour/supervision, night shifts, inspection, maintenance/relocation, traffic management and supports have explicit study allocations. Station exceptions and special structures stay separate. The $9m central purchase allowance for 18 whole-beam launchers remains unquoted, with transporter, lifting gear, freight/duties, assembly, commissioning, supports, spares, crews, relocation and station-modification exclusions. It is not a segmental-gantry quote. The installed civil rate's embedded erection allowance remains unverified: purchase expenditure creates no additional claimed saving until an explicit scope-matched removal is justified.

ERP drafts identify each launcher, transporter and lifting frame as an Asset, with purchase/commissioning records, operating configurations, inspection/maintenance, spares, competence, location, availability, allocation and dismantle/ship/recommission tasks. Cash purchase, project cost allocation and residual value stay separate; initial Baghdad cash is not eliminated by reuse. Transfers cannot overlap allocations and require subsequent-city compatibility.

Department → unit → crew → qualified worker → task is a validated chain. Each front and shift requires supervision/lifting, operators, riggers/signalling, survey/QA, mechanical/electrical maintenance, delivery coordination and factory acceptance. Workers cannot fill both shifts concurrently. Handovers, relief/leave coverage, competence expiry, supervised commissioning and equipment-specific assessment are explicit; unfilled drafts do not grant permission to work.

## Battery and chronological energy contract

Profiles specify supplier/cell identity and evidence revision, gross/usable kWh, complete mass/volume, electrical configuration/range, temperature/SOC/age-dependent charge and discharge limits, efficiency/cooling, life, installed/replacement price and qualification. Catalogue sodium values are illustrative pack sensitivities, not asserted vendor performance. Vehicle tare changes by the difference from the included LFP pack; that mass propagates to traction energy and costs. Cases hold usable capacity and service constant: LFP throughout, sodium stationary/LFP onboard, sodium both. The small onboard pack remains a separate duty-cycle option.

The network duty uses declared fleets, dispatch termini, headways, station distances, dwell and installed site capacities, with an explicit average-speed timing assumption and stock-readiness checks. Minute chronology allocates concurrent arrivals across shared cabinet power, subtracts setup time, applies charge taper/temperature/SoH, accounts for regen acceptance, seasonal solar/cleaning, hot-weather auxiliaries, missed visits, outages, reserves, degradation and replacement. Report minimum SOC, unserved duties and missed charges, not annual totals alone. The study needs a calibrated timetable and full seasonal chronological validation before service acceptance.

`osr-bms` requires an explicit commissioned profile with matching pack, chemistry, evidence revision, qualification and valid calibration. A chemistry-string change cannot select or derive sodium protection from LFP defaults. Existing trip, latch, cooldown, off-gas/fire isolation and derating behaviour are preserved and shared by the validated evaluator. Planning profiles are not BMS commissioning profiles.

## Regeneration and acceptance

The outstanding review is traced in `remaining-work.json`, covering all 20 software, engineering, commercial, workforce, deployment and governance areas. Source-hashed records may be entered through `connected-delivery-evidence.json`; a receipt is not authority authentication or acceptance. No supplier, appointment or qualification is invented. The user-provided Iraqi precast capability basis remains qualitative, without selecting a named facility.

`beam-stage-assurance.json` adds gross-section properties, uniform-selfweight moments, lift/store/transport support reactions, sling forces and launcher-position envelopes. Geometry comes from the canonical Pi family. These are statics screens with explicit mass/support/dynamic assumptions. Prestress, reinforcement, connections, material/release strength, fatigue/vibration, anchors, bearings, stability and manufacturer load charts remain required for an independently checked design.

`station-capacity.json` calculates the union of shared-layout equipment envelopes on each platform and its remaining area and contiguous lane. Lift-out capacity and circulation/density comparisons use source-entered criteria; an empty register yields unknown throughput. Multilevel demand allocation, street barriers, surveyed entrance walking routes, evacuation/rescue and station/launcher structures require site assessment.

`procurement-evidence.json` separates dated preliminary, firm and contracted quotations from factory capacity and purchase commitment. Its catalogue beam requirements reference identified span components; special spans remain outside ordinary orders. Tooling already amortised in a unit price cannot be added as project tooling. The USD 9m allowance remains purchase cash only. `cashflow-sensitivities.json` links those identified payments to each conditional schedule at assumed 4/8/12% simple ACT/365 interest with full borrowing of the identified cash. Unknown costs, scope-removal credit, complete financing and accepted revenue remain null, so these sensitivities cannot be published as complete capital or net savings.

The generic front splitter can reassign the available radial donors in another city without requiring six donors or fabricating 18 active fronts. Configured spare launchers add no independent output. Other-city runs remain independent, unqualified planning scenarios; transfer from Baghdad is not authorised before its critical path and compatibility/recommissioning are accepted.

ERP execution records now bind lifecycle release to the actual project/front. Required transfers need transfer, compatibility and recommissioning evidence. Actual work start/close timestamps are controlled; active work cannot be reopened to hide an allocation, and cancellation requires reviewed handback. Completed/cancelled work history continues to participate in worker-rest checks. These business controls grant no railway engineering acceptance.

The identified span register partitions running intervals at millimetre precision into proposed 20 m/25 m catalogue spans and explicitly unresolved special spans. Ordinary beam requirements have stable component and span IDs; special spans receive no ordinary-beam order or invented design. Proposed supports require survey and structural acceptance. Disconnected sections incur a stated relocation allowance instead of receiving continuous launcher progress. Fixed allocation and a conditional six-machine transfer to additional ring fronts use the same 18 launchers, contracted-throughput requirement and 72-transporter fleet. Added ring access and support readiness are assumptions, not feasibility evidence.

Ring stock serves both headings at the declared dispatch stations, including controlled wrap distances in reverse. The energy study continues through three service days without resetting train or storage state. A declared study strategy permits grid replenishment within each site's remaining import capacity. Native section timing comes from `osr-sim`'s scenario loader and rest-to-rest movement model. The fixed-assignment diagnostic and coupled dispatch controller are separate: the controller keeps an energy-held train at its actual station, continues charging and removes it from subsequent dispatch availability until recovery. It reports distinct held journeys, hold minutes and missed opportunities separately. Minute discretisation, berth/depot access, transient traction, conflict authorities and field calibration remain open; no service or chemistry acceptance follows from these screens.

Explicit working calendars now gate erection, casting, acceptance, deliveries and foundation release on closed days. Holidays, dated maintenance, available windows and permission for the erection night shift are configurable. A six-day sensitivity retains the same fleet and installed quantities. Daily supplier-specific shifts, permitted night work and named relief rosters need actual evidence; no statutory holiday list or permission is invented.

Native simulator commissioning uses `[[battery_commissioning]]` records, each naming train, profile ID, pack identity, chemistry, evidence revision, nominal pack voltage, model capacity and a complete `CommissioningProfile`. Every train must be covered if any binding is supplied; duplicate packs, mismatched chemistry/capacity and unqualified profiles fail. The onboard evaluator uses the selected immutable calibration, cell count and voltage and exposes identity in its report. Legacy absent bindings remain labelled by zero commissioned trains and are synthetic planning shadows. Catalogue chemistry profiles contain no accepted commissioning records.

ERP native Task/ToDo transitions now require a submitted `OSR Construction Release`, reviewed by a user with HR and project authority roles. Evidence is private, task-bound and hashed. Controlled Employee authorisations and Asset lifecycle records require appropriate HR/project roles to change and are re-read under resource locks. Work checks active company/department identity, unrevoked competence, asset configuration/commissioning, inspection/maintenance coverage, unresolved repairs, native shifts, approved leave and holiday lists, reviewed night permission, relief/handover, overlap and rest across completed shifts. Completing work additionally requires a reviewed completion record. Revoked reviews and changed attachments cannot grant work. Installation and local tests establish code paths, not a deployed workforce, actual competence or physical railway release.

Station-specific assignment accepts sourced OD cohorts with declared station legs, headings, transfers and a peak window. It counts each paid journey once, additional transfer boardings separately, section loads and station waiting accumulation against that window's declared service. Empty inputs produce unknown demand. The common 3,000 pax/h input is a separately labelled stress case; measured lift-out throughput, circulation, rescue and entrance-based catchments remain evidence gates.

The Iraq national brief and funding document use one current country basis. The USD 8.312bn `local_positive` comparator is distinct from the unpriced accelerated case and the historical USD 7.581bn funding calculation. No debt-clearance month, national budget or realised saving is claimed for the current case. Existing Iraqi precast facilities and expertise are acknowledged without selecting a named facility or assuming spare contracted capacity.

Run `.venv/bin/python tools/automation/connected-build-study.py --city baghdad --refresh-cad` and `--check`. Regenerate station kits, native FreeCAD station libraries and IFCs with their established generators, and generate `civil-cost-model.toml` from calibration. The connected package contains quantities, fronts, resource and procurement/payment sensitivities, conditional opening dates, battery/operating/replacement costs, financing gaps, proposed entrance geography, ERP asset/crew drafts and a hashed source/output manifest. Prior adopted cost/finance/coverage packages remain identified comparators while unresolved supplier, structural and access inputs prevent promotion to an accepted budget or programme.

Regression gates: one island/two faces; access equipment matches geometry; shifts change resources not physical quantities; no erection beyond accepted/delivered beams or supports; contracted supplier limits; mutually exclusive access and asset transfers; battery mass/energy/cost propagation; and source/assumption provenance. The implementation sequence is station/civil topology, suppliers/logistics/shifts, costs/reuse, batteries and city regeneration.
