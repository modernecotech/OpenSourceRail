# Baghdad scope and industrial review — 4 October 2026

> **Historical revision review.** Its figures describe the revision reviewed below. The reworked central alignment, current staffing/depot scope and current cashflows are in the [Baghdad city summary](../cities/catalogue/west-asia/Iraq/Baghdad/README.md) and [programme recalculation](../cities/catalogue/west-asia/Iraq/Baghdad/engineering/programme-recalculation/README.md).

The [programme recalculation](../cities/catalogue/west-asia/Iraq/Baghdad/engineering/programme-recalculation/README.md) is the latest Baghdad staffing, depot, procurement-origin and financing study. The [complete proposal](../cities/catalogue/west-asia/Iraq/Baghdad/README.md) publishes it alongside its source files, twelve monthly cashflows, contract registers and six-month placement schedules. It supersedes the earlier staffing/depot assumptions for this study; the original catalogue design, generic city pages and financing cases remain separately identified comparators. No national borrowing or future factory sales are credited to Baghdad.

## People and operating coverage

There are two concurrent station posts during two normal eight-hour shifts. That gives 728 daily shift assignments for 182 stations, rather than 728 employees covering every day. The retained 05:30–02:00 service requires an additional 4.5 hours of late cover. After weekly, leave, training, sickness and handover allowances, station cover requires 1,800 FTE within 3,762 permanent railway FTE. Role, line and recruitment-cohort registers reconcile payroll and pre-opening training. A shorter station opening day would require a separate service/revenue scenario.

The pay basis uses the employee median of IQD 614,000 in the 2021 Labour Force Survey, reported by the [IMF](https://www.imf.org/-/media/files/publications/cr/2023/english/1irqea2023002.pdf). An assumed 5% annual index gives the editable 2026 proxy; the general grade is 1.5 times that proxy, and technical, supervisor, senior and director grades are 2.25, 3, 4 and 5 times it. Employer costs and overtime are explicit. This is not a measured current median, and the old household-income proxy is not substituted for employee wages or altered to improve fare affordability.

Construction, train manufacture, upstream component manufacture and permanent operations are separate establishments. The construction register is a configured work-package crew screen; it is not a complete contractor payroll or a schedule for the new depot/factory sites. Contract rates already contain labour. A separate labour-content stress tests an assumed 20% content with 50% wage uplift and incremental EPC once, without inventing a measured overlap credit. Factory payroll funds the full establishment through the actual scheduled order; productive labour in unit costs is deducted before adding capacity payroll.

## Depots and manufacture

Each of the nine lines receives one depot sized for its full six-car fleet, including spares and cold reserve. Storage totals 831 slots at 121 m per train position, using the actual 111 m train. Workshop bays follow annual bay-hour demand independently of storage tracks. The item register prices storage/workshop/access tracks, turnouts, drainage/access, workshop shells/equipment/services, lighting/fire services, offices/stores, wash plants, wheel lathes and isolation/rescue. Existing depot PV/BESS is counted once. Land, utilities, actual ground conditions, connected access alignments and installed charging/grid upgrades remain open; depot launch/throat conflicts still require a full-service replay.

The component study follows the actual six-car BOM quantities: 9,972 bogies and motor/inverter sets each, 4,986 battery packs, 19,944 door cassettes and 29,916 window cassettes. Process cells match the train factory's required rate and include Chinese process machinery, Iraqi facilities/materials, labour, support, maintenance, qualification and residual imported inputs. Bogie fabrication, motor assembly, battery-pack assembly and door manufacture show positive whole-order margins under the unquoted inputs. Window manufacture has a negative Baghdad-only margin; future national orders are not assumed to rescue it. Imported cells/BMS and other critical parts remain. Battery pack assembly does not imply local cell manufacture.

Serial qualification within the 18-month facility target remains an assumption. A six-month supplier-delay case shifts rolling-stock invoices, line openings and operating receipts, prices idle payroll and preserves the full operating horizon. A 25% raw-input price stress tests the same selected facilities. License, yield/rework, tooling overlap and actual vendor quotations require evidence before investment.

## Alignment and finance

The study permits a 40% elevated ceiling and screens 29.148 km of candidate additional viaduct around exceptional segments, reaching 20.32% elevated. Intervals include approach allowances, merge overlap and exclude existing elevated/bridge lengths. Elevation alone removes no horizontal bend. Wider-radius geometry, station moves, land, gradients, egress, geotechnical and utility constraints need design work. The 0/25/50% routing-penalty-removal cases are counterfactuals; no penalty reduction is adopted as an achieved saving.

Government funds exactly 25% of each case's capital. Imported invoices are split 50% government USD cash and 50% proposed Chinese USD credit. The remaining government appropriation and all domestic bonds, bank/gap debt and mezzanine are IQD. Machinery-heavy months follow their invoice downpayments; the total government contribution, rather than each month's share, is constrained to 25%. Foreign procurement intensity, foreign debt and reporting equivalents are separate measures. The old 148 km / USD 18bn third-party example retains its distinct scope and assumed foreign-currency funding; it is not a matched tender comparison.

Mezzanine substitutes for 10% of domestic residual capital borrowing. The tested terms are project assumptions: 6% cash coupon, 4% PIK, 2% fee, 15-year balloon and senior DSCR 1.20 before junior cash payments. Deferred coupons and PIK increase debt until maturity; unpaid balloons become overdue balances, with simple-interest sensitivity and no automatic refinancing or conversion. Gap draws or unsourced support cannot fund junior distributions. Source/use, senior principal, junior principal/PIK and overdue-interest identities are checked independently. Semiannual schedules report flows separately from closing stocks and distinguish face-denomination rounding from actual cash raised.

The revised senior case still has unsourced support and unpaid debt at the horizon. Mezzanine reduces immediate funding pressure by deferring payments but increases terminal debt and leaves 85 junior draw vintages in default. It is not recommended as the solution to the operating deficit. Indexed fares, OPEX inflation, advertising/kiosks/rentals and inherited additional receipts are already included. Green terms, grants, rights income and 2% concessional gap credit remain uncommitted; the 8% gap-rate stress shows that financing dependence. There are no dividends. Verified capital, affordable revenue or accepted scope savings are still needed.
