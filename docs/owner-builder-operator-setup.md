# Owner–Builder–Operator Setup Plan

OpenSourceRail needs an institution that can own the design, procure openly,
build locally, accept evidence and operate the railway for decades. The default
is a **public asset owner with a ring-fenced delivery and operating company**,
not a temporary project office and not a turnkey vendor holding the design data.

This is a jurisdiction-neutral mobilisation plan. Local government must obtain
legal, labour, tax, procurement, railway-safety, environmental and insurance
advice before creating entities or delegating statutory duties.

## Recommended Default

```text
elected government / public sponsor
└── public asset owner and funding authority
    ├── audit and risk committee
    ├── safety, environment and assurance committee
    ├── design/data authority (retained client capability)
    └── ring-fenced rail delivery and operating company
        ├── programme, commercial, finance and land
        ├── system integration and configuration
        ├── infrastructure delivery
        ├── rolling-stock/local-manufacture delivery
        ├── operations-readiness and railway operations
        ├── engineering, maintenance and asset management
        └── customer, stations and community relations

independent of delivery reporting:
independent safety assessor • independent design checker • external auditor
regulator/authorising body • emergency services • insurer
```

The public owner keeps the corridor, assets, open technical baseline, data,
configuration rights, safety duties that law does not permit it to delegate,
and the ability to retender individual packages. The company employs the people
who integrate, build, commission, operate and maintain the system. Contractors,
factories and specialist suppliers deliver bounded work packages to that company.

For a small local authority, the company can begin as a ring-fenced municipal
business unit and incorporate after the development gate. For several cities,
a national or regional manufacturing and design company can supply vehicles and
standard civil products while each city retains its own asset owner/operator.

## Entity Choices

| Model | Use when | Advantage | Main risk / control |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ring-fenced authority directorate | Earliest feasibility or a single small pilot | Fastest start; existing public controls | Skills can disperse; give it a dedicated budget, accountable officer and configuration/data ownership |
| Wholly public corporation | Default for construction and operation | Stable employer, clearer accounts, can serve multiple corridors | Political or commercial drift; protect public-service objectives and reserved owner decisions in its constitution |
| Public–public joint venture | Several authorities share a factory, depot or operator | Pools scarce engineering and procurement capability | Ambiguous accountability; one named owner for each asset and safety duty |
| Mixed delivery joint venture | A local industrial partner brings plant/capability | Accelerates technology and workforce transfer | Vendor lock-in; owner retains design/data, audit, step-in and competitive procurement rights |
| Long concession | Only where a funded authority can regulate outputs for decades | Transfers defined service obligations | Financing and information asymmetry; not the default for a first open system |

Do not create one legal entity for every work package. Use one accountable public
owner and one delivery/operator company unless local law requires separation.
Keep manufacturing as an internal business unit, subsidiary or competitively
procured joint venture only when its workload and liabilities justify that step.

## Reserved Decisions And Independence

The owner board reserves route and service outcomes, total budget, borrowing,
land acquisition, major contract awards, safety-policy acceptance, entry into
public trial/revenue service, disposal of assets and changes to open-design/data
rights. The company board controls delivery within those limits.

Four lines must remain distinct even in a lean organisation:

1. **Delivery does the work.** Production and construction managers own output.
2. **Engineering accepts technical conformity.** The design authority controls
   requirements, interfaces, calculations, drawings and deviations.
3. **Quality verifies the record.** Quality can stop work and does not report to
   the production manager whose work it inspects.
4. **Independent assurance challenges the case.** The assessor/checker has direct
   access to the owner board and is not paid on successful approval alone.

The regulator authorises where law requires; it is never treated as the project’s
designer. The project must not self-certify merely because a role exists on an
organisation chart.

## Accountable Executive Team

| Accountable role | Owns | Cannot delegate away |
|---|---|---|
| Owner accountable officer | Public value, funding, land, statutory sponsor duties | Business-case truth, board reporting and reserved decisions |
| Chief executive | Company performance and organisational competence | Clear accountability and adequate resources |
| Programme director | Scope, schedule, interfaces and benefits | Integrated baseline and recovery decisions |
| Chief engineer / design authority | Requirements, technical baseline, interfaces, concessions | Fitness of engineering decisions and competent approval routes |
| Safety and assurance director | Safety-management system, hazard record, assurance plan | Escalation of unacceptable risk and assessor access |
| Operations director | Rulebook, OCC, stations, service delivery and emergency response | Operational readiness and competent operating staff |
| Asset and maintenance director | Maintenance system, depot, spares and whole-life performance | Safe asset condition and maintenance handback |
| Manufacturing director | Vehicle/product factory output, tooling and industrialisation | Production conformity and first-article discipline |
| Infrastructure director | Survey, utilities, civil/track/station delivery | Workfront release, temporary states and construction handover |
| Commercial/local-content director | Packaging, competition, suppliers and claims | Fair procurement, conflicts and auditable local-value evidence |
| Finance director | Controls, cash, treasury, audit and affordability | Solvency, segregated funds and truthful forecasts |
| People/competence director | Workforce plan, labour relations and competence records | Role authorisation, fatigue and training-system integrity |
| Digital/configuration director | CDE, asset IDs, cybersecurity, software and data custody | Reproducible baselines, access control, backup and audit trail |

One person may cover several roles during feasibility, but each decision still
records which hat was worn. Chief engineer, quality lead and safety/assurance
lead must not be subordinated to production output when accepting their own gates.

## Capability Build: People And Facilities

These ranges are mobilisation allowances for one pilot corridor and one LM3
first-article stream, not universal staffing standards. The service plan, local
law, automation level, shifts, contracting strategy and emergency arrangements
must produce the approved establishment.

| Phase / permanent capability | Core client/company people | Delivery workforce outside the core | Purpose |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Feasibility and entity setup | 8–15 | 5–15 advisers/survey specialists | Sponsor, finance, route, engagement, engineering and legal/procurement control |
| Development and procurement | 35–60 | 20–60 designers/investigators | Requirements, surveys, reference adaptation, safety plan, packages and approvals |
| LM3 pilot factory | 20–30 engineering/quality/planning | 80–140 production, stores and test over one shift | Tooling, local parts, vehicle assembly, first article and production learning |
| Infrastructure delivery client | 20–35 | Contractor workforce from quantities/programme | Workfront release, interface control, site assurance and handover |
| Pre-opening operator/maintainer | 35–60 leadership, engineering, trainers and planners | Recruits/trainees sized from timetable and rosters | Rulebook, OCC/depot/stations, trials, emergency exercises and asset takeover |
| Steady operation | 25–45 central functions | Frontline establishment from service, station, depot and shift model | Safe service, maintenance, customer support, renewals and continuous improvement |

Use local apprenticeships and paired roles from the first design package: every
imported specialist has a named local counterpart, deliverable training record
and exit criterion. Count local value from payroll, invoices and origin evidence,
not contract labels.

Minimum initial facilities are a controlled project office/CDE, survey and field
stores, a metrology/quality room, training and simulator room, secure software and
configuration environment, and—when authorised—the pilot factory, depot/OCC and
civil production/laydown sites defined by their engineering packages.

## Management Systems To Establish

Create these as working processes with owners and records before buying large
packages:

- corporate delegations, board calendar, conflicts, whistleblowing and internal audit;
- requirements, interface, configuration, document and design-change control;
- safety, hazard, human-factors, security and emergency-management systems;
- competence, role authorisation, medical/fatigue controls and just-culture reporting;
- procurement, supplier qualification, local-content evidence and counterfeit-part control;
- project controls: scope, estimate, schedule, risk, change, contingency and earned progress;
- factory/site quality: ITPs, travellers, material/serial traceability, calibration and NCRs;
- asset information, maintenance, spares, warranty, obsolescence and return-to-service;
- environmental/social consent, land, utilities, traffic, community complaints and accessibility;
- treasury, invoice/commitment control, external-currency exposure and whole-life renewals reserve;
- cybersecurity, identity/access, backup/restore, incident response and operational-technology boundary.

Start with the repository’s configuration, QA, maintenance and evidence records,
then qualify the deployed CDE/ERP/EAM/identity/signature systems. A Git commit or
server attestation is useful provenance but is not a legal signature or approval.
The [mobilisation status](owner-builder-operator-mobilisation-status.md) now carries
these eleven systems as accountable, gate-linked records. Each stays
`not-established` until all required controlled documents, evidence references and
named approval are present.

## Commercial And Industrial Structure

Package procurement so local firms can qualify without giving one bidder the
entire system:

| Package family | Default commercial route | Owner-retained control |
|---|---|---|
| Survey, ground, utilities and enabling works | Local professional/services and works lots | Control network, data licence, accepted ground model |
| Repetitive precast/steel/civil products | Framework with first-article then call-offs | Product definition, mould/tool ownership, inspection rights |
| Vehicle structures, GFRP and interiors | Local factory or JV with transparent make/buy plan | CAD/source, tooling, BOM, configuration and right to qualify alternatives |
| Specialist doors, brakes, bearings, traction, batteries and control hardware | Competitive performance/interface packages | Interface drawings, diagnostics, spares/data, change notification and escrow where needed |
| System integration and assurance | Retained internal authority plus bounded specialist support | Requirements, hazard/configuration records and final acceptance |
| Operations and maintenance | Public company by default; competitively source support/outcomes | Rulebook, asset history, competence and step-in continuity |

Every contract should identify deliverables, acceptance evidence, open formats,
background/foreground IP, tooling ownership, cybersecurity access, audit rights,
training, local counterpart, spares/obsolescence, warranties, change control,
termination assistance and safe handback. Avoid debt or supply terms that require
an unnamed future monopoly supplier.

## Stage-Gated Mobilisation

| Gate | Indicative timing | What must be true before passing |
|---|---:|---|
| G0 — Public mandate | Month 0–3 | Named sponsor, problem/service outcome, initial funding, corridor protection and accountable officer |
| G1 — Entity and controls | Month 2–6 | Legal form, board/committees, delegations, bank/audit/procurement controls, design/data rights and initial executives |
| G2 — Development baseline | Month 4–12 | Survey plan, stakeholder/land strategy, concept of operations, system requirements, cost/risk baseline and safety/assurance plan |
| G3 — Procure and prototype | Month 9–18 | Competitively packaged work, supplier qualification route, factory/site plans, training partnerships and accepted first-article plans |
| G4 — First articles and enabling works | Month 15–30 | Tooling accepted, first vehicle/civil products tested, site-specific designs checked, NCR learning incorporated and operating organisation recruiting |
| G5 — Construction and operational readiness | Programme-derived | Released workfronts, progressive handover, maintainability/spares data, trained rosters, trial timetable and emergency exercises |
| G6 — Trial and revenue service | Authority-derived | Accepted safety case, independent assessment, insurance, regulator authorisation, asset acceptance and owner-board decision |
| G7 — Stable operation and replication | First 12–24 service months | Reliability/maintenance evidence, defects closed, audited benefits/local value, renewals reserve and controlled lessons fed into the reusable baseline |

Timing overlaps intentionally; passing a later gate never waives an earlier open
condition. Build schedule and headcount from the selected corridor’s resource-loaded
project twin after survey and procurement strategy replace the planning assumptions.
The machine-readable [mobilisation status](owner-builder-operator-mobilisation-status.md)
turns this sequence into 18 dependency-checked work packages over the default
month 0–60 planning horizon and eleven management-system records; every role,
system, package and gate is open in the template.

## First 90 Days

1. Name the owner accountable officer and an interim chief engineer; record all
   statutory duties and decisions that cannot be delegated.
2. Choose the entity model, approve reserved matters, form audit/risk and
   safety/assurance committees, and publish conflicts/procurement rules.
3. Secure the OpenSourceRail technical baseline, CDE, asset-ID/configuration rules,
   repository mirror, backups and data/IP licence in the owner’s control.
4. Recruit the programme, commercial, finance, operations, maintenance, safety,
   quality, survey/land and digital leads; appoint independent assurance through
   a conflict-checked route.
5. Freeze a 12-month development budget and cash-control process, including
   external-currency exposure and explicit contingency authority.
6. Commission corridor survey/ground/utility work, stakeholder engagement,
   concept of operations and the local legal/regulatory route.
7. Turn the generated mechanical, civil, station and city packages into a package
   responsibility matrix; assign an owner and closure date to every open gate.
8. Agree apprenticeship/university/technical-college partners and pair local staff
   with every external specialist from their first deliverable.

## Twelve-Month Definition Of Done

By month 12 the sponsor should be able to show, without relying on a vendor slide
deck:

- the legal owner/operator path and named accountable executives;
- an approved concept of operations, requirements baseline and route evidence plan;
- a cost, funding, cashflow, risk and external-capital envelope with sensitivities;
- a procurement/package plan that preserves competition and owner-held design data;
- a safety/assurance strategy agreed in principle with the authorising route;
- factory, depot, OCC, civil production and construction mobilisation plans;
- a competence and workforce pipeline tied to actual roles and shifts;
- an immutable open-gate register linking every claim to evidence and an owner;
- a board decision either to proceed to first articles, re-scope, pause or stop.
