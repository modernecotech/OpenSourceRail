# OpenSourceRail delivery roadmap

OpenSourceRail connects three products: city planning and service, railway engineering and manufacturing, and controls and maintenance. Local design, fabrication, repair and open knowledge are the governing economic aims. The [project introduction](../README.md) explains the full platform; the [architecture](ARCHITECTURE.md), [engineering status](ENGINEERING-STATUS.md), [cost baseline](COST-BASELINE.md) and [v0.7 release record](RELEASE-v0.7.md) are the current decision documents.

The v0.6 passive vehicle, the v0.7 synthetic 30 km railway, the Samawah case and the Baghdad protected-conductor network have separate candidate identities and cost assumptions. A change in one does not revise the others. The [v0.6 engineering findings](design-engine/v06-design-closure/README.md) and [maglev archive](archives/v0.5-maglev/manifest.json) remain historical research, outside current release checks.

## Current development

1. Keep the selected digital candidate joined through route, train, propulsion, brakes, civil, controls, production, timetable and fourteen capital scopes. Change affected quantities and costs with the candidate identity.
2. Maintain the representative car, precast span, station interface and control-electronics schedules as one production planning package. Count mounting, cabling, cooling, access, assembly and repair work.
3. Develop the [Baghdad concepts](../cities/Baghdad/README.md) with zero tunnels, an elevated protected-conductor preferred candidate, a city-background map, one capital and whole-life estimate per concept, and consistent train, station and power assumptions.
4. Keep the Workbench journey and [ERPNext/Frappe HR](../deployment/erpnext/README.md) and [FUXA/gateway](../deployment/supervision/README.md) handoffs attached to selected hardware and source-bound records. A dry run remains identified as a dry run.
5. Reduce routine build time and checkout size by rebuilding affected outputs, retaining one source for published assets, and keeping historical numerical replay outside normal development.

The digital release makes no claim of measured traction, brake or material performance, a surveyed alignment, supplier quotations, fabricated equipment or passenger-operation approval. Future project delivery must use the applicable engineering, procurement and safety processes. The [current release record](RELEASE-v0.7.md) states exactly what v0.7 published.
