## Method

`(material quantity × dated statistical material rate + declared person-hours × dated base-wage rate) × 1.20`

The 20% is an unallocated planning uplift on the represented direct material plus base labour only. It is not verified overhead, profit, tax, contingency or margin. Hours are assumed, not measured. The CSO wage is a statistical base wage, not an employer-loaded shop rate.
