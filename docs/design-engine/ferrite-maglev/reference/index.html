Therefore a 600 kW total electrical cap cannot maintain that acceleration to 80 km/h; the controller must taper acceleration or the power system must be resized.Open
MAG-050Produce reproducible 2D screening models and 3D finite-element models with nonlinear steel, grade-specific magnet curves, finite array ends, module seams, winding slots, joints, conductive covers, mechanical tolerances and mesh convergence. Export SI-unit force and loss maps with geometry/material/model revision identifiers. Verify force by at least two independent methods where practical, such as Maxwell stress and virtual work.Open
MAG-051Publish the usable operating envelope, including lift capacity at the weakest magnet state and maximum normal gap, lateral stability/control margin, continuous and transient current limits, saturation margin, demagnetisation margin, thermal limits and torque capacity for asymmetric loads. A suggested initial screening reserve of 1.3 times AW3 static weight gives 382.59/395.34 kN in Cases A/B. This is a project assumption, not a substitute for deriving dynamic design loads or a regulatory safety factor. The full AW3 body/rail load and asymmetric passenger distribution must be retained.Open
MAG-052Couple these maps into a six-degree-of-freedom vehicle model and flexible guideway model. Include actuator delay, sensor noise and bias, bus-voltage sag, current saturation, force ripple and structural resonance. Determine control bandwidth from unstable mode growth and disturbance rejection; microcontroller clock speed is not a stability proof.Open
MAG-053Verify failure cases individually and in credible combinations: a magnet block cracked or incorrectly oriented; a missing module; a coil open or short circuit; an inverter stuck on or off; failed gap sensor; stale communications; guideway joint offset; a blocked cooling path; and common DC-bus interruption. A failed coil does not automatically remove permanent-magnet attraction. A shorted coil may generate speed-dependent forces and heat. Capture these effects in the fault model.Open
MAG-054Prove stationary support and guidance, controlled stopping, power-off support and recovery separately. Fixed skids or capture surfaces may avoid deployment mechanisms, but require load, friction, energy and stability analysis. An AW3 31 t car at 80 km/h contains approximately 7.65 MJ of translational kinetic energy; any emergency stopping system must account for its energy path. Permanent magnets remaining magnetised do not by themselves guarantee contactless stability after a control failure.Open
MAG-055Use progressive evidence gates: material coupon and block acceptance; static instrumented module rig; force-versus-gap/offset and thermal correlation; restrained closed-loop levitation; moving sled with representative joints and speed-dependent rail losses; multiple-module rigid/flexible body; and a representative loaded car/beam demonstrator. Do not advance from bench levitation directly to passenger operation.Open
