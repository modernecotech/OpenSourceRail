Passenger junctions are transfers between independent fixed lines; no physical train routing or switch is inferred. Fixed accelerator modules do not create a passenger station or rescue facility without its independently priced equipment.

Rope mass and minimum breaking force use the manufacturer [Bridon6Series table](https://ropes.bekaert.com/au/en/home/products/6-series). Termination efficiency and design factors are project assumptions; no railway rope assembly or UK manufacture is confirmed. Prices are unquoted targets.

Complete active winding/force/thermal design, embedded anchors, portal connections, certified winch/gear/brake/rope assemblies, soils, independent power, located access, evacuation and physical acceptance remain open.
