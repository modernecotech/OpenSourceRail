# Seat-led comfort and lightweight equipment options

OpenSourceRail investigates simple, locally maintainable trains on concrete beams with passive steel facings. Permanent magnets and suspension controls stay onboard; stations provide propulsion. This study tests a less crowded cabin concept with more seating, while showing its mass and passenger-throughput consequences. No comfort, structural or operating qualification is claimed.

[System ethos and current status](../../../../README.md) · [Engineering readiness](../engineering-readiness/README.md)

**Research default: 44 fixed seats + two wheelchair users, zero standing = 46 people/car.** The default is 2+2 seating with local access/post clearances: seven complete four-place rows and six two-place rows, plus four wall-bench seats. A declared 450 mm clear pan, 800 mm pitch and 650 mm aisle are retained. Wall benches occupy the opposite side of the two door zones; wheelchair bays use the end regions. These dimensions are hypotheses, not accessibility approval.

The alternative places wheelchair bays opposite the doors: 48 fixed seats + two wheelchair users = 50 people/car. Those two bays replace the door-zone benches. The wider-pan control fails the retained clear width; the 2.65 m exterior width is not usable interior width.

## Lightweight parts: what was counted

- **Seats and benches:** the layout ledger counts 2 mm GFRP pan/back stock, foam pads, aluminium RHS supports and M8 fastener stock. That thin construction fails the separate 1.5 kN/place component fixture. A 30 mm-core sandwich on deeper aluminium rails is the next structural experiment, not an installed-seat release. Bonds, seat restraints, backrest loads, fire performance and complete joints remain open.
- **Doors:** two doors on one side replace four total doors. The ordinary platform already used two active doors, so normal boarding frontage is retained. Deleted openings receive counted opaque infill; the existing aperture frames remain. Platform side, terminal turnback, rescue access and emergency egress need a route-wide design.
- **Windows:** 33 glazing cases compare the exact laminated-glass stock against coated rail-polycarbonate proposals, including retention, seals and subdividing mullions. Thin PC gives a density saving but may leave the linear plate model domain. A thicker, subdivided option can exceed the glass stock mass. Typical supplier material properties do not qualify the proposed window system.
- **Floors, roof and aluminium chassis:** separate panel/member alternatives count material and added interfaces. Necessary bending/shear fixtures do not establish local passenger loads, complete body modes, fatigue, fire or hot-weather performance. Their prospective savings are not subtracted from the same-parent vehicle comparison.
- **HVAC:** two, four and five package proposals carry their separate known hardware mass. The five-unit reference is retained for the main comparison. Thermal sensitivities preserve fresh-air supply per person and cover dry/humid 50°C cases. Actual supplier output, input power and N−1 performance at 50°C remain unknown.

One two-place bench fixture reaches 11.47 kg and 4.59 mm combined deflection under the declared 1.5 kN/place load. The first three door options fail the declared 1 kPa/10 mm strip-deflection fixture. The next iteration retains those failures and adds 60/80 mm-core doors: 22.93/27.49 kg per two-leaf opening and 6.65/3.63 mm deflection. Both pass that necessary fixture, keep the 41 kg operator reference and are heavier than the 20.45 kg reference leaf stock with explicit fasteners. Their 61/81 mm leaf depths need a new track, opening, access and egress interface evaluation.

The 0.9 mm aluminium-skin/H80 floor counts 530.54 kg versus 700.53 kg retained floor stock and passes the declared necessary fixture. The first roof options, including the retained reference under this particular strip/support hypothesis, fail the 2.125 m span-deflection target. A second iteration adds eight counted transverse aluminium crossrails and counts their joint stock and checks panel/rail forces and deflection separately. Any saving must include those supports; the 150×60×3 mm rail option reaches 494.72 kg total roof stock, only 23.36 kg below the retained reference after 95.28 kg of added rails, joints and clips. It passes the declared fixture with a proposed 162 mm depth. Global two-way action, actual attachments, headroom and HVAC clearance still need a matched model.

Panel comparisons use declared continuous-section stiffness and local strip loads; tiled joint continuity and whole-body participation remain unknown. Bench loads and door-pressure fixtures are declared research hypotheses, not acceptance criteria. Source material URLs and temperature limitations are included with each record.

Primary material references: [coated rail polycarbonate](https://plaskolite.com/docs/default-source/pds/pds183_tuf_tg.pdf), [Divinycell H core](https://diab-media.azureedge.net/eyajkrhd/diab-divinycell-h-may-2026-rev26-si.pdf) and [glass-epoxy plate comparator](https://www.hippe.de/en/products/detail/product/ep-gc-202-as). Their published coupon properties do not qualify the new assemblies.

## What this does to the suspension gap

At 28 mm and 80°C the smaller retained PM force contour exceeds the default's 1.3× accounted load by 1,364 N/module, before unclosed extra mass. The two contour calculations still differ by 2,035 N/module. Those samples are not confidence bounds and are not a converged force map.

Lower payload improves arithmetic reserve, but at an unchanged attractive-PM gap can also cause excess upward force. A stable equilibrium, lateral restoring/damping behaviour, sufficient active-current authority, thermal duty and safe power-loss response remain unproved. No force at a new gap or current is invented. No new magnetic finite-element solve or physical journey was executed for this cabin study.

## Comfort and capacity must be presented together

The defensible description is **higher seated share and less crowding at a lower declared capacity**. Ride quality, accessibility and thermal comfort still require evidence. Matching the original throughput with shorter headways needs more departures, fleet, station power and a proven protected-arrival system; it is not established by the arithmetic. Alternatively, evaluate longer formations and their platforms as a separate specification. No city baseline or structural crowd load has been silently reduced.

## Evidence and next work

Executed: 72 bounded layout variants ({'pass': 50, 'fail': 22}), 33 glazing variants, 22 panel options, 14 bench/door component fixtures and 36 composed mass/support/thermal/service comparisons. Exact geometry, source hashes, failures and unknowns are retained in [the compressed study](study.json.gz). [Compact results](summary.json), [receipt](manifest.json) and [interactive comparison](../../../workbench/trip/readiness/comfort/) use the same study identity.

Next: rebuild one equipped native body with actual seat/door/window positions; establish restraints and accessible swept paths; validate the hot-weather glazing/HVAC package; converge multiaxis magnetic/current maps; then qualify hover, faults and a same-candidate journey. Panel and frame changes require their own matched structural and magnetic interface evaluation.
