# Exact candidate native module

FreeCAD and STEP occupied volumes/centroids recover the same module mass from declared densities. Existing exact same-definition CAD/STEP and their reopen receipts are retained unchanged when their exporter/source hashes agree.

Retained native triangles are independently checked for finite nonempty closed oriented topology, exact source/void surface membership, bounds, signed volume and centroid. Tessellation volume tolerance is1%; BRep remains1e-7. The deterministic orthographic image is checked pixel-for-pixel in its geometry viewport. All96 decoded frames of the four-second/four-stage video are checked against native geometry masks/material colours; fonts and encoder bytes are excluded from semantic matching.

The clip is final-position part visibility, not motion, collision, lifting or assembly-procedure simulation. Complete wire routing, cooling, connectors, fit and physical qualification remain open. No manufacturing or physical release is claimed.
