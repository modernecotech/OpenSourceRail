This bounded study applies the package-07 compact-drive body to Samawah's
existing five-line outline. It is a useful **held planning result**, not a
service or project-cost closure. It does not replace or edit the earlier city
publication.

The kinematic request uses 20 m/s, 0.4 m/s² acceleration and 0.6 m/s²
deceleration as declared calculation assumptions. It does not demonstrate that
the train can achieve those values. No available-force map, independent
braking map, grade profile, local prices, ground profile or utility survey is
provided. Vehicle mass values retain their source uncertainty; installed mass
is incomplete.

One concrete geometry conflict is retained: two line-1 station markers are
79.977 m apart while the request uses a 120 m standard platform. No station was
silently removed or merged. The line needs an alignment/access review.

All fourteen lifecycle accounts are present, but complete scope totals and
whole-life NPV remain `null`. The BOM/process subtotal is an incomplete known
quantity only. Guideway counts are reference quantities without the detailed
civil load check. Two production/civil shifts do not cover the declared
18-hour service day; staff roster and relief coverage remain unknown.
