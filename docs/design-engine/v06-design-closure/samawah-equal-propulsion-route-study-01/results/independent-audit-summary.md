# Independent packet audit

An independent read-only audit of attempt 02 confirmed the 45 payload hashes, root manifest and comparison/route-generation identities. The baseline and revised study manifests and their 20 artifacts each verified; the 65 baseline and 67 revised source hashes matched the pinned source commit. Both studies report replay verification and did not reuse cached studies. The audit did not rerun the workflow and was not OS-sandboxed.

The audit confirmed the same vehicle/body identities, fixed five-line propulsion policy and common demand, headway, occupancy, availability, speed and acceleration inputs. It also noted that adaptive dwell is derived and can change with the route/runtime. Stations remained fixed, and all sampled route deviations stayed below the 20 m tolerance.

The route length falls 5.64% and fleet request falls from 71 to 68, while base CAPEX and NPV rise 9.27% and 3.06%. The revised case includes a $97.93m base allowance for unsurveyed right-of-way, access and crossings. It remains a planning allowance, not a quote or measured construction cost. No physically supported available-force map exists; the comparison does not establish a feasible journey or physical acceptance.
