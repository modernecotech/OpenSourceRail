## Connected calculations

- Five equipped vehicle proposals propagate through both 1.5 km and 2 km cells. Each physical cell binds the actual vehicle, launch equipment, modular civil geometry and formation/route. Vehicle-component ancestry stays explicit; the old LIM architecture is not relabelled as mechanical drive.
- Eight propulsion distributions include sparse/distributed/continuous LIM, stationary pinch rollers, cable/tug and a separate onboard-rescue investigation. Twelve roller count/location layouts (the baseline and eleven physical revisions) have their own hardware IDs, counted inventory, necessary force screens and next-experiment frontier. No integrated winner is inferred from incomplete prices or force maps.
- Mechanical trajectories use finite moving contact, rated shaft torque/power, gearing and explicit clamp/grip/contact hypotheses. The controller requests acceleration; actual capacity caps determine supplied force. Contact/time faults split integration steps. Mass and force uncertainty are independent. Work, kinetic energy, resistance, grade, independent brake pressure/adhesion, counted-disc adiabatic heating and auxiliary energy are calculated together.
- Every main cell retains thirteen contexts: empty/normal/crowd at both mass bounds, wind/heat, uphill/downhill, failed bank, power loss, emergency braking and low adhesion. Terminal arrival, requested-service screens, model validity and physical approval remain separate.
- Actual 24-axle positions and finite station-drive contacts transfer trajectory loads to the generated guideway. Static weight excludes the rotational equivalent mass. Out-of-guideway and unexecuted contexts stay visible. Sampled simple-span reactions do not establish wheel–rail, beam, bearing or foundation qualification.
- Station platforms use forty transportable 5.5 m cassettes across two stations. Defined girders, bearings, columns and footings carry declared dead/crowd loads. Motor stock is applied at its actual longitudinal locations, with unweighed gearboxes, cooling, protection and other plant held open.
- Dimensioned blanks produce net/gross stock, removal and offcut quantities, process routings and conditional labour/tooling plans. Purchased envelopes have no invented raw-material yield. Rates are explicitly Class C assumptions; complete installed prices and whole-life costs remain unknown. ERPNext drafts remain disabled.
- The read-only Workbench exposes architecture/ancestry, mass and cost, scenario trajectories, closure/tests, and production/construction. Part definitions and physical occurrences can be inspected separately.

## Actual native methods

The retained motor packages execute a moving-secondary field/circuit model using BDF2 after one backward-Euler startup. Independent R–L analytic controls and saved trace/source/tool checks are separate from physical validation. Three matched-horizon runs measure temporal and spatial sensitivity; numerical field-work defects are not relabelled as physical heat.

Located-stock structural calculations run six-DOF frames and directional plate grillages with finite offset joints, mounted equipment/passengers and two meshes. Cantilever, torsion, joint and Navier-plate controls check the mathematics. Material and connection assumptions do not supply actual laminate, fatigue, hot/wet, fire, buckling, crash or joint allowables. Large displacements and mesh sensitivity require model/design work before any selection.

The original structural producer's saved-state verifier rejects equal JSON floats and NumPy scalars. A separate, source-pinned review adapter normalizes numerical representation while retaining the original tolerances and reconstructing geometry, mass loads and matrix response arithmetic. Original sources, inputs, results and logs are preserved. This is neither another field solve nor physical correlation.

## Inputs and remaining physical work

`intake.template(bundle)` defines owned, hashed, candidate-bound inputs. Authentication and replay providers come from trusted application code, never input JSON. Machine-specific paths, self-issued approvals and mixed service formations are rejected. Unresolved material/component/site/quote values never become zero. The mechanical operating-map adapter is held until measured contact/slip/spin-up and controlled brake models exist; it cannot inherit a P1 LIM map.

No physical records have been supplied. Prototype motor/contact rigs, brake and bogie tests, laminate/joint/body tests, repeated electrical/thermal duty, geotechnics, HIL/sensing/timing, independently reviewed protection/recovery, manufacturing trials, commissioning and an operating pilot remain necessary. A successful build cannot complete those steps. The existing qualification workflow remains authoritative; this numerical layer cannot issue approval.
