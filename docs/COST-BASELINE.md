# v0.7 cost and delivery baseline

**2026 US dollars. One planning estimate per named configuration.** These are OpenSourceRail engineering budgets, not supplier bids. The quantities, rates, fleet and programme for each configuration are in the linked ledger.

| Configuration | Installed capital | Per route-km | 30-year real present cost | Delivery programme |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| [Synthetic 30 km reference corridor](reference-v07/cost/README.md) · 276-place powered-bogie train, 12 stations, 13-strand 10 m precast span | **US$1.025bn** | **US$34.17m** | **US$1.668bn** at 4% | **43 months** after approved corridor definition |
| [Baghdad 148.2 km first programme](../cities/Baghdad/COST-AND-DELIVERY.md) · 700-place protected-conductor train, 64 stations, all elevated | **US$7.866bn** | **US$53.07m** | **US$13.986bn** at 4% | **2027–2031**, five-year planning build |
| [Baghdad 605.652 km independent citywide concept](../cities/Baghdad/COST-AND-DELIVERY.md) · 700-place protected-conductor train, 280 stations, all elevated | **US$31.018bn** | **US$51.21m** | **US$54.763bn** at 4% | **2027–2035**, overlapping packages |

The two Baghdad networks are independent alternatives. The 605.652 km capital is a standalone estimate and does not build upon the 148.2 km concept. The synthetic corridor and Baghdad have different service, fleet, stations, civil mix and cost rates; their totals are not interchangeable. Baghdad's higher-capacity train is a separate child concept and does not inherit the 276-place vehicle's traction or braking performance. Each ledger shows its own counted direct work, project allowance, operation and renewals. Debt interest, financing fees, fare revenue and foreign-exchange risk belong in a project financing model.

The 30 km budget counts fourteen capital scopes and one 20% project-risk allowance. Its direct scope includes 51 six-car sets, 6,000 joint-free 10 m beams, 6,002 support positions, 60 track-km and twelve accessible stations. It prices an overhead-fed powered-bogie concept rather than station-launch machinery. The [vehicle package](reference-v07/vehicle/README.md) and [civil package](reference-v07/civil/README.md) give the dimensions, loads, equipment counts and construction method behind the budget.

The Baghdad estimate counts its own fourteen scopes, 242 and 934 trainsets including reserve, depots, stations, viaduct and protected traction supply. Both networks have zero tunnels and no overhead wires in the selected planning candidate. Its first programme uses the [Iraqi 148 km, seven-line, 64-station RFI](https://investpromo.gov.iq/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/3.-RFI-English-%D9%86%D8%A8%D8%B0%D8%A9-%D8%B9%D9%86-%D9%85%D8%AA%D8%B1%D9%88-%D8%A8%D8%BA%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%AF-E-20.2.2024.pdf) for network scale. The 605.652 km map, station allowance and delivery rate are strategic planning assumptions.

Baghdad's annual operation uses a peak-all-day upper bound on assigned passenger paths, not a route timetable. The independent citywide plan costs 50.099 km of reserved guideway without passenger service. Its monthly beam schedule includes a casting ramp and one-month release lag, while the additional yard/front costs remain unpriced.

The earlier [US$737.991m synthetic station-launch calculation](design-engine/line30-current-20261117/README.md) remains a different-candidate research control. It is not the v0.7 powered-bogie budget or a Baghdad unit rate. Supplier quotes, measured installed mass, approved civil details and surveyed ground will replace the explicit planning allowances when the prototype and procurement designs advance.
