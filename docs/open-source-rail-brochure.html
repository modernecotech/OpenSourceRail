For cities that want useful rail, built to last

Give your city a railway it can own.

OpenSourceRail helps public transport leaders plan, build and operate modern urban rail while keeping the skills, jobs and decisions that matter close to home.

Start with a clear service plan and a maintainable asset base. Test the network before construction, connect design to delivery, and grow from a depot or pilot corridor toward dependable citywide service. The public portfolio contains 265 developing-world city models across 43 country programmes.

What the city gets

For passengers

More legible routes, useful service plans and a railway designed around real stations, capacity and daily journeys.

For local industry

Open interfaces and repeatable packages support local fabrication, integration, training and long-term maintenance.

For decision-makers

See cost, energy, delivery sequence and evidence together before a city commits to a build path.

From ambition to operation

  1. Choose a city, corridor and service ambition.
  2. Generate and compare a deterministic city digital twin.
  3. Review routes, stations, fleet, energy, cost and buildability.
  4. Turn the selected design into CAD, IFC, quantities and delivery packages.
  5. Start with a depot, workshop or pilot corridor and train local teams.
  6. Expand only as field evidence, assurance and operating capability mature.

From city intent to accountable maintenance
A city operating advantage

Make the railway easier to plan, deliver and care for.

One connected workbench gives city teams a shared view of the network, projects, assets, work orders, simulations and evidence, with each authority still clear.

A shared city picture

City Studio, simulation, OCC training, ERPNext/Frappe HR, FUXA and railway assurance share the same city, asset and revision context. Leaders can see what is proposed, operators can see what is ready, and maintainers can see what needs attention.

Shape the service

Explore corridors, stations, demand, capacity and civil choices over local GIS before a route becomes a construction commitment.

Control delivery

Turn an approved design into assets, work packages, schedules, order-by dates, local/imported cashflow and manufacturing quality gates.

Run the railway

Connect asset registers, work orders, maintenance plans, defects, inspections and handback evidence to the equipment people actually operate.

Build confidence

Keep GIS, CAD, IFC, cost, simulation and test outputs tied to source and tool hashes. Provenance is visible; approval remains a human and regulatory decision.

Start where people feel the value

Adopt the non-safety owner-operator stack at an existing depot, workshop or pilot corridor: ERPNext + Frappe HR + railway Ops Core + simulator + asset/evidence portal. Improve reliability and maintenance discipline before any train-control software is certified.

A responsible path to service

This is planning and engineering-screening evidence, not a safety certificate, construction release, supplier quote, approval or operating authority. The target is GoA 4, but train control stays in simulation or shadow mode until deployment evidence is independently assessed.
