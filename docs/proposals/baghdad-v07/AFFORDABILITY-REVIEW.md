# Baghdad v0.7 | capital-cost correction and design-to-cost work

**17 November 2026 correction.** The US$18.0bn and US$69.6bn figures in the first proposal are **conventional-metro budget comparators**, not measured OpenSourceRail capital requirements. The former is US$121.5m per route-km, almost the same scale as the [US$17.5bn Baghdad programme reported by the Czech Embassy in 2024](https://mzv.gov.cz/baghdad/cz/obchod_a_ekonomika/hospodarska_odvetvi_iraku/kolejova_doprava/ohlaseny_projekt_vystavby_metra_v.html). That similarity follows from the model's conventional civil and station assumptions; it is not proof of a cheaper local railway. The 605.65 km comparator is US$115.0m/km. Both should be kept as **controls to beat**, not used as financing requests or advertised OpenSourceRail installed prices.

## What made the numbers high

| Assumption in the 14-scope ledger | Concept A, 148.2 km | Concept B, 605.65 km | Engineering issue |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Elevated civil at **US$45m/km** | US$5.002bn | US$20.441bn | No accepted Baghdad beam, pier, foundation, joint, erection or supplier-price bill supports this generic rate. |
| Tunnels at **15% of route** and US$120m/km | US$2.668bn | US$10.902bn | A copies the [2024 government RFI](https://investpromo.gov.iq/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/3.-RFI-English-%D9%86%D8%A8%D8%B0%D8%A9-%D8%B9%D9%86-%D9%85%D8%AA%D8%B1%D9%88-%D8%A8%D8%BA%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%AF-E-20.2.2024.pdf); B copied its fraction onto a zero-tunnel research map and unsurveyed infill. B has **no mapped 90.85 km tunnel takeoff**. |
| Stations/access at US$12m-55m per asset | US$1.582bn | US$5.526bn | Access, egress, lifts, fire safety and interchanges must remain, but standard station geometry, shared access and local construction have not been designed or quoted. |
| 20% contingency on every direct scope | US$3.000bn | US$11.606bn | Explicit risk provision is necessary; a blanket percentage cannot prove that unknown ground, utility, airport and power work is covered. |

The first three categories are **61.7% of A's direct cost and 63.5% of B's**. Even granting civil construction for free, the retained non-civil scope would still be **US$8.53bn for A and US$30.93bn for B**. This shows that reducing the viaduct rate alone cannot deliver the earlier 30 km benchmark's all-in US$24.6m/km. Fleet, stations, traction, grid, land, depot and assurance need the same engineering challenge. Possible work-breakdown overlap between deck/walkway, station entrance/land works and depot/tooling has not been demonstrated; no saving can be claimed until the same physical item is reconciled once in a candidate-bound bill.

## The project's lower-cost evidence and its limit

The [current-source 30 km OpenSourceRail benchmark](../../design-engine/line30-current-20261117/README.md) has a provisional all-in base of **US$24.6m/km** and an elevated civil component of **US$3.59m/km**. Its current evaluation is `947dd7cbcac7c260da721150912fba47531c96fd3f7a28365c394249608ee869`; the older benchmark remains historical. The component rate is about **12.5 times lower** than the Baghdad comparator's US$45m/km. It is a synthetic 20 m beam/foundation/erection proxy, not an installed Baghdad quote. A segmented transportable beam would add an engineered joint, temporary support, extra lifts, QA and local ground/traffic work. Stations, river crossings, utility diversions, power and the complete vehicle are not made cheap by transferring that component rate.

As a transparent *one-variable arithmetic check only*, replacing the elevated-civil rate with US$3.59m/km while retaining all other 13 Baghdad scopes, quantities, design allowance and contingency gives US$12.20bn for A and US$45.93bn for B. **These are not estimates, offers or affordability targets.** They demonstrate that the published comparators did not price the project's own civil hypothesis. Removing all assumed tunnels would be another unproven route change; land, river, heritage, airport and disruption constraints may make it impossible or more expensive. Mandatory safety, accessibility, evacuation and rescue remain in every option.

## The engineering route to one credible affordable price

1. **Choose actual civil classes per corridor.** Survey right of way, airport entrance, Tigris crossings, utilities, flood levels and geotechnical zones. For B, stop importing A's tunnel fraction. Retain infeasible surface/elevated trials as evidence.
2. **Qualify one repeated viaduct system.** Carry complete twin-track deck, 20 m span or justified alternative, bearings, joints, piers, foundations, rail, fasteners, walkways, seismic and erection stages into a 1.5-2 km demonstrator. Measure fabrication hours, lifting, possessions, reject rate and local supplier prices.
3. **Price one complete accessible station family.** Include the 102 m full-train platform, egress, lifts, fire/life safety, utilities, passenger protection and interchange access. Develop elevated, ground and any required underground variants from site layouts.
4. **Select equal-service trains and propulsion.** The current 276-place six-car vehicle at four-minute headway cannot carry the RFI's 3.25m/day hypothesis. Measure demand and decide formation, platform, headway, installed mass, station power, braking, grid, rescue and fleet as one candidate. Compare station launch and powered-wheel research at the same service.
5. **Reconcile one installed bill and cash-flow model.** Every counted component appears once across civil, vehicle, stations, power and depots; local/UK origin, tooling, labour, spares, OPEX and renewals are explicit. Get Iraqi bids and independent checks before publishing a single OpenSourceRail capital estimate and a bank case.

**Current decision:** retain the two route concepts and correct the price status. There is **no defensible single OpenSourceRail installed-capital estimate** for either concept today. A cheaper number created by replacing an allowance before physical qualification would mislead Amanat Baghdad and lenders. The [arithmetic audit](evidence/affordability-cost-result.json) and [civil challenge](evidence/affordability-civil-result.json) preserve the exact inputs, sensitivity calculations and holds.
