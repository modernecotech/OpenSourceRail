# Baghdad v0.7 | cost and financing note

**Price basis: November 2026 nominal US dollars. These are two concept-level programme funding envelopes, not supplier bids, approved estimates, borrowing requests or UKEF commitments.** The separate [Concept A ledger](cost-ledger-a.json) and [Concept B ledger](cost-ledger-b.json) each carry 14-scope arithmetic, quantity/rate assumptions and source hashes. One base figure is presented for each concept; unresolved quantities remain explicit in the ledgers.

| Programme | One base capital funding envelope | Quantity basis |
|---|---:|---|
| A: seven-line first programme | **US$18.0 billion** | 148.2 km, 64 assumed station classes, four depots; official RFI civil mix of 15% underground, 10% at grade, 75% elevated |
| B: 2035 metropolitan programme | **US$69.6 billion** | 605.65 km schematic candidate inside the 500–800 km vision; 226 line-stop markers priced without an unproven interchange credit, 12 depots assumed |

Both figures include a 20% explicit contingency over 14 direct scopes: tunnel/portals; elevated civil; at-grade civil; stations/access/evacuation; track/guideway/walkways; signalling/control/comms; station power/braking; grid/feeder interfaces; rolling stock; depots; land/utilities/disruption; design/supervision/assurance; testing/commissioning; and rescue/tooling/local industry. A is **US$15.002bn direct plus US$3.000bn contingency**. B is **US$58.028bn direct plus US$11.606bn contingency**. These are transparent budget hypotheses with transferred or assumed rates. The A envelope is similar in order of magnitude to the **US$17.5bn** 2024 reported project figure, but similarity does not validate either number. The [official RFI](https://investpromo.gov.iq/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/3.-RFI-English-%D9%86%D8%A8%D8%B0%D8%A9-%D8%B9%D9%86-%D9%85%D8%AA%D8%B1%D9%88-%D8%A8%D8%BA%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%AF-E-20.2.2024.pdf) gives route and scope hypotheses, not a tendered construction price; the reported figure appears in the [Czech Embassy's 2024 project summary](https://mzv.gov.cz/baghdad/cz/obchod_a_ekonomika/hospodarska_odvetvi_iraku/kolejova_doprava/ohlaseny_projekt_vystavby_metra_v.html).

The B mapped research network is about 513.34 km before its airport extension. Six new screened infill corridors total 89.21 km of schematic chords, making the 605.65 km budget candidate. The US$69.6bn figure is tied to **that** geometry and its 226 line-stop markers, not to the earlier arbitrary 600 km sensitivity. These are line-stop assets, **not 226 proven unique station sites**; 12 infill anchors lie within 600 m of existing stops and may share interchange access, subject to design. The priced civil class split, depots, fleet and powered zones remain assumptions. The earlier 494.43 km dossier's US$8.35bn rate cannot be prorated: its costed route measure conflicts with `city.json`'s 513.34 km, it has zero tunnels, and its fleet, sites and equipment differ.

## What the numbers do and do not buy

The rates are November 2026 programme assumptions. They are neither Iraqi quotations nor escalated from the [World Bank's Bogotá elevated-metro appraisal](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/771821533439847685/pdf/PAD-final-07162018.pdf) and [São Paulo Line 2 appraisal](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099022725110032757/pdf/BOSIB-0b1c6377-dcd4-470d-88ca-2342fc535d3e.pdf), which are scope comparators only. The underground civil allowance needs particular challenge against site-specific stations, ground, water, utilities and river crossings. The trainset, powered-zone, depot, access and grid counts are provisional; the 276-place six-car reference cannot carry the RFI daily forecast on the stated service. The ledger has positive allowances for equipment and fitout awaiting detailed placement. No unlocated physical item is set to zero merely because its installation is not drawn.

No responsible **annual operating cost, fare revenue, debt-service coverage, life-cycle cost or local value-added percentage** can be calculated for the revised candidates until demand, normal train service, traction duty, fleet, energy tariff, staffing, maintenance and replacement cycles are chosen. The earlier 14-line outline's US$344.9m/year OPEX is for a different service and is not transferred. Interest during construction, financing and guarantee fees, taxes/duties, inflation, foreign-exchange movement and the actual land settlement are also not established by these capital envelopes. Those items must appear in the next lender model rather than silently being treated as included.

## Financing structure to develop with named parties

1. **Public sponsor and rights.** Amanat Baghdad/NIC confirm the 2024 DBOMFT concession position, procuring authority, land/utility obligations and any winning consortium rights. The Iraqi Ministry of Finance must confirm any guarantee, debt or availability-payment authority.
2. **Affordability.** Obtain a measured origin-destination study and fares/concessions policy; calculate phased passenger demand, annual OPEX, renewals, cash flow and affordability support. The old illustrative US$1 fare case did not cover its own OPEX before debt, so neither new concept should rely on farebox debt service without a new model and funded public payment obligation.
3. **Package financing.** Name Iraqi/local manufacture and construction lots, UK exportable supply lots, other international content and the buyer down payment. [UKEF Buyer Credit](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/buyer-credit-facility) can support up to 85% of an **eligible UK export contract**, with at least 15% direct buyer payment and an acceptable bank; it is not 85% of either whole network. [UKEF's UK-content approach](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/ukefs-approach-to-foreign-content/ukefs-approach-to-foreign-content) and [Iraq country cover](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/country-cover-policy-and-indicators) require case-specific assessment. The country page's £1bn–£2bn market appetite is indicative, not allocated project finance.
4. **Credit and ESHR package.** Obtain EPC/O&M and supplier quotes, independent traffic and technical advice, staged draws, debt service and reserves, currency and interest hedges, security, sponsor equity, covenant model, environmental/social and human-rights assessment, land/resettlement plan, anti-bribery/sanctions review and public consultation. [UKEF project assessment](https://www.business.gov.uk/how-we-assess-your-project/) and [ESHR policy](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/uk-export-finance-environmental-social-and-human-rights-policy/policy-and-practice-on-environmental-social-and-human-rights-due-diligence-and-monitoring) govern the relevant UK-backed tranche.

**Financing decision today:** fund and authorise the comparative feasibility, surveying, demonstrator and procurement development. Construction debt or a UKEF application for the whole programme is not supported by the current evidence. Each eventual tranche needs its own bankable, candidate-bound cost and revenue case.
