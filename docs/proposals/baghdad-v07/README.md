# Baghdad urban rail | v0.7 proposal for Amanat Baghdad

**17 November 2026 | Engineering concept and financing discussion | No route or financing approval implied**

[Presentation PDF](../../../output/pdf/Baghdad-Metro-v0.7-Proposal.pdf) · [Cost and finance](COST-AND-FINANCE.md) · [Candidate and evidence register](EVIDENCE.md) · [Concept A GIS](rfi-schematic.geojson) · [Concept B GIS](concept-b-2035-map.geojson)

This proposal places two different programmes before Amanat Baghdad. **Concept A** adapts the National Investment Commission's seven-line, 148.2 km 2024 request for information (RFI) into an initial metro programme. **Concept B** tests a 605.65 km schematic metropolitan network within a **500–800 km** 2035 vision. The two concepts have separate physical and evaluation identities. A is a potential first part of B, but neither inherits the other's cost, demand, fleet or physical acceptance. A build-out completion date of 2035 is a programme objective, not a construction commitment.

**Recommended immediate decision:** mandate Concept A as the first detailed feasibility and procurement comparison, while safeguarding Concept B's metropolitan corridors in the same study. This is a decision on **study and corridor protection**, not physical selection or a request for construction debt. Amanat Baghdad/National Investment Commission should compare both against one surveyed origin-destination model, corridor and land records, airport access agreement, geotechnical investigation and funding plan. A prior winning consortium was announced for the 2024 DBOMFT opportunity. The legal/contractual position must be confirmed before this proposal can be offered as an alternative, supplement or design input. The [Iraqi Ministry of Planning's 10 November 2026 metro workshop](https://mop.gov.iq/archives/40342) explicitly discussed revising routes and undertaking further feasibility study.

### Why revise the earlier map

The earlier generated Baghdad outline has 14 lines, **513.34 source-declared route-km** in `city.json`; its dossier's **494.43 costed km** uses a different route measure. That discrepancy must be reconciled before financing. It has 533 assumed six-car trainsets. Five short lines (10–14) account for 34.36 km and 46 trainsets. They are not all duplicates: several stops are over 1 km from long-line stations. Concept A does not import them as independent services. Concept B **retains them** until their access can be carried by through routes; no fleet or cost saving is booked. The existing station is about **3.04 km straight line** from the mapped airport passenger terminal and about **2.15 km** from the Jadriya place marker. A Sadr City centre marker is 645 m from an existing station, but the district-wide coverage is limited; calling all of Sadr City unserved would be inaccurate.

The available local layers already include WorldPop 2026, GHSL, OpenStreetMap walking streets and a walking graph, WorldCover/OSM water, and shallow soil samples. They improve screening, but do not establish ridership, an accessible walking path, flood levels, utility conflicts or foundation capacity. In a 1 km radial WorldPop screen the current stations cover about 2.58 million of 8.60 million modelled residents (30.0%). Three Sadr anchors add about 445,500 modelled residents within the two OSM Al-Sadr district polygons; two Jadriya anchors add about 49,800 within a 2 km study circle. These are **overlap-adjusted, straight-line population screens**, not passenger forecasts or approved station sites. The [mapping provenance and limits](../../../cities/catalogue/west-asia/Iraq/Baghdad/site-context.json) remain attached to the candidate.

## Concept A | 148.2 km first programme

The seven named corridors and nominal lengths are those in the [official National Investment Commission RFI](https://investpromo.gov.iq/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/3.-RFI-English-%D9%86%D8%A8%D8%B0%D8%A9-%D8%B9%D9%86-%D9%85%D8%AA%D8%B1%D9%88-%D8%A8%D8%BA%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%AF-E-20.2.2024.pdf): Green 19 km, Red 27.7 km, Blue 22 km, Purple 14.5 km, Yellow 30 km, White 23 km and Airport 12 km. The RFI names 64 stations, four depots and two control centres. Its 15% underground, 10% at-grade and 75% elevated proportions are planning assumptions, not surveyed civil quantities. The RFI says exact distances and station numbers require feasibility and owner approval.

The [official Arabic RFI](https://investpromo.gov.iq/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Baghdad-PDF.pdf) names `ساحة الجادرية` (Jadriya Square) on Yellow. The English transliteration and exact square location require owner confirmation. Fifteen named waypoints in the GIS register remain without a reliable coordinate; they are retained as names with `null` geometry rather than guessed positions.

The Airport route must terminate at an accessible **passenger-terminal entrance**; the terminal point currently used is [OSM way 28647537](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/28647537), not a surveyed station position. The Yellow/Jadriya and Blue/Purple/Sadr access anchors are shown as requirements for fit testing, with coordinates and unresolved RFI names in [the waypoint register](rfi-waypoints.json). The schematic GeoJSON links place centroids. It is **not an engineering alignment** and must never be measured to replace the RFI's nominal route length. Airport security/property, Tigris crossings, grades, utilities, interchange footprints, station spacing and accessible entrances need owner and site verification.

### Capacity must be redesigned against demand

The current development vehicle seats 44 people plus two wheelchair places per car, six cars per train: **276 normal places**, with no planned standing. Seven lines at the RFI's four-minute headway, two directions and 18 hours would offer at most **1,043,280 places/day** even if every train were full throughout the day. The RFI's **3.25 million passengers/day** is an unverified forecast and cannot be served by this reference formation under those assumptions. This is a necessary-bound failure, not a timetable optimisation detail. A surveyed demand distribution must choose train size, standing policy, platforms, headway, fleet, traction, braking, power and civil loads together. The reference's passive wheel bogies and station-powered concept remain the first comparison; powered-wheel and other research options need separate complete installed mass, safety, recovery and whole-life evaluations.

## Concept B | 500–800 km 2035 metropolitan programme

Concept B is a **strategic network scale**, not a claim that the present 14-line generated outline is ready to construct. Its 513.34 km source-declared map plus the straight-line 3.11 km extension of line 5 to the airport terminal and six 89.21 km schematic infill corridors form a **605.65 km minimum screen**. The [candidate delta register](concept-b-2035-delta.json) marks three Sadr stop/access anchors on lines 9/10, two Jadriya anchors near lines 1/2 and the airport terminal. The [infill evaluation](concept-b-infill-evaluation.json) proposes 61 further stop anchors. These are **not approved stations**. All five short lines remain in the mapped programme until a through-running replacement passes an origin-destination, walking-access and transfer test. Programme stages must preserve an operating network at each opening, with depots, grid supply, fleet, rescue and control sized stage by stage.

| Infill corridor | Schematic length | Stop anchors | Purpose and interface to resolve |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| B-I01 Sadr east-west | 12.10 km | 9 | Cross-district access and transfers; street fit and station safety. |
| B-I02 Sadr north-south | 10.07 km | 7 | Connect northern and southern Sadr catchments. |
| B-I03 east inner arc | 16.00 km | 11 | Connect east-bank employment and interchange catchments. |
| B-I04 Karkh north-south | 21.60 km | 14 | Provide a long western through corridor and transfers. |
| B-I05 southwest cross-city | 12.74 km | 9 | Link southwestern districts; Tigris crossing study required. |
| B-I06 northern cross-city | 16.71 km | 11 | Tie northern corridors; Tigris crossing study required. |

The six corridors add about **1.51 million** modelled residents to the corrected candidate's overlap-adjusted **1 km radial** screen: 3.09 million becomes **4.59 million of 8.60 million** in the 2026 raster. A stricter, still hypothetical OSM walking-graph screen reaches **2.13 million** after filtering tagged steps/private roads and other unsuitable links, versus **0.93 million** for the original map. The [separate walking evaluation](concept-b-walking-screen.json) includes its filter and source hashes. It still cannot establish safe, step-free, accessible access: footways, crossings, kerbs, gates, slopes and legal entrances need field checks. Neither population screen is ridership or a 2035 forecast. The network may require more infill/feeder service to match the ambition of a major world-city metro.

The **12 million-plus 2035 metropolitan population** is a capacity stress scenario requested for this concept, **not an adopted forecast**. It needs an agreed geographic boundary and reconciliation with the Iraqi census, local WorldPop raster and the [UN World Urbanization Prospects 2025](https://population.un.org/wup/) definition. A different boundary can give a materially different population. The 500–800 km ambition and 2035 delivery require a multi-front construction and financing programme; the yearly opening rate, land release, utility diversion, supply chain and operating subsidy must be demonstrated before committing to that date.

## Engineering, construction and local industry

Both concepts use the same safety, accessibility and passenger-protection obligations. An open, repairable, locally manufactured station-powered wheel-supported train is the first physical hypothesis. No installed propulsion has yet passed route-grade, stopping, thermal, current, recovery and emergency requirements for these Baghdad routes. The next engineering package must compare station launch, powered-wheel and other admissible concepts at equal passenger demand, formation and stop pattern; size the complete vehicle including doors, HVAC, batteries, braking, protection, wiring, joints and maintainability; and test one standard viaduct span, pier, foundation and erection method against Baghdad ground and utility conditions. A 1.5–2 km physical demonstrator should validate propulsion, stopping, maintenance access, vibration, interfaces and construction logistics before series procurement.

The manufacturing plan should specify Iraqi fabrication, assembly, inspection and repair hours by package; imported materials and machinery; tooling; approved drawings; QA records; spares; and origin value by cost rather than part count. Public manufacturing tables should show **counted vehicle components** and **equipment/fitout allowance awaiting detailed placement**. The latter is a positive mass and cost provision and must remain in the technical ledger until allocated.

## Cost and finance

Each concept has **one** base funding figure in [the cost and financing note](COST-AND-FINANCE.md). These are planning budgets, not fixed bids or loan approvals. Their internal quantity/rate uncertainties remain visible in the engineering evidence, without low/high price columns in this external proposal. The older 494 km cost-measured/14-line US$8.35bn Class C outline is not transferred to either concept because its geometry, tunnel content, fleet and station scope differ. Fourteen installed scopes, risk contingency, local/imported origin, annual operating costs and 30-year life-cycle obligations must be measured against each chosen candidate before a credit application.

UKEF finance is a potential tranche for an eligible UK export contract, not a source for the entire metro. The buyer contribution, UK content, sovereign/municipal repayment support, security, lender diligence, environmental and social review, land acquisition, procurement authority and foreign-exchange risk require named counterparties and agreements. The [UKEF Buyer Credit](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/buyer-credit-facility) and [Iraq country cover](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/country-cover-policy-and-indicators) are eligibility/context, not commitments. The 2024 RFI's DBOMFT concession and announced consortium rights must be resolved with Amanat and NIC first.

## Proposed stage gates

1. **Mandate and existing rights:** Amanat/NIC confirm sponsor, concession position, ownership and data access; establish both candidate boundaries and the population geography.
2. **Measured corridor and demand case:** origin-destination survey, accessible network walks, airport terminal agreement, geotechnics, utilities, river/flood and depot sites; choose line and station geometry by passengers and whole-life cost.
3. **Equal-service engineering:** train/propulsion/civil alternatives, a 1.5–2 km demonstrator, buildable assembly, safety case, quantities and independent verification.
4. **Financing case:** package-level Iraqi/UK origin and supplier quotations, one costed base, staged annual cash flow, fare/subsidy decision, ESHR and land plans, bank/UKEF term discussions and independent technical adviser.
5. **Construction and opening:** let and build only the stages that satisfy the above gates, with accessible and financially supported operation at each stage.

No physical design, ridership forecast, route, price or financing has been approved by Amanat Baghdad, NIC, a bank or UKEF. This document is a concrete proposal for the decisions and investigations needed to make that approval possible.
