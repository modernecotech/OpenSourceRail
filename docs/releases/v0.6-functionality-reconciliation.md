# v0.6 functionality reconciliation against v0.4 and v0.5

This audit compares the v0.6 working tree at `1ebfaa56b136fe61a68c6062f5d4c72373b77688` with the v0.4.0 release (`205e8dc158c83fcdc2dca9ceb8bad0a7e8b3f61e`) and the v0.5.1 release (`2617b04e722ca24b7ad9120f94610fe869f27053`). The v0.5.2 development record is also used for workflows added after that tag. A retained code path or historical packet is not counted as a current, integrated v0.6 capability.

## v0.6 cleanup and restored entry points

- `./osr design --help` and `./osr plan --help` now show command guidance instead of falling through to a status/error record.
- The planning editor’s engineering link now opens the v0.6 Workbench. Historical headway and 1,500 m spacing screens are labelled as comparisons from the retained city packet, not current v0.6 requirements.
- The planning compiler emits only location, stop, dwell, headway and OD proposals, with their source hashes and current candidate identities. It does not copy v0.5 vehicles, powered guideways, fleet, costs, quantities, operating outcomes or release states.
- `./osr plan verify DIR` checks the planning package, edit bundle, retained geography inputs, selected v0.6 candidate and source hashes before review.
- The retained Trip Workbench is reconnected to its existing bounded loopback research service. Its magnetic vehicle, loads and 180-second service comparison remain separate from the selected v0.6 physical candidate and service target.
- Current engineering, movement-authority, procurement and physical-release dispositions remain false. The current selected reference and city-performance hold are unchanged.

## Work still required to restore the wider platform

The current sourcing snapshot contains 100 immutable offline observations: 41 source records, 16 product records, five published catalogue offers, two FX observations and 36 benchmarks, including a separate reported Kurdistan Region cement market observation. Its federated purchasing roll-up covers 31,989 physical scopes in 7,042 requirement groups: 2,269 source-declared manufacturing groups retain 27,216 located occurrences and 4,773 unlocated accounts remain separate. The balanced evaluation covered 137 groups, with 6,905 pending, zero compatible priced matches and nine disabled RFQ drafts. All evaluated purchase quantities remain unknown. This improves sourcing scope and domestic cost reference data; it does not relax identity, scope, quantity or specification matching.

A candidate-bound material takeoff now partitions all 27,216 located occurrences across those 2,269 manufacturing groups and preserves the 4,773 unlocated accounts separately. It derives 4,780.58 t of gross model stock, including 4,512.02 t / 1,804.81 m³ of nominal concrete stock. Copper and aluminium totals receive dated World Bank raw-commodity comparisons only; those values are not component prices or cost bounds. The takeoff is not an installed mass account and cannot be costed as a complete procurement BOM because order units, process hours, yields and compatible finished-product offers remain absent.

The practical-launch comparison now applies a common 1.5 km / 2 km route matrix to 12 propulsion concepts, for 288 analytical required-demand and necessary-bound cases under the same vehicle, loading, speed, acceleration, grade, dwell and headway assumptions. Its 2 km 144-case view remains available for older consumers. The fixed-grip one-train-per-pitch cable bound falls short of 8,280 pphpd at both distances (about 6,551 at 1.5 km and 5,624 at 2 km). Available propulsion maps, electrical/mechanical losses, cooling, independent stopping and recovery, peak grid demand, complete installed quantities and protected fleet schedules remain open, so this is not a force-driven journey or preferred propulsion selection. The 900 m shared winding and five 20 m separated-bank study retain separate geometries and BOM scopes.

An illustrative Iraqi rebar cut/bend rate now computes source-priced material plus declared labour hours and the requested 20% unallocated uplift. At 4, 8 and 16 skilled-worker hours per input tonne, the screens are IQD 1,231,800/t, IQD 1,276,800/t and IQD 1,366,800/t. The labour cycles are assumptions, not measured factory routes, and no railway takeoff uses the figures yet. The current [sourcing dossier](../design-engine/sourcing/README.md) records the source dates, scope, local supplier leads, packet identities and open costs.

The purchasing-group roll-up is now present, but it still needs candidate-specific order units, net/gross quantities, yields, operations and supplier compatibility for lamination stacks, copper winding/testing, bogie assemblies, standard fastener SKUs, GFRP panels/tooling, and precast beams with reinforcement, labour and transport. Those units must then flow through one canonical candidate bundle to Iraqi installed rates, finite-resource factory/civil schedules, monthly cash requirements, ERP drafts, IFC properties and field records. The cost/weight sensitivity controller is implemented, but no current BOM groups declare impact ranges, uncertainty bases, dependent decisions, alternatives or affected reruns, so it cannot yet prioritize by numeric decision value. Missing prices or equipment remain unknown. The 16-layer planning workflow provides a reviewed proposal handoff, but its historical route is not a substitute for site survey or operating evidence.
