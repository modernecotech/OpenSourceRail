# OpenSourceRail v0.5.2 development

This revision develops a fixed-zone ferrite-maglev operating and manufactured-system proposal on `main`. The published v0.5.1 release/tag remains preserved. This is development evidence, without manufacturing, operating or physical approval.

Fixed station LSMs launch and receive the train; selected longer legs add finite midway accelerator zones, while remaining guideway is unpowered. Independent onboard PM-biased support/guidance and a 30 kWh services battery remain necessary. Baghdad uses six 17 m bodies, four doors/car and a 14.2 t/car tare target. The new counted frame/hardpoint details and emergency-brake proposal consume existing growth reserve. Complete weighed equipment and qualified body strength remain open.

The primary planning strategy targets urban passenger stops at least 1.5 km apart and about 2 km on average,4–5km suburban stops and located midway acceleration. Junctions are passenger transfers between independent fixed lines. Close mandatory transfers remain declared spacing/access exceptions. The 1 km/88s/180s component benchmark is retained separately from longer-profile verification. The supplied 90 s/190 s station benchmark remains a separate comparison. City plans now locate proposed operating stops on measured mapped polylines, assign retained access records, and include tangent terminal-loop geometry and extra powered zones. Every line reports original versus operating counts, transfer/spacing conflicts, booster/rescue quantities, capacity targets, actual headway, fleet and held cases. Geographical fit, access, grades, magnetic curves and disturbed operation remain unqualified.

A retained 1 km moving six-car numerical benchmark executes actual Rust support allocation against six rigid bodies, repeated flexible beams and a longitudinal coupler chain. It retains failed candidates, independent numerical controls, refinement, force/work checks, gap traces and a calculated-motion video. Current primary geometry and stiffness derive from shared configuration; the original 25 m × 1.80 m box is preserved as a comparison. Ideal independently allocated forces, uniform mass/inertia hypotheses, linear beams and virtual sensing do not prove physical shared-field operation.

The manufactured design dossier supplies counted frame members and panel supports, hardpoint/lap/fastener candidates, coupler stiffness and strength screens, stator/bearing interfaces, prestress/handling alternatives and distributed emergency-brake/curve load paths. It maps all 100 supplied obligations and 179 applicable requirements to deliverables and missing evidence. Larger-footing candidates include explicit concrete, reinforcement and earthwork deltas. Native FreeCAD/STEP details reopen and have retained images and a visibility movie; untrimmed stock is not an interference-approved complete assembly.

The retained 1 km integrated campaign composes actual Rust reservation/occupancy ownership and station control over incoming trip, dwell and committed departure. Negative cases include receiver, sensing, drive, resistor, stale observation, phase and commitment faults. Independent friction-brake and station-winch proposals add force, energy, battery-reserve and civil-interface quantities. Real detectors, hardware protection, curve recovery and cross-controller handover remain open.

The retained benchmark uses240 kW/car input and90% assumed conversion. New longer operating cases evaluate their own pack/charger/dwell constraints instead of inheriting those figures. The authored loss-free 90 s benchmark still needs 1.20 MW/train for 45 s charging within 60 s dwell. Rating selection, charge taper, thermal duty and grid capability remain unqualified. City subtotals separately contain fleet, ordinary elevated civil work, terminal civil work, station electrical targets, a conditional larger-footing increment, located boosters and independent rescue kits. All are planning targets, without supplier quotations; complete installed cost is unknown.

New `corridor`, `booster-kit` and `booster-native` packets add finite-zone trajectories and Rust fault replay, material takeoffs, equivalent thermal duty, spatial reactions and independently priced recovery alternatives. The recovery comparison retains failing midpoint-only slow-winch cases, while counting larger batteries, faster drives and standalone rope/drum/portal/foundation/access arrangements. Real motor maps, block protection, route access, physical hardware and transfer geometry remain unqualified.

The new [Baghdad interchange-hubs packet](../design-engine/ferrite-maglev/interchange-hubs/README.md) investigates extended walking links on retained guideways and coordinated shared-site concepts with changed guideway chords. The default273 proposed stops and10 transfer-spacing conflicts remain unchanged; every alternative is unselected. Connector lengths, walking-speed sensitivities, partial electrical target credits and changed operating duties are explicit. Shared-site station tangents, curvature, levels, platforms, actual internal walks and costs remain undesigned. A100m compact walk is an assumption, and extended links are not labelled compact hubs. Access, flow, lift resilience, heat/shade, resting places, evacuation and complete connector cost remain additional requirements; no train-routing switches are introduced.

The source-bound `moving-train`, `system-campaign`, `manufactured-dossier` and `manufactured-native` packets join the station studies, native CAD, adaptive search and city workflow. The moving video shows calculated positions with declared amplification. Other videos show final-position geometry visibility, without collision-free erection or chute-deployment acceptance. Ring/block orientation references receive no railway force or mass-saving credit.

All design-completion, physical-validation, manufacturing-release and physical-release flags remain false. No requirement becomes accepted merely through ledger mapping, numerical screens, CAD export or publication integrity.

The operational comparison uses 2 km/140 s with 50 kWh/car, 400 kW/car charging and 60 s dwell, plus 5 km/375 s with 50 kWh/car, 600 kW/car and 96 s dwell. Both have 350 m station zones; the suburban booster covers the union of all load cases. The 300 s suburban AW3 case remains a faster comparison with AW0/AW2 failures. Exclusive whole-leg reservations still exceed the 180 s target, so no retained-capacity or sectioned-protection claim is made.
