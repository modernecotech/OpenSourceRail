# Make/Buy and Localization Plan

This plan implements RFC 0021's principle: own the interfaces and evidence,
localize ordinary fabrication early, and localize safety-critical catalogue
parts only after process capability and qualification are demonstrated.

| System | First production | Early local work | Later localization gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bogies | Buy complete qualified bogies for the prototype and approximately first 20–30 trainsets | Inspection, wheel reprofiling, brake service, harnesses, final vehicle marriage | Local frame welding/final assembly after stable demand, EN 15085-quality welding, dimensional capability, NDT, fatigue evidence, and supplier audit; continue buying qualified wheels, axles, bearings, suspension, brakes, motors and gearboxes |
| Doors | Buy drive, lock, sensing, emergency release and controller kits | Fabricate leaves, frames, rails, housings, thresholds, adapters and harness tails to the OSR cassette ICD | Localize drive parts only after lifecycle, obstruction, lock integrity, environmental and software-interface qualification |
| Articulation | Buy spherical bearing, yaw/pitch joint, anti-lift elements, elastomers and bellows | Fabricate steel adapter frames, drawbar support, floor bridge, covers, trays and service guides | Localize joints/bearings only after proof, fatigue, wear and fire evidence |
| Windows | Buy complete laminated solar-control rail/bus safety-glass cassettes | Local aperture frames, drains, adapter plates and installation | Local glazing only after optical, impact, fragmentation, thermal and adhesive-system qualification; acrylic/polycarbonate remains limited to noncritical covers/interiors |
| Seats | Buy or locally fabricate simple qualified longitudinal bench shells | Local steel/aluminium frames, mounts and replaceable trim | Release only with material fire/smoke/toxicity certificates and mount/static proof-load evidence |
| Battery | Buy qualified cells/modules, BMS sensing, contactors and fire-detection components | Local enclosure frames, vent ducts, cooling manifolds, mist pipework, harnesses and pack integration under supplier controls | Cell manufacture is out of scope until a separate industrial program exists; pack integration requires IEC 62928-based test evidence |
| Traction | Buy matched motor/controller sets | Local brackets, coolant manifolds, cables and connectorized harnesses | Motor/controller localization only after dynamometer, insulation, EMC, vibration and functional-safety qualification |
| Body | Local from first article where capability exists | Welded S355 datum skeleton, modular GFRP cassettes, sacrificial nose, interior panels and final assembly | Production release after weld procedure/NDT, composite fire/bond/insert, water ingress, tolerance and FEA gates |

The semi-permanent drawbar carries longitudinal train loads separately from
the passenger gangway. Door, glazing, HVAC, traction, battery and
articulation suppliers shall fit published supplier-neutral envelopes rather
than drive changes into the primary steel structure.

All localization decisions are serial-number traceable. A lower purchase
price never overrides missing material certificates, process qualification,
inspection records, software/configuration control, spares support, or the
deployment safety case.
