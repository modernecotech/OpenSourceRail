# Inter-car articulation - `light-metro-3car`

The `light-metro-3car` uses two semi-permanent articulated gangway
modules, one between Car A/B and one between Car B/C. This is not a
Jacobs-bogie train: every 17 m car still has two standard two-axle
bogies. The articulation module carries inter-car structural loads,
keeps the passenger passage sealed, and routes trainlines while allowing
the bodies to yaw, pitch, and roll independently on curves and vertical
profiles.

## Reference architecture

The detailed CAD envelope is generated by
[`inter_car_articulation()`](../../../mechanical-py/src/osr_mech/rolling_stock/systems.py).

| Subsystem | Design intent |
|---|---|
| Lower central joint | Maintenance-light radial spherical bearing at 720 mm above top-of-rail, with anti-lift keeper and vertical anti-climb shear keys |
| Semi-permanent drawbar | Forged or welded drawbar between underframe anchor castings; carries traction/brake buff and draft loads within the consist |
| Upper articulation | Twin upper roll-yaw-pitch links at the gangway roof line to stabilise body relative motion without carrying floor load |
| Passenger gangway | Double-wall corrugated bellows, bolted clamp frames, flexible side walls and ceiling, segmented anti-slip turntable floor |
| Service routing | Separated HV jumper, redundant TCN-E Ethernet jumpers, CAN-FD/safety-loop jumper, coolant hose loops, HVAC air sleeve, drain channels |
| Maintenance access | Removable turntable service hatch, quick-access bellows clamp bolts, drain traps, replaceable wear plates and bearing inspection covers |

Reference product families used for sizing are HÜBNER-class tram/metro
articulation and gangway systems and Schaeffler-class compact central
articulated pivots. HÜBNER's public material describes modular lower
and upper articulation systems, bellows, turntables, energy guidance,
and air-duct integration for urban rail vehicles; Schaeffler's central
pivot reference describes a lower central joint using a radial spherical
plain bearing with anti-lift function for turn, tilt, and pitch movement
under operating loads.

## Kinematic envelope

The CAD carries explicit framed clearance envelopes so reviewers can
see the motion allowance in PNG/FreeCAD views without hiding the joint.

| Motion | Design clearance | Basis |
|---|---:|---|
| Yaw | +/- 12 deg | Covers the RFC 0009 `standard-urban` 90 m minimum curve with margin |
| Pitch | +/- 6 deg | Covers vertical curves, lifting, and bogie height variation |
| Roll | +/- 5 deg | Covers cant, suspension roll, uneven loading, and maintenance lift cases |

The clearance envelope is owned by OSR. The supplier may use a different
internal link or bearing arrangement if it keeps the same carbody
interfaces, passage envelope, and motion envelope.

## Load path

| Load case | Primary path | Detail requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Traction and braking draft | Underframe anchor casting -> drawbar -> lower spherical bearing -> opposite anchor | Proof to consist draft/brake load from traction and emergency braking |
| Buff/compression | Anti-climb shear keys and drawbar washer stack | No override into passenger gangway trim |
| Vertical relative movement | Lower spherical joint + upper links | Anti-lift keeper must retain bodies during jacking/lifting cases |
| Yaw/curve movement | Lower spherical joint with upper roll-yaw-pitch links | No binding at +/- 12 deg yaw |
| Gangway passenger load | Segmented turntable floor into both adapter frames | 5 kN/m2 service load target before supplier-specific proof |
| Crash isolation | End crash energy remains at outer couplers; articulation protects inter-car passage and trainline integrity | No articulation-mounted crush can in v1 unless supplier requires it |

The primary steel at each car end must include an articulation adapter
frame, underframe anchor casting, upper clevis brackets, bellows clamp
frame nutplates, drain lands, and service hatch aperture. These are
fabricator-owned steel details; the gangway module remains a tendered
COTS/BID package.

## Trainline routing

The articulation separates high-energy, data, safety, coolant, and HVAC
paths so a single abrasion or water-ingress failure cannot remove all
services.

| Route | Location | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| HV traction jumper | Right-hand protected conduit | Shielded, orange, bonded at both carbody frames; HVIL loop routed adjacent |
| TCN-E redundant Ethernet | Upper right protected conduit | Two physically separated TSN Ethernet ports continue the consist ring |
| CAN-FD + emergency-brake safety loop | Mid-height right conduit | Hardwired emergency-brake loop remains electrically independent |
| Coolant flexible hose loops | Low left service bay | Drip tray below, quick-disconnect only in depot |
| HVAC air sleeve | Upper left duct | Flexible sleeve, condensate drainage, no external add-on duct |
| Drain channels | Both floor edges | Water traps accessible through gangway floor hatch |

## Maintenance and replacement

| Task | Interval target | Access |
|---|---|---|
| Visual bellows and clamp-frame inspection | Daily walkaround / depot entry | Exterior and interior gangway faces |
| Drain trap clean-out | Monthly or local dust/rain interval | Floor service hatch |
| Bearing/keeper inspection | 6-month A-check | Centre hatch and underframe access |
| Upper-link spherical eye inspection | 6-month A-check | Ceiling hatch / roofline service cover |
| Bellows replacement | 10-15 year overhaul planning interval | Clamp frames unbolt; module removed as a cassette |
| Full articulation module replacement | Heavy overhaul | Cars supported on jacks; drawbar unloaded; service jumpers disconnected |

## Procurement closure

The v1 BOM treats the complete gangway/articulation module as a BID
line and the trainline energy-guidance kit as a SOURCE/MAKE line. At v2
release, the supplier package must provide:

- carbody adapter frame drilling pattern and shim pack limits,
- lower central joint bearing rating, anti-lift proof, and inspection
  method,
- upper link rating, spherical eye replacement process, and rubber/metal
  fire-smoke evidence,
- bellows, turntable, and interior panel EN 45545 evidence,
- energy guidance bending-radius limits and harness retention method,
- water ingress, drain, and cleaning instructions,
- articulation motion test report at the OSR yaw/pitch/roll envelope.
