# Roof moulds, HVAC, solar and service fitout

The LM3 roof is a layered, serviceable assembly. Structural roof bows and
equipment rails (`LM3-BDY-P080`) carry loads. Clip-on roof skins
(`LM3-BDY-P133`) and local fairings (`LM3-ROOF-P030`/`P040`) provide weather,
aerodynamic and maintenance closeout only.

## Part and zone definition

| Item | Function | Must not carry |
|---|---|---|
| `LM3-BDY-P133` | 1 m roof skin and trim variants | HVAC, PV, lifting or walkway structural loads |
| `LM3-ROOF-P010` | bolted/gasketed HVAC curb, duct collar, condensate tray and drains | Loads outside the released HVAC interface |
| `LM3-ROOF-P020` | PV rails/pads, clamps, bonds and isolation labels | Unverified adhesive-only module retention |
| `LM3-ROOF-P030` | removable HVAC intake/exhaust skirt, curb fairing and access hatches | Curb, lifting or fall-protection loads |
| `LM3-ROOF-P040` | PV/gland plinths, antenna closeouts and walkway edges | Antenna/PV proof loads or worker load |
| `LM3-EXT-P040` | supplier roof HVAC unit | Carbody loads beyond frozen feet/curb |
| `LM3-EXT-P050` | supplier PV module and clamp kit | Roof-skin load; rails resolve into structure |
| `LM3-EXT-P070` | antennas, walkway pads, lifting covers and labels | Unreleased roof penetrations |
| `LM3-FIN-P020` | trial radiative coating on exposed fairings | Any structural, sealing, bonding or anti-slip role |

## Mould family

The common roof mould fixes crown, 1,000 mm pitch, side returns, clip lands and
dry-joint geometry. Replaceable inserts in `LM3-TOOL-ROOF-FAIRING-MOULD`
produce:

- plain roof skin;
- HVAC curb/fairing and removable skirt;
- PV rail/pad and junction/gland plinth;
- antenna/radio closeout;
- retained inspection or lifting cover;
- walkway edge and drain closeout.

Openings are CNC trimmed after cure from the common datum. No roof penetration
is hand-cut on the vehicle. Solid laminate surrounds fasteners and cut edges;
all trimmed edges are sealed, and every removable part has retained hardware.

## Arrangement and keep-outs

The released roof coordination drawing must freeze HVAC feet, supply/return
drops, condensate drains, PV rails/clamps, cable glands, resistor heat zone,
antennas, lightning/bonding network, lifting covers, walkways and fall-safe
access. It must also reserve hand/tool access and a removal path for each unit.

Radiative coating is allowed only on the remaining exposed roof/fairing area.
It is prohibited on PV active surfaces and clamps; HVAC intake, exhaust and
heat exchangers; antennas/sensors; walkways; lifting covers; earth bonds;
glands; drains; seals; safety labels; and all service or adhesive lands.

## Controlled assembly route

1. Survey roof bows and rails before corrosion-coating closeout.
2. Install and leak-test the HVAC curb, duct collar, tray and drains.
3. Fit PV rails/pads, bonds and cable-gland plinths; perform pull, pitch and
   continuity checks before modules obscure them.
4. Dry-fit plain/fairing roof mouldings, hatches, skirts and closeouts; check
   airflow, thermal and service envelopes.
5. Apply qualified exposed-roof finish with the controlled mask map.
6. Install HVAC, PV, antennas, resistor shields, walkways and covers.
7. Complete roof water, condensate-flow, bonding, PV isolation, antenna,
   airflow and service-removal tests.

The HVAC and PV product selections remain supplier freezes. These moulds and
interfaces let the local factory progress without pretending that a generic
box is the final certified unit.
