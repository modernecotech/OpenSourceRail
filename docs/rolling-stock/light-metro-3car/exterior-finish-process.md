# Simplified exterior finish: base protection, livery film and cool roof

The simplified process removes most masked multi-colour painting, not the
protective systems beneath it. The production sequence is:

1. qualified corrosion protection on steel;
2. white/light-neutral in-mould gelcoat or compatible base coating on GFRP;
3. pre-cut, replaceable graphic film for the green waist band, logos and local
   colour blocks;
4. a separately qualified high-reflectance roof coating on exposed roof
   fairings only.

## What film can and cannot replace

Film can replace decorative colour-band masking and repainting on smooth,
fully cured, accepted substrates. It cannot replace steel primer/topcoat,
cavity wax, edge sealing, composite gelcoat/base protection, fire barriers,
anti-slip surfaces, structural adhesive, weather seals, safety markings or
electrical insulation/bonding.

The reference procurement row is `LM3-FIN-P010`: a bay-numbered, pre-cut film
kit with controlled overlaps, edge treatment, datum marks and repair patches.
Use a current film system whose manufacturer explicitly supports rail-vehicle
graphics. 3M's current rail bulletin requires pre-installation inspection,
clean substrate preparation, application controls, quality inspection,
maintenance and removal procedures; its rail catalogue includes IJ180-class
film. That makes it a useful procurement anchor, not an automatic LM3 approval.

Primary references:

- [3M Application: Rail instruction bulletin](https://multimedia.3m.com/mws/media/2419613O/3m-instruction-bulletin-application-rail-ra.pdf)
- [3M Controltac Graphic Film 180mC product page](https://www.3m.com/3M/en_US/p/d/b00026338/)

The selected film, ink, overlaminate and edge system still require project fire,
adhesion, temperature, UV/weather, cleaning-chemical and removal evidence on
the actual cured GFRP/steel base finish.

## Film manufacturing and application route

| Seq. | Operation | Evidence |
|---:|---|---|
| 10 | Freeze colour artwork, module/bay seams, door/window/cowl breaks, logos and prohibited zones | Approved livery map and cut files |
| 20 | Complete and sign substrate inspection; verify full paint/gelcoat cure, repair defects and clean by selected supplier process | Per-car pre-installation record |
| 30 | Plot/cut and label left/right/bay/cowl pieces on `LM3-TOOL-FILM-TEMPLATE`; make an adhesion/removal coupon | Cut-kit map and accepted coupon |
| 40 | Dry-apply from controlled datum; squeegee air to open edges; keep knives off the vehicle finish | Installer and environment record |
| 50 | Finish seams/edges and post-heat only where the selected system requires it | Edge/overlap inspection map |
| 60 | Water/wash inspect and demonstrate local patch plus complete 1 m module removal | Appearance, ingress and repair record |

Film terminates before EPDM joints, drains, captive fasteners, glass edges,
lamp apertures, bonding points, safety labels and access lands. Prefer each
graphic piece to stay on one replaceable 1 m module so collision repair does
not require stripping an adjacent good panel.

## Calcium-carbonate radiative roof candidate

Purdue's 2020 open paper demonstrated a single-layer CaCO3-acrylic research
paint with 95.5% solar reflectance and 0.94 normal emissivity in the atmospheric
window. Those values are useful **initial screening targets**, not guaranteed
vehicle values. The paper does not establish rail fire compliance, adhesion to
the LM3 laminate, flexibility under vibration, erosion, depot-wash durability,
soiling behaviour, repairability or aged optical performance.

Primary research:

- [Li et al., Full Daytime Sub-Ambient Radiative Cooling in Commercial-like Paints](https://docs.lib.purdue.edu/coolingpubs/369/)
- [Purdue-hosted open paper PDF](https://engineering.purdue.edu/NANOENERGY/publications/Li_Cell%20Reports%20Physical%20Science_2020.pdf)

`LM3-FIN-P020` is therefore `concept`/trial-only. It cannot be promoted to a
trainset production finish until this gate is passed:

| Gate | Minimum evidence |
|---|---|
| Formulation | Controlled binder, CaCO3 particle-size distribution/concentration, additives, shelf life, mixing and application window |
| Rail material | Fire/smoke/toxicity and chemical compatibility accepted for the deployment case |
| Substrate | Adhesion, flexibility/thermal cycling, edge compatibility and repair on the actual GFRP base system |
| Exposure | UV, humidity, heat, rain, dust/sand, abrasion and depot-wash ageing |
| Optical | Measured solar reflectance and thermal/sky-window emittance when new and after ageing/soiling/cleaning |
| Operations | Glare/visibility, runoff, cleaning, damage inspection and local repair procedure |
| Vehicle trial | One-car A/B roof-zone temperature, HVAC energy, soiling and maintenance comparison through representative hot service |

The trial mask excludes PV surfaces/clamps, HVAC air and heat-exchanger paths,
antennas/sensors, walkways, lifting covers, earth bonds, glands, drains, seals,
labels and service lands. No HVAC-energy or passenger-temperature saving is
claimed until the one-car trial measures it.

## Factory simplification result

The body cell retains one small controlled coating capability for steel repair,
GFRP edge repair and the roof candidate. It no longer needs to mask and spray
the full green/silver livery scheme. White GFRP parts can leave the mould with
their qualified base surface, while numbered film kits move decorative work to
a clean, low-dust application bay and make one-metre collision repairs local.
