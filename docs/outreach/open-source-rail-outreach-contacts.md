# OpenSourceRail Outreach Contacts

Last checked: 2026-06-06.

This is a starter contact list for informing relevant organizations about
OpenSourceRail. It uses public role emails or official contact forms only.
Re-check each source before sending, send small personalized messages, and do
not add these contacts to a newsletter or bulk campaign without consent.

## Outreach Notes

- Lead with the public-good angle: open designs, transparent cost assumptions,
  repeatable city design tooling, parametric mechanical references, and an
  invitation for review or collaboration.
- Tailor the ask by audience: rail groups may care about standards and safety;
  open-source groups may care about licensing and collaboration workflow;
  city-mobility groups may care about affordable implementation paths.
- Use one clear call to action, such as "could you point this to the right
  working group?" or "would this be suitable for your newsletter/community?"
- Include repository link, a concise one-paragraph summary, and an opt-out line.
