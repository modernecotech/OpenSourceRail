# What completes v0.7

v0.7 is a development programme, not a released railway. Its software
goal is a complete and repeatable journey: edit one corridor, compare
service and costs, inspect the same candidate's vehicle and civil work,
follow a representative module into production and maintenance planning,
and receive a dossier that states both results and limits. A good release
would let a new user repeat that journey from a clean installation and
understand where each conclusion came from.

## Complete the installed candidate

The current Samawah comparison keeps the same vehicle reference on both
sides. The next step is to bind one detailed six-car candidate with its
door, service equipment, mass and inertia, running gear, propulsion,
brakes and civil interfaces. Mating dimensions and removal paths must
be resolved rather than filled with placeholder positions. A complete
installed mass needs actual equipment, joints, fasteners, wiring,
cooling and protection, with unknowns explicitly bounded. If the
manufacturing child differs physically from the selected reference, it
must receive its own service, structural and economic evaluations.

Propulsion needs a force and thermal duty that can support the proposed
journey. Braking and rescue must work independently under the relevant
faults and poor-adhesion cases. The civil choice needs rail support,
bearings, piers, foundations, moving loads and construction stability.
The crane must be capable at the actual installation radius. These are
not small finishing touches: they can change train mass, station power,
cost and the passenger timetable.

## Complete one controlled module

The door operator should have a mated geometry and a practical removal
route. Controlled drawings, tolerances, materials, process steps and
inspection limits should follow the same revision. The bill of materials
should identify bought and made parts and their origin. A full candidate
linked module should then pass through actual receipt or manufacture,
quality review, assembly, installation, fault or condition event,
replacement and handback in the business and supervision systems.
Current synthetic software custody tests are foundations for this route;
they are not its completion.

## Complete the city journey

The native GIS exchange needs a supported reader workflow from edit to
recalculation and refreshed project, with Samawah first and Baghdad as
the generalisation check. The dossier should give before-and-after maps,
service and fleet, quantities, energy and all fourteen cost scopes,
including unchanged results and explicit unknowns. Transferred rates
and unsurveyed conditions should be easy to see. A reader should be
able to tell whether a held result comes from an incompatible source,
an unsupported model or absent field evidence.

## Verify the software and name the physical work

The clean-install acceptance case should reproduce the guided journey,
check the downloaded report and source identities, and show that an
unchanged study is reused appropriately while a changed one receives a
new result. Independent review should challenge the candidate links,
numerical models and the completeness of the published claims. The
v0.7 release notes should describe available functions, previews and
holds in ordinary language.

Physical prototypes, supplier qualification, real measurements and the
approvals for manufacture and passenger operation remain their own
programme. A complete software journey will make those needs clearer
and more traceable. It will not transform a calculated railway into a
safe operating one by changing the colour of a dashboard.

## A reader's test of completeness

A new reader should be able to choose the supported Samawah example,
understand the proposed edit, inspect the before and after results and
find the source of a cost or fleet change. They should be able to see
which result is held because of absent site evidence and which component
still needs a drawing or physical test. They should then open the door
module and follow its proposed making and replacement route. If an
unrelated result is reused, the interface should explain why. If a
physical candidate changes, the interface should refuse a stale result.

The same sequence, adapted to one Baghdad corridor, is a stronger test
than a list of isolated features. It exercises planning, service,
engineering, production and review as one product. It also gives the
team a clear stopping condition for a software release: a supported
journey that another user can repeat, with open physical work stated
honestly. The subsequent prototype programme can then use that traceable
project as a starting point for measurements and approved decisions.
