## Terms used in this book

**Candidate** means one physical arrangement of the railway and its relevant
interfaces. **Evaluation** means a calculation or test under declared
conditions. **Selection** means a decision to use one candidate for a stated
purpose. **Source-bound** means that a result identifies the inputs and
method to which it applies. **Whole-life cost** includes construction,
operation, maintenance, renewal and end-of-life work under an explicit
period and discounting assumption. **Installed mass** includes the equipment,
mounts, wiring, cooling, protection and other items actually needed on the
vehicle, beyond a convenient stock subtotal. **Physical acceptance** would
require appropriate measurements, approved limits and independent review;
it is not conferred by a software demonstration.

Images marked *concept* communicate intended form or use. Engineering model
views illustrate declared geometry and methods, but are only evidence for
their exact model and checks. No image in this book is an approved
manufacturing drawing, surveyed railway route or operating permission.

Contributions and independent review are welcome when they preserve the
provenance and evidence boundaries of the design.
