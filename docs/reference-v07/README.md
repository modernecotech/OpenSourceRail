# v0.7 reference railway concept

This is the **single 30 km engineering and procurement planning configuration** for v0.7: [candidate specification](concept.toml), [representative vehicle](vehicle/README.md), [10 m span and station interface](civil/README.md) and [one cost and delivery ledger](cost/README.md). The vehicle and civil studies were produced under separate research identities. Their joined configuration here has its own explicit planning ID; it has no claimed native same-candidate evaluation or physical acceptance.

| Output | Planning result |
|---|---:|
| Service | Two tracks, twelve stations, 51 six-car trains including reserve; 8,280 offered normal places/hour/direction at the 120 s control headway |
| Train | Six 17 m cars, 276 normal places, 24 motorised axles, 1.5 kV DC overhead and independent rail-brake concept |
| Structure | Joint-free 10 m two-track precast beam family, 6,000 beam placements and 6,002 support positions over the synthetic 30 km |
| Installed capital | **US$1.0235bn** in 2026 dollars, **US$34.12m/route-km** |
| Whole-life present cost | **US$1.6659bn** for 30 years at 4% real |
| Delivery | **43 months** after an approved route and funding decision |

The 30 km case is a synthetic, fully elevated pricing control. Baghdad has a [separate 700-place train and costed network concept](../../cities/Baghdad/COST-AND-DELIVERY.md); it does not inherit the 276-place train's equipment rating, stations or route price. Samawah remains a separate city case. The v0.6 passive-wheel vehicle remains the historical selected reference and is not silently replaced by this planning configuration.

The vehicle package provides its layout, component counts, mass allowance, traction and braking requests, assembly order and repair access. The civil package provides a dimensioned span/station interface drawing, material and support quantities, erection order and a 2 km construction module. The cost ledger connects the same planning quantities to fourteen scope amounts, operation, renewals and programme. These are suitable to request design development and comparable supplier quotations. They are not fabrication drawings, site foundations, train-control approval or authorization for passenger operation.
