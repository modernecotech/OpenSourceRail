## Assembly datums and fit

Use metres and right-handed rigid transforms `T(parent ← child) = translation(x,y,z) · rotation`, with vehicle x forward, y toward the door side, z above rail head. On the straight reference, the quoted bogie and door rotations are the 3 × 3 identity; the opposite train orientation uses a 180° rotation about z at the track frame. The nominal car envelope is 17.0 × 2.65 m. Bogie frame origins are `T(car ← bogie±) = translation(±6.4,0,0)`; four axle centres/car are at x = ±5.35 and ±7.45 m. Each powered cartridge has a 0.64 × 0.52 × 0.52 m nominal bounding box at its axle. Door-opening frame origins are `translation(−4.5,+1.325,1.0)` and `translation(+4.5,+1.325,1.0)` for the two one-sided 1.4 m clear openings. These are placement proposals; the published door child has **no accepted installation datum or part transforms**. A 0.72 m proposed slide and 0.15 m tool radius require at least 0.87 m travel-plus-tool clearance beyond each moving leaf position; the 2.9 m guide path needs a minimum 3.2 m tool-access length if tools approach both ends. Sweep the entire opening/closing, adjustment and outward removal path, including seals, handholds, platform edge and vehicle sway.

For a beam, use a straight-track frame with x along its 10 m centreline, y across the 2 m beam width, z above the bearing plane. The 1.4 m deep independent box has four bearing positions, with exact seats, anchorage and rotations to be fixed by the structural designer. One beam is placed on one track; a two-track 10 m bay needs two beams and eight bearings. Surveyed rail head, track centres, foundation and station interface datums override conceptual site coordinates. Crane hooks, shipping saddles, stability bracing and bearing installation require full swept load envelopes, not final resting boxes.

For the power site, define `T(station ← module)` from surveyed grid-room floor, with electrical segregation and service access controlled by the utility and supplier. No module footprint, converter mass, cable bend radius, heat rejection or removal sweep is currently fixed. Hold procurement drawings until these are returned. The station platform template is 125 × 8 m; its passenger, maintenance and evacuation clearances remain separate from the electrical room.

## Counted interfaces and build sequence

The car has two bogies, four powered axle cartridges, two inverter/cooling racks, one roof collector, one roof HV protection set, two independent rail-contact brake modules and the service battery separate from traction power. Count at least four axle couplings, four torque arms, eight motor bushes, 32 motor bolts, 16 rack bolts, eight collector bolts, eight isolation bolts, two bogie earth loops, four HV loops and four coolant supply/return hoses **per car**. The two brake modules add four shoes, four actuators, four guide slides, eight end stops, eight retention pins and 16 shoe bolts. Supplier bearings, seals, hose fittings, connectors, sensors, guards and protected brake energy remain open line items, never zero quantities or zero cost. The nominal motor has only 52 mm lateral and 106 mm vertical static clearance. Even the proposed ±50 mm translation allowance reduces those to **2 mm lateral and 56 mm vertical** before yaw, roll, heave, wheel wear, curve/turnout motion or tool clearance. Those modes and a supported downward brake removal over a pit must be swept before fabrication release; the 2 mm necessary bound does not establish fit.

The research door child counts 74 part types and 694 pieces/opening: 97 local-fabricate, 568 standard purchased and 29 qualified purchased pieces. The opening requires a header/body load path, 2.9 m routed guide extent, 5.8 m belt loop, pulley and belt tension, motor/reducer attachment, two leaf motion paths, limit stops, sensors, protected wiring and retained access panels. The source handoff lists seven explicit interface groups and has neither accepted mounting datums nor approved inspection limits. Quote the whole removable module and prove its finite motion and replacement path; do not use a static closed-door CAD view as the fit test.

Fabricate and fixture the carbody, bogie and roof hardpoints; build the axle/motor torque path; support and fit brake modules; fit cooling racks and protected flexible runs; install roof equipment; fit the doors, HVAC, interior and service battery; marry bogies; weigh and measure axle loads; then perform isolated low-speed, door, brake, electrical and thermal tests. The **720 direct labour-hour/car** allowance splits 260 body, 120 bogie/drive, 80 door/interior, 80 HVAC/auxiliary, 100 wiring/cooling and 80 final/test. It excludes component manufacture, engineering, rework, supplier lead time and physical qualification. Ten technicians on one eight-hour shift would consume nine direct assembly days if all inputs are ready.

Cast one beam in a measured reusable mould; record batch, cover, reinforcement and bearing-seat geometry; cure, inspect, weigh, support on transport saddles, lift, place and restrain; then fix rail, drainage and protected access. A selected 10 m beam has 11.3376 m³ counted concrete, 3.1126 t counted steel, 29.964382 t bare mass and 37.4554775 t provisional hook load. These are digital design quantities, not a signed lift. A 2 km two-track module requires 400 beams and 1,600 bearings; at four factory beams/day and eight site lifts/day, the direct production/lift minima are 100 and 50 working days, subject to curing, foundations, access and parallel crews.

## Price and local production basis

The cost [basis](../cost/basis.csv) supplies **one** 2026 USD rate per integrated scope: US$5.9m per six-car powered set, US$14,240 per selected 13-strand precast beam with bearings/detail, US$8,000 per ordinary pier/footing position, US$5,000 per beam haul/lift, US$3m per grid/rectifier site, US$1.25m per station auxiliary feed and US$8m per complete elevated station. The extra US$240/beam is a planning allowance for 79.96 kg additional strand at about US$3/kg installed. US$5.9m/set corresponds to US$983,333/car for budgeting only; this is not a supplier quote or a sum of approved child prices. The separate research underframe direct allowance of US$11,944.48 is a **partial materials control** included within the vehicle envelope. The door child cost is included within the vehicle envelope. No missing equipment is priced at zero.

Modelled local-work fractions are 35% for powered rolling stock, 75% for beam/bearing detail, 80% for ordinary foundations, 85% for haul/lifts, 35% for the grid/rectifier package and 40% for station auxiliary feed. They are procurement targets for quoting and training, **not measured Iraqi value added**. Local shops can quote body/beam fabrication, enclosures, harnesses, brackets, lifting/erection and repair; motors, power semiconductors, protection, qualified bearings and door safety devices remain buy-qualified until capability and QA are demonstrated. Each RFQ must report material origin, process hours, tooling, inspection, delivery and replacement cost as well as a single installed unit price.

## QA, ERPNext and maintenance handoff

The [lifecycle draft](lifecycle-draft.json) is an import mapping and inspection plan. Its records are disabled drafts with no ERPNext site transaction, issued order, goods receipt, recorded measurement or installation. An authorised production team can create each Item and revision, approve BOM and Routing, issue RFQ and purchase orders, record supplier certs and receiving inspections, release work orders, record serial/lot installation, then open defects and replacements against that exact serial. The existing source [underframe ERPNext draft](../../design-engine/v07-representative-car/erp-draft.json) is also disabled and unposted.

Mandatory inspection points are: material and supplier identity; body, bogie, door, beam and station datums; joining/torque and bearing retention; full door/brake/bogie motion and tool/removal sweep; car and axle weighing; concrete strength and lift plan; insulation, earth, isolation and cooling leak checks; emergency stop, power-loss brake, rescue and thermal duty. Enter measured values, calibrated instrument IDs, approved limits, inspector identity and disposition before a receipt can pass. A failed inspection creates a nonconformance and rework/replace decision; it never changes the source candidate ID silently. Vehicle repair isolates overhead, traction DC, service battery, brake energy and coolant, supports the affected module, uses the checked removal path, records replaced serials and repeats retention, insulation, leakage and functional checks before return.

The completion condition for this package is a reproducible **quotation and prototype planning dossier** with quantities, responsible make/buy scope, route, budget, and identified drawings/tests for every safety-critical interface. A manufacturing release needs weighed installed equipment, supplier drawings, verified sweeps, approved structural and electrical limits, physical tests and independent sign-off. No such release is claimed here.
