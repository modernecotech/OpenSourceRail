City and revision

Geographic layers

Current city map layers are source-verified context for route review. Routes, stops, buildings, roads, water and places are not surveyed alignments, approved station sites or verified right-of-way.

Station and service proposal

Stop additions, moves and retirements, headway, dwell, stop spacing and junctions are proposal inputs only. Revise OD rows before retiring a station referenced by demand. These edits do not establish v0.6 capacity, protected headway, access or operating feasibility. Straightening redraws the proposed alignment and stations; land, utilities, crossings and passenger access must be recalculated.

Origin–destination revision

JSON rows use origin and destination stop IDs, trips_per_day and basis (synthetic or observed-unaccepted). Observations need provenance and independent review.

Advisory planning comparisons

    Recalculate the priced city

    Edits retain their exact base and source identities. Recalculation updates the mapped service, fleet, all cost scopes and delivery drafts. Capacity or access failures stay visible and priced.

    Replace route geometry with explicit station anchors

    Coordinates use longitude/latitude. Station chainages refer to the new route. No station is silently rescaled or moved.

    Exported edits bind the exact indexed city-route and station revision. Copy the downloaded bundle into the repository; the compiler rejects stale bases and conflicting stop order.
