GIS layers

Unavailable inputs are disabled. Population is aggregated to1km cells; sampled streets and soil points are labelled. Displayed access radii are illustrative.

Station and service proposal

Stop additions, moves and retirements, headway, dwell, stop spacing and junctions are proposal inputs only. Revise OD rows before retiring a station referenced by demand. These edits do not establish v0.6 capacity, protected headway, access or operating feasibility. Straightening redraws the proposed alignment and stations; land, utilities, crossings and passenger access must be recalculated.

Origin–destination revision

JSON rows use origin and destination stop IDs, trips_per_day and basis (synthetic or observed-unaccepted). Observations need provenance and independent review.

    Exported edits bind the exact base revision. Copy the downloaded bundle into the repository; the compiler rejects stale bases and conflicting station order. Use ./osr plan compile edits.json --output build/my-plan, then ./osr plan verify build/my-plan, to produce and verify an immutable, selected-candidate-bound planning handoff. It contains no v0.6 route-performance result, procurement quantity, price, construction schedule or release evidence.
