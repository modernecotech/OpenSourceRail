This is a comfort and service proposal.

The lower passenger capacity is explicit. No stable hover, complete installed mass, hot-weather comfort or safe shorter headway has been demonstrated. All permanent magnets remain onboard; the ordinary guideway stays concrete with passive steel facings.

Weight is one part of the suspension gap

At a fixed gap the permanent magnets can also pull too strongly for a lighter load. Active vertical stabilization, lateral control, converged 3D current maps and fault response are still required.

Lightweight assemblies

Seat material resolves part of the retained 800 kg interior budget; it does not erase other fitout. The layout’s thin-seat stock fails the separate load fixture. A thicker sandwich bench is the next experiment: 11.47 kg per pair under the declared preliminary load. The initial doors fail their deflection fixture. Deeper 60/80 mm-core revisions pass that fixture with more mass and unknown installation fit; removed apertures receive counted infill.

Plastic windows include retention and mullions: thin sheets may leave the plate model’s validity domain, while thick sheets can weigh more than glass.

Floor, roof and aluminium-frame options remain component comparisons. The preliminary floor option saves about 170 kg of stock. The passing revised roof saves only about 23 kg after counting eight extra crossrails and their joints, with a new 162 mm depth hypothesis. These savings are held outside the vehicle total until a matching equipped structure is evaluated. Actual 50°C HVAC capacity, glazing solar properties, emergency window release and one-sided-platform compatibility remain unknown.
