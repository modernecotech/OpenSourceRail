OpenSourceRailCurrent development design

Simple infrastructure. Comfortable, frequent trains.

A simpler wheel-supported train, launched at stations.

Version 0.6 uses six cars with four-abreast seating and wheelchair spaces. Passive wheel bogies run on conventional rails carried by concrete. Station linear motors launch and receive the train through passive aluminium fins. Services batteries power HVAC, doors and essential equipment.

Sourced reference-cell explorer · start with ./osr-cell workbench --state build/reference-cell. Five research vehicles, part-level mass/cost, evidence and manufacturing views.

Continuing numerical engineering · start with ./osr design progress serve --state build/continuing-cell. Inspect conditional launch trajectories, station supports, manufacturing blanks and unresolved physical tests. Research results do not release a railway.

Open city planning · edit current candidate-bound geography and service proposals and compile a candidate-bound proposal with ./osr plan compile. The handoff does not include route performance, prices or release evidence.
