# Roadmap

OpenSourceRail v0.5.2 development coordinates fixed station/booster-zone ferrite
maglev across mechanical, civil, software, manufacturing and city planning. Station
LSMs launch and receive the train; longer suburban legs compare located midpoint
accelerators. Independent onboard support and passenger services remain active
during the unpowered coast. Junction stations transfer passengers between fixed
lines. Urban spacing targets 1.5 km minimum and approximately 2 km average, with
explicitly classified suburban 4–5 km alternatives. [Implemented work](../engineering/ferrite_maglev/README.md)
and the [current report](design-engine/ferrite-maglev/reference/index.html) identify
the tested digital scope. Accepted engineering and physical releases remain zero.

## Primary maglev closure queue

The [coordinated design loop](../engineering/design_search/README.md) implements
the first carrier/guidebeam campaign. Its development packages are tracked below;
implemented software does not close physical requirements.

| Package | Implemented in this iteration | Remaining completion gate |
|---|---|---|
| Dependable evaluators | Local moving-load prestress envelopes; procurement before first consumption; explicit beam/span selection; regression cases | Material, joint, supplier and site correlation |
| Common contracts | Physical, evaluation and selection identities; complete/unknown inventory; source-bound registry; rigid transforms and conservative stock sweeps | More physical families and complete installed inventories |
| Domain adapters | Current nonlinear magnetic plant and actual native structural/civil chain | Independent spatial field benchmarks, installed joint models, repeated electrothermal duty |
| Campaign runner | Bounded tasks/dependencies, numerical workers, resource limits, restart, immutable evidence and failure-directed revisions | Broader capabilities and measured input ingestion |
| Multi-option comparison | Three carrier/beam families, bounded revisions, Pareto next experiment and separately gated integrated ranking | A candidate surviving simultaneous support, guidance, thermal, mass and operation requirements |
| Research twin | Selected native CAD/STEP, BOM, IFC/IDS/BCF, assembly scope and material-constrained draft delivery | Complete installation, manufacturing release and full route/fault confirmation |

The primary context remains six cars and 180-second headway for 14,400 normal
passengers/hour/direction. Changed speed, station spacing and formation are
explicit research alternatives, with complete service and civil consequences.
The immediate experiment priority remains geometry-resolved magnetic forces on
the same physical stock, followed by counted joints/cooling and coupled gap,
temperature and deformation maps. No integrated survivor is implied by a
completed digital campaign.

The [design completion execution plan](design-engine/ferrite-maglev/research-closure/README.md)
preserves all 576 qualification tasks across eight workstreams and orders the
next magnetic, propulsion and curve-layout experiments. Additional bounded
optimisation has executed 240 magnetic cases with no joint-screen survivor and
28 propulsion duty comparisons with 17 conditional coupon passes. Neither
changes the canonical candidate or completes the four-stop electromechanical
journey. Exact-source audits and negative controls accompany the new results.

The next executed design round adds 256 counted support/cooling cases, 18
separate-guidance material bounds and 160 finite-carrier/curve cases. Service
comparisons vary speed, station spacing, dwell and formation at both the current
comparison mass and the proposed support material-mass floor. Normal passenger
capacity, complete tail clearance, charging, added platform/powered-zone scope
and thermal failures stay visible. The [research specification](../engineering/maglev_research/design-intent.toml)
records these alternatives; no integrated configuration or low-cost advantage
is accepted. [README coverage](design-engine/ferrite-maglev/research-closure/readme-coverage.md)
maintains current introductions without altering frozen scientific packets.

The city briefs show current line/service tables, energy, local production routes and low/reference/high CAPEX/OPEX/debt sensitivities. The [connected Baghdad pilot](design-engine/ferrite-maglev/pilot-delivery/README.md) develops walking catchments, explicitly synthetic OD/accessibility, resource-constrained construction, procurement/monthly cash and serial asset/QA/maintenance interfaces for a selected current line. The native lifecycle campaign uses simulated assets in isolated business applications; it does not commission a railway. Extending that pilot across the whole catalogue, restoring the interactive GIS editor and independent SUMO comparison, and qualifying observed demand, sites and equipment remain separate work. Station-led GOA4 dispatch and the progressive-departure candidate retain their independent protection and acceptance gates. Whole-life affordability, owner-held design knowledge and domestic capability remain central selection criteria.

The [qualification programme](design-engine/ferrite-maglev/qualification/README.md)
maps the applicable requirements to dependency-ordered tasks, physical test
procedures and guarded raw-evidence assessment. It supplies the execution route
for this queue; every physical acceptance gate remains open until actual evidence
and independent dispositions exist. New [fitted structural CAD](design-engine/ferrite-maglev/assembled-body/README.md),
[multi-train controls](design-engine/ferrite-maglev/line-capacity/README.md) and
[shared-hub geometry](design-engine/ferrite-maglev/shared-hub-layout/README.md)
close defined digital studies while retaining their unresolved interfaces.

The implemented station study adds jerk-limited launch/coast/arrival calculations,
magnetic-band overlap, receiving-brake duty and headway/power/cost sensitivities.
The structure study adds quantity-derived GFRP cases, orientation seeds, five beam
families, support/foundation screens and a two-shift bottleneck model. These are
calculated candidates and production targets; they do not supply the missing
structural, magnetic or measured factory evidence.

The adaptive designer uses preceding results to propose vehicle-mass, beam/span
and actual Rust-gain variants. It preserves ancestry/traces and uses separate
confirmation cases. Its original search plant is a stationary single rigid car;
the moving six-car plant and longer-corridor confirmations are separate evidence.
All currently use ideal magnetic force channels. A preferred candidate remains a bounded
research result, without automatic promotion to accepted geometry or city criteria.

The dependency runner rebuilds stale implemented studies and holds downstream
outputs after failures. Completing every automated check does not complete the
engineering queue. Published v0.5.1 remains a distinct preserved release; this
revision is documented in [v0.5.2 development notes](releases/v0.5.2.md).
