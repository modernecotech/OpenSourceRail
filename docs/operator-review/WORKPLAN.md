# From design review to railway opening

This is a work programme for a prospective railway owner and operator. It starts with decisions they can take using the published designs. The dates below are proposed work periods from appointment of a sponsor and access to the required people and records. The digital reference can support consultation and indicative quotations. The owner releases money for construction and passenger operation through separate approvals.

## Cost and test the shift workplan

## Building the core railway

The [connected core timetable](../reference-v07/integration/TIMETABLE.md) assigns 576 passenger trips per day to 44 sets. Its proposed stabling plan requires three berth positions at each terminal. Price crossover, stabling and rescue arrangements against the blocked-berth and failed-set cases.

The 30 km cost case allows **43 months** after approved definition. The owner's engineer confirms land, utilities, grid and ground in months 0–6. Civil contractors release foundations and piers in months 6–24. Two precast yards cast in months 8–31 and two erection fronts place beams in months 15–35. Rail and power teams install in months 26–38. Station, depot, grid and controls teams commission individual systems in months 29–40. The operator and independent assessor use months 40–43 for integration, exercises and trial running. The supplier must reconcile beam storage and haulage during the casting lead and provide a possession plan for each lift. [Cost, quantities and programme](../reference-v07/cost/README.md) · [Factory and workforce plan](delivery/README.md)

At definition, settle the site and 2 km demonstration geometry. Before a repeat order, witness one bogie exchange and inspection, a body lifting trial, one accessible boarding setup, one beam lift and bearing replacement, and loaded braking and loss-of-supply recovery. Instrument interfaces on the actual build. Obtain independent disposition of each failed limit, approval of operating rules, competent rosters and the emergency plan. Only then can the owner agree the passenger-opening date. [Prototype interfaces](../reference-v07/integration/README.md) · [Mechanical and electrical test assemblies](../../engineering/interface_studies/REFERENCE-RIGS.md)

## Opening a railway in Baghdad

The **seven-corridor** and **citywide** programmes have independent fleets, stations, power sections, budgets and construction dates. The 2027 start and the first full-service budgets in 2032 and 2036 are scenario assumptions. Earlier offers fell short of government requirements. NIC described new studies and investment opportunities. Iraq's Ministry of Planning convened a metro workshop on 10 November. Use late 2026 to early 2027 to prepare proposals and obtain the authority's revised terms and dates. [Amanat Baghdad interview](https://almadapaper.net/458226/) · [NIC interview](https://ninanews.com/website/News/Details?key=1322248) · [Ministry workshop](https://mop.gov.iq/archives/40342)

1. **Define the first passenger service.** Select a terminal pair against observed trips, interchange quality, access to jobs and hospitals, depot connection and the ability to turn trains. Walk the terminal-building route with airport operations. Locate Sadr City and Jadriya entrances with local users. The fourteen-route operating case defers five access sections, including both M04 branches. Survey their connections before adding their passengers or train movements. [Maps and location register](../../cities/Baghdad/maps/README.md)
2. **Build a self-sufficient opening package.** Start with the [corrected Airport service and price comparison](../../cities/Baghdad/engineering/simulation/AUDIT-RESULTS.md). Its working alignment covers 17.46 km. The priced capital subtotal leaves feeder equipment, end closures and owner exposures open. The [earlier $813.7m opening estimate](../../cities/Baghdad/AIRPORT-OPENING.md) covers the 12 km schematic and its own dedicated facilities. Take off the actual running spans, difficult foundations, station structures, conductor, return circuit, substation, controls, rescue and access roads for the chosen section. Price shared assets and temporary works explicitly. Assign line isolation, emergency access, medical response and replacement parts to named teams. A fraction of the complete network budget cannot establish the opening cost. [Baghdad civil, station and vehicle studies](../../cities/Baghdad/engineering/mechanical/README.md)
3. **Agree how the city pays.** Compare public budget, supplier finance and mixed debt using annual draws, denomination, tenor, fees, guarantees, renewal reserves and public-service payments. Test downside ridership and power costs alongside construction delay. Attribute a budget obligation to the agency able to pay it. Record lender and government commitments separately. [Funding calculator](finance/index.html) · [Baghdad annual cash schedules](../../cities/Baghdad/COST-AND-DELIVERY.md)
4. **Put the open design to work in negotiations.** Issue the same service and priced quantity schedules for turnkey, vendor-led local production and open local integration. Seek revised price and verified Iraqi value added at each localisation level. Ask for editable drawings, source/build access, diagnostic rights, tooling title, warranty and spare parts. An Iraqi team should build the repeat article and complete an overhaul. Compare the resulting whole-life price and demonstrated capabilities even if another vendor wins. [Bid return and negotiation method](../../cities/Baghdad/BUYER-COMPARISON.md)
5. **Commission with the operator.** Recruit control-room, station, depot, track and power teams before acceptance trials. Exercise a failed train, passenger evacuation including assisted travel, heat or grid disruption, and a transfer of control between shifts. Register inspection intervals and spare holdings against installed asset IDs. Demonstrate dispatch, braking and electrical isolation under approved limits. Hand over as-built models, configuration history, defects, emergency procedures, maintenance budget and staff authorisations together. [Control cabinet and boarding trial](../../cities/Baghdad/engineering/controls/README.md) · [Employment and training](delivery/README.md)

## Compare street access and deck-fed erection

For a long run of standard spans, review two terminal precast yards and inward
deck-fed erection beside the road/crane method. Appoint the precaster, temporary
works designer, substructure contractor, traffic authority and erection
contractor before fixing the launch sequence. The construction programme needs
an agreed handover for every pier, starter bays, released deck track, safe
delivery access at both ends, the meeting span and demobilisation. Confirm
acoustic and access limits before pricing the third shift. [Cost, shift and
road-occupation comparison](../../cities/Baghdad/engineering/mechanical/civil/RAIL-FED-ERECTION.md)

## What to record after opening

The operator owns a monthly return of paid journeys, fare receipts, denied boardings, on-time departures, passenger assistance failures, asset availability, energy, maintenance hours, labour by grade and arrears on renewal funding. The maintainer links each repeated fault to the asset serial, model revision, software version and repair order. The owner compares actual costs and Iraqi value added with bid commitments each quarter. Record the observed baseline before agreeing performance targets or deductions.
