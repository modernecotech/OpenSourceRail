Who pays before the whole network opens

The central Iraqi capital allowance is $8.369 billion in constant 2026 USD. Apply the original annual construction draw weights once. Half the capital comes from debt at 4% real. Construction interest is on midyear draws and principal repayment starts in 2032 over 20 years.

Retain the full $12.27m annual payroll from the first passenger opening as a conservative mobilisation assumption. Remove payroll already embedded in train, station and maintenance allowances. Scale energy and other train costs by connected train-km, line maintenance by open length, and station running by named stop occurrences. The 64-station cost allowance needs an agreed assignment across the 52 named occurrences. Pre-opening staff mobilisation needs a separate commissioning estimate.

Allocate mature paid journeys by offered train-km pending line-specific counts. New lines take 40%, 70%, 100% of that demand in successive six-month periods and retain the last value. Full-network annual OPEX reaches $185.73m and mature net fares reach $79.52m. This assumes comparable passenger trip lengths and transfer rates across stages. Interchanges, mode share and peak loading require survey.

Age each parent renewal from the month that line enters service. Allocate fleet renewals by diagrammed sets and infrastructure renewals by route length. Price these renewals at the retained engineering allowances until contracts establish Iraqi replacement costs. The cash horizon ends after 30 years from the original full-service year. Earlier lines operate for additional months within that horizon.
