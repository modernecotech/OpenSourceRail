Model scope and configuration

One centred six-car train, one island and fourteen 10 m bays per track. Rail head is the vertical datum. Colour identifies component families. Door solids mark the openings. Window layout, shell thickness, wheel tread, rail profile and installation markers are illustrative. Pier solids indicate support positions. Platform substructure, foundations, station access, fasteners and cable routes require separate design.

Design occurrences have no physical serial or commissioning record. The scene supports dimensional review and discussion of access. Installation mass, safety performance and passenger operation require separate evidence.
