# Viaduct construction method

Planning method for the [civil master plan](civil-works-master-plan.md). Normal strategic span average is 25 m; use the [identified Baghdad spans](../../cities/catalogue/west-asia/Iraq/Baghdad/engineering/connected-build/span-layout.json) for orders and erection. Pi20, station approaches, closures, tight curves and special crossings stay individually resolved. Refer to the [design basis](viaduct-design-basis.md), [substructure kit](viaduct-substructure-kit.md), [bearing schedule](viaduct-bearing-and-movement-schedule.md) and [transport envelope](viaduct-transport-and-erection-envelope.md).

## 1. Release a complete workfront

The construction manager assembles a package for each continuous run: line/chainage, span and support IDs, survey datums, accepted design revision, land and access, trial holes and utility isolation/diversion, ground model, temporary works, traffic/pedestrian arrangements, environmental limits, approved lifting route and emergency access. Record station/junction/water/road boundaries and whether the launcher can pass, cross with a checked support arrangement, or dismantle/relocate. A visually continuous alignment is not proof of machine passage.

Stake support centres and offsets from redundant control; check against track alignment, utilities and adjoining structures. Establish settlement/vibration monitoring where excavations, piling or temporary loads affect property. Release piling/crane platforms with verified bearing, drainage, edges and underground-service exclusion. Install hoarding, vehicle/pedestrian segregation, lighting, welfare, spill response, washout and spoil management before production.

## 2. Select and construct foundations by ground zone

Select shallow foundations, bored shafts, driven bents, pile groups or ground treatment using the [foundation system](foundation-and-production-system.md). The selection must consider actual axial/lateral actions, settlement, seismic/collision, groundwater, scour, nearby buildings and installation access. Record support-specific geometry and installed quantities. Do not assign one pile depth to the route or assume a generic foundation volume per kilometre.

| Method | Proposed sequence | Release and retained records |
|---|---|---|
| Shallow footing/socket | Verify excavation/support and groundwater method; excavate to design level; inspect founding material; install blinding, reinforcement/inserts and formwork; cast, cure, survey and backfill in tested layers | Formation acceptance, level/bearing basis, reinforcement and concrete records, drainage/settlement protection |
| Bored shaft | Trial method and test foundation; prepare working platform; drill using design-selected casing/slurry; record strata and geometry; clean/inspect base; lower checked cage; place concrete by the released dry-hole or submerged method; inspect/test and trim for connection | Drilling and slurry records, cage position, concrete continuity/volume, integrity/load tests, accepted actual length |
| Driven bent/piles | Verify vibration/property constraints and platform; trial installation; lift and pitch with approved restraints; drive/splice to the accepted criteria; test and survey; trim and connect | Driving/hammer/penetration records, damage/splice checks, verification tests and final pile level/length |
| Pile group/cap | Install and verify group; excavate/shoring/dewater as designed; prepare pile heads; fit reinforcement/cap shell and column interface; cast/cure and restore drainage | Group effects and lateral design, pile/cap records, connection survey, excavation and backfill release |

For submerged bored-shaft concreting, use the designer's tremie procedure, verified mix workability/retention and continuous placement records; no generic embedment, strength or acceptance number is issued here. [FHWA's drilled-shaft manual](https://www.fhwa.dot.gov/engineering/geotech/nhi18024.pdf) is a method-development reference for drilling, concrete placement and inspection. Site trial, project specification and independent review determine the applicable procedure and test criteria.

Treat groundwater disposal and drilling spoil as planned waste streams. Maintain excavation support and dewatering until the designer permits removal; monitor drawdown and adjacent settlement. Failed integrity/strength/dimension results enter NCR control before a support is released.

## 3. Columns, caps, abutments and bearings

Compare in-situ columns with precast columns or shells using complete connections, temporary bracing, access and installed costs. Survey foundation/interface levels; prepare sockets or starter reinforcement; install the column/form with checked temporary restraint; inspect reinforcement, inserts, access and earthing; cast/grout and cure to the connection-specific strength. Maintain bracing until the designed permanent path is established and signed off.

For a precast or hollow-shell cap, verify complete mass, CG and lifting anchors against the configured crane/launcher and actual ground reactions. Survey seating and reinforcement/socket interfaces; erect, restrain, connect and complete infill/grouting under the approved stage design. Do not treat the 25–35 t catalogue cap target as a measured lifting weight. In-situ caps require independently checked formwork/falsework, pre-pour inspection and staged striking criteria.

Construct abutments, retained approaches and wing walls with released earth pressure, settlement, drainage and flood/scour design. Survey bearing seats and orientation. Install fixed/guided/free bearings, anchorages and grout to supplier/project instructions, including temperature datum, permitted movements and replacement-jacking interfaces. A support-ready packet includes foundation verification, column/cap strength, geometry, bearing inspection, connection status and the actual next launcher/delivery reactions.

## 4. Produce and accept full-span beams

Use suitable existing Iraqi factories after auditing available production slots and the complete process. Freeze drawings, tendons/anchors, reinforcement, embeds, lifting/support locations and shop traveller against each beam/track/span ID. Survey mould and bed; inspect cages, ducts/inserts and calibrated stressing plant; cast the released mix with batch traceability and compaction method; retain curing temperature/maturity and test specimens.

Prestress transfer, demoulding, lifting, storage and installation are distinct strength gates. Release time comes from the qualified mix and test/maturity relationship, not elapsed hours alone. The existing 48-hour planning cycle stays a capacity assumption until trials validate it. A 24-hour target requires a new qualified cycle and equipment/thermal assessment. Sequence transfer and lifting to the supplier design; inspect cracks, camber, bearing/lifting zones and dimensions after transfer and handling.

The current Baghdad complete-member studies are approximately 69.68 t for Pi20 and 85.51 t for Pi25; with the illustrative 4 t gear, suspended studies are 73.68 and 89.51 t. These are unqualified budgeted masses from [the connected beam register](../../cities/catalogue/west-asia/Iraq/Baghdad/engineering/connected-build/civil.json). Bare-section targets are a different scope. Require actual complete-member mass/CG and lifting design before selecting machinery; a nominal 100 t machine does not establish configured capacity.

Accepted members enter segregated serialized storage on designed supports with verified ground/stacking/restraint. Quarantine NCR items. Preserve strength, dimensions, lifting-anchor certificates, support positions and dispatch readiness in the traveller. Transport handling adds inspections at loading and receipt; acceptance at the factory does not clear transport damage.

## 5. Erect and commission the launcher

Deliver components against the [logistics plan](construction-logistics-plan.md). Verify assembly space, ground bearing, traffic closure, lifting equipment and the launcher foundation/deck support arrangement. Assemble to supplier instructions; inspect structural/pin/bolted connections, electrical/hydraulic systems, brakes, limit devices and emergency arrangements. Complete required tests and documented commissioning at the actual configuration before use.

The stage design covers assembly, loaded/unloaded operation, launch/advance, turning or relocation, one-beam eccentricity, gust exposure, parked/storm state, delivery vehicle positions, breakdown and dismantling. Check every loaded pier, cap, bearing and previously erected span. Over-deck deliveries require construction load paths and protection against unauthorized additional plant. Ground-delivered erection requires a separately released access path and unloading position.

## 6. Repeat the bay cycle

1. Confirm daily/shift release: next span/support IDs, current beam traveller, work permit, weather/visibility within supplier limits, exclusion/traffic arrangements, healthy equipment, competent rested crew and communications. Recheck changes since the previous shift.
2. Verify seats, bearing orientation/levels, lifting gear and beam identity. Place transport in the permitted position; secure against movement. Review load, CG, lift path, rigging and contingency landing location with the team.
3. Attach only the released lifting points/gear. Apply the controlled trial-lift/check from the lift plan. Confirm balance, connections and clearances before travelling; stop for any unexpected movement or communication loss.
4. Place the first single-track beam on surveyed seats. Maintain the designed lateral/torsional restraints and required crane/launcher support. Do not release the load merely because it touches the bearings.
5. Place the second beam using the checked asymmetric sequence. Survey alignment, bearing contact and joint gaps; complete permanent/temporary restraints and necessary connection/grout strength gates before releasing the next stage.
6. Complete the bay inspection/accepted record. Advance only when the construction-load path is released, next supports are ready, delivery and next-machine positions are checked, and the worksite remains segregated.
7. Hand over a stable inspected bay to following trades; record outstanding restrictions, access/edge protection and permitted construction loads. Update stock, installed component IDs, cycle time, NCRs, weather and equipment hours.

Measure preparation, loading/arrival, each beam lift and securing, inspection, advance, handover and interruptions separately. Two lifts plus launcher advance are one double-track bay cycle. The plan does not prescribe wind limits, lifting capacity, rigging angles or concrete strengths: the approved equipment/stage design supplies them. Complex or tandem lifting needs its own competent written plan; [HSE lifting-operation guidance](https://www.hse.gov.uk/construction/safetytopics/lifting-operations.htm) is a practice reference.

## 7. Connections and railway finish

Construct the selected continuity connection or link slab only to the released reinforcement, pour, cure, bearing and stage sequence. A link slab preserves independent girder-end support; it does not justify reducing bearing lines. Retain temporary restraints and movement controls until their removal is authorized. Inspect grout, concrete, joints, waterproofing continuity and drainage paths.

Install independent walkway/barrier cassettes, fall containment, cable/drainage troughs and earthing interfaces with their own handling and fixing designs. Keep the escape walkway clear. Survey track datum; prepare waterproofing/alignment interfaces; install plinths or the selected trackform, adjustable fasteners and rail; complete rail/structure interaction, movement and geometry checks. Install systems only under an agreed load/penetration/earthing interface; unreviewed drilling into prestressed members is prohibited by the proposed method.

## 8. Run boundaries, specials, recovery and demobilisation

Before each station, closure or disconnected run, decide whether to pass using a designed temporary support, bridge with an engineered launcher configuration, or dismantle and reassemble. Model closure permissions, access, transport, setup/commissioning time and resource reservations. A shorter span is not automatically launcher-compatible. Special spans require their own product and order; no Pi25 order is generated for a residual unsupported length.

Prepare recovery for hydraulic/electrical failure, suspended-load stall, damaged bearing, unseated beam, loss of support readiness and weather stop. Define a safe supported/parked condition, exclusion and communications, engineered rescue/repair, inspection and re-release. Stop deliveries when stock/access is saturated. No improvisational re-rigging, unreviewed props or moving a suspended load over the public is authorized by this plan.

After a front finishes, check the remaining critical path and candidate ring fronts. Inspect/dismantle, transport, verify new supports/access, recommission and reassess the crew before reassignment. Release a machine to another city only after the complete Baghdad programme and resource review. Close final bearing/structure surveys, as-built records, drainage and access, defects and monitoring before structural handover.
