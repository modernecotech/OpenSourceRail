# LM3 Wayside Rerailing Access Interface

Status: optional design-reference envelope for selected recovery nodes. It is
not a universal requirement to pave the whole alignment and is not a released
foundation or lift design.

## Purpose

Vehicle portability does not by itself make a derailment easy to recover. The
hydraulic cylinders and transverse bridge need level bearing, road-accessible
offload space, an unobstructed route to the vehicle, and an exclusion zone.
`civil-wayside-rerailing-access-interface` reserves those interfaces at
strategic locations such as depot approaches, terminal throats, junctions and
long or operationally difficult line sections.

## Controlled Envelope

| Interface | Design-reference value |
|---|---:|
| Rerailing hardstanding | 18,000 x 4,000 mm |
| Equipment offload/staging pad | 6,000 x 4,000 mm |
| Temporary exclusion zone | 22,000 x 12,000 mm |
| Transverse bridge length | 4,000 mm |
| Bridge-bearing zones | X = +/-5,600 mm at the LM3 transverse jack pairs |
| Rail gauge | 1,435 mm |

The CAD source shows one-sided road access. Deployment designs may mirror it,
but must preserve the cross-track equipment path and verify that the response
vehicle can stop and unload without fouling another open railway or public
traffic lane.

## Site Selection

Recovery nodes should be selected from an operational response-time study,
not at arbitrary uniform spacing. Prefer locations with all-weather road
access, low-gradient tangent track, drainage, lighting/power opportunities,
communications coverage and enough space to establish railway and public
exclusion zones. Avoid locating the only planned node where a bridge parapet,
tunnel wall, platform, live conductor, utility corridor, steep cutting or soft
embankment prevents lateral translation or equipment deployment.

## Civil Release Requirements

1. Survey track, cess, access-road, drainage, buried services, overhead/live
   electrical equipment and adjacent property constraints.
2. Establish allowable ground bearing and settlement for the released jack,
   bridge, cribbing and response-vehicle reactions, including saturated and
   post-incident ground conditions.
3. Design the slab, reinforced formation or deployable spreader solution from
   those reactions; the catalogue envelope contains no assumed soil capacity.
4. Preserve two 1.6 m wide cross-track handling routes at the LM3 transverse
   jack pairs and keep covers, cabinets and drainage hardware removable or out
   of those routes.
5. Define traffic management, railway protection, traction-power isolation,
   lighting, communications and emergency access arrangements.
6. Trial the production equipment and representative vehicle at one first
   article node, then survey and record the as-built bearing/staging datums.

The paired vehicle/equipment load cases and prohibited configurations are in
the [LM3 portable field-rerailing concept](../rolling-stock/light-metro-3car/field-rerailing-concept.md).
