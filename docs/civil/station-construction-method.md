# Station civil and access construction method

Apply with the [civil master plan](civil-works-master-plan.md), [other civil works](other-civil-works-methods.md), [logistics](construction-logistics-plan.md) and [inspection plan](civil-inspection-and-handover.md). Each station has a generated individual package. Physical platforms, boarding faces and access assemblies are separate quantity bases. The current layouts identify equipment and interfaces; they do not release station structures, fire design or street entrances.

## Station package and interface freeze

Freeze station ID/chainage, elevation and topology, physical platform lengths/widths, track spread/return, concourse and roof geometry, boarding faces, train/PSD clearance, shafts/stairs/escalators, accessible routes, utilities and drainage. Review actual boarding/alighting/transfer pulses, queues and equipment-outage capacity against the chosen layout. Include fire separation, evacuation and assisted rescue, cleaning/maintenance and emergency access. Confirm land rights for every entrance and pedestrian connection, rather than only the station centre point.

Coordinate structural spans/supports with the running viaduct and turnouts. Station widenings, lift shafts, concourse loads, roof/wind actions and launcher passage require a station-specific structural and temporary-stage design. Do not assume the running Pi25 deck or standard cap carries all station loads. Freeze load-transfer boundaries and dimensional datums before commissioning shop drawings.

The station lead prepares a delivery package with civil, architectural, MEP, fire/access, track, power/earthing, signalling/PSD and operations interface owners. Record sequencing constraints, tolerances and hold points. Select modular/precast platforms, stairs, shaft shells or steelwork only after installation access, lifting, connections, fire/durability and complete cost are reviewed.

## Elevated station sequence

| Stage | Construction method and coordination | Exit evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Enabling | Maintain roads, shop/pedestrian and emergency access; locate/divert utilities; construct isolated working platforms and approved crane/haul areas | Land/utility releases, traffic plan, ground/temporary-works checks |
| Foundations/substructure | Install station-specific foundations and verify; cast/erect columns, caps and shaft/concourse supports with temporary restraint | Actual foundation/test records, pre-pour inspections, strength and dimensional release |
| Primary structure | Erect beams/steelwork/platform support and concourse in checked stages; release bearings, connections and bracing | Stage-load checks, lifting records, welding/bolting/grout and survey acceptance |
| Platforms and edges | Install slabs/units, drainage, edge/PSD support and walkways; survey platform height/gap against track/train dynamic interface | Slab/connection records, tolerances and complete clear circulation envelope |
| Shafts and stairs | Build pits/shafts/landings and stair structures; fit temporary covers/barriers; keep access for equipment installation | Lift-maker interface, pit waterproofing, openings/loads, fire/access and fall-protection review |
| Roof and enclosure | Assemble checked roof/canopy/containment with restraints; complete flashings, drainage and weatherproofing | Structural/fixing and water tests, clear evacuation and maintenance routes |
| MEP and railway systems | Install ductwork, cables, earthing, plant bases, lighting, drainage, fire and passenger/PSD equipment against approved penetrations | Civil/system handover, isolation and inspection/test records |
| Access equipment | Supplier installs/commissions lifts and escalators after shaft/power/clearance acceptance; preserve stair access and protection during work | Certificates, functional/rescue/outage tests and independent access acceptance |
| Finishes and street routes | Install nonslip surfaces, wayfinding, lighting, barriers/crossings and accessible approaches; reinstate construction areas | Walk-through, drainage/lighting/finish checks and local street handback |
| Integrated handover | Test evacuation, assisted rescue, queues, PSD/train alignment, maintainability and outage operation with the final installed configuration | Accepted as-built station packet, resolved defects/restrictions and opening approval |

Coordinate roof, platform-edge, shaft and track work so no crew works beneath another uncontrolled load or open edge. Temporary public routing cannot use an unfinished platform or uncommissioned lift. Define safe supplier installation paths for replacement motors/cabs/PSD modules; close permanent apertures only after large equipment has passed.

## At-grade station sequence

Release land, crossings, utility isolation and traffic/pedestrian diversion. Construct drainage and reviewed earthworks/ground treatment; verify settlement and finished formation. Install services and foundations before slabs/platforms; provide protected draw pits and crossings. Cast or erect platforms and access structures with surveyed track/edge datum and bedding/support control.

Lay the selected slab/trackform and rail; complete platform faces, PSD support, canopy and systems. Maintain a continuous step-free street-to-platform path with drainage and crossings; isolate construction traffic. Complete lighting, signage, fencing, barriers and road/public-realm restoration. Test train/platform and access interfaces using the actual installed configuration and outage/rescue plan.

At-grade topology does not imply zero station civil cost. Soil treatment, retained levels, canopy/wind, service trenches, drainage/outfalls, safe street crossings and accessibility may govern the package. Elevated and ground-level interchange platforms need a reviewed transfer route and shared-resource capacity assessment.

## Track spreading and running-viaduct interfaces

The generated running-span schedule excludes station/transition intervals. Resolve each excluded chainage with the station support plan, track-spread geometry, curves/cant, platform loads, movement joints and switch/closure interfaces. Confirm the station-end pier/cap boundary is counted once. Reconcile the approach lengths and support/beam take-off before ordering; do not fill the interval with rounded Pi25 bays.

Maintain a station-interface register covering datum, loads, tolerances, bearing/joint roles, waterproofing/drainage, egress, earthing/stray current, cable routes and erection access. The bridge and station authorities sign the structural/temporary-stage boundaries; track and systems leads sign their following-trade interfaces. Record whether the launcher crosses, parks short, or relocates around the station.

## Logistics and workforce

Deliver bulky station structures and access equipment in installation order. Reserve off-road receiving space, checked crane/platform positions, shaft access and damage-free storage; keep lift/electrical components dry and secure to supplier requirements. Check dimensions and CG for stair/shaft/canopy modules independently of viaduct beam haulage. Plan returnable racks, packaging and rejection/replacement movements.

Reserve separate survey, concrete/precast, steelwork, roofing, access-equipment, MEP, track/PSD and independent inspection crews. Resource-share only when actual shift and task reservations allow it. Include work at height, rigging quantities, confined/pit access where applicable, lifting equipment authority, rescue competence, relief, heat management and handovers in the workload plan.

## Hold points and handover

Stop before foundation production, primary lifting, temporary-support removal, covering reinforcement/ducts, waterproofing concealment, rail/PSD fixing and public access. Inspect platform equipment containment and continuous circulation paths after installation, including cabinets, covers and barriers. Test one-lift-out routing and throughput, assisted rescue, emergency power where selected, evacuation and smoke/fire assumptions; graph reachability alone is insufficient.

As-built deliverables include support/structure and service drawings, surveyed rail/platform geometry, installed component serials, material/connection/test records, operations/rescue and maintenance manuals, replacement access, defects, BCF/NCR closure and independently accepted station release. A civil handover for systems installation does not authorize passenger opening.
