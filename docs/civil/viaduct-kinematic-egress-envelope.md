# Viaduct Kinematic And Egress Envelope

The controlled planning width is derived from the train and evacuation need:

| Component | Width |
|---|---:|
| Dynamic train envelope | 2.97 m |
| Clear escape walkway | 1.00 m |
| Opposite-side clearance | 0.25 m |
| Sway, handrail, tolerance, and study reserve | 0.22 m |
| **Minimum tangent planning clear width** | **4.44 m** |
| 25 m straight-span chord allowance at 300 m radius | 0.261 m |
| **OSR reference clear width** | **4.72 m** |

The 4.72 m value is a planning seed, not a substitute for a deployment-specific
kinematic simulation, cant/throw calculation, evacuation flow study, handrail
envelope, or accessibility review. The escape ledge must remain 1.0 m clear
after cable covers, drainage, screen posts, and construction tolerances.

For a straight span on a circular curve, mid-chord offset is:

`offset = radius - sqrt(radius^2 - (span/2)^2)`

| Span | Offset at 90 m radius |
|---:|---:|
| 15 m | 0.31 m |
| 20 m | 0.56 m |
| 25 m | 0.87 m |
| 30 m | 1.26 m |

The reference 25 m straight unit retains about 0.28 m after the train,
walkway, opposite-side clearance, and protected 0.22 m reserve. It therefore
passes the catalogue screen at 300 m radius without spending that reserve but
fails at 90 m. OSR-US or another curved/special deck is then
required unless the alignment is relaxed.

Measure the 1.4 m containment/parapet datum above the finished escape walkway.
Cover every structural gap with a maintainable evacuation-path plate and keep
cable routes continuous without blocking drainage or bearing inspection.
