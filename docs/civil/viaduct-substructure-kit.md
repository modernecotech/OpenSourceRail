# Viaduct pier and abutment kit

The controlled planning geometry is generated by
`osr_mech.civil.substructure`. A standard elevated cross-section uses two
single-track decked Pi-beams at 3.5 m centres on one shared hollow/precast-shell cap.
These are interface and quantity envelopes, not stamped foundations.

## Pier EBOM

| ID | Product | Quantity basis | Release gate |
|---|---|---:|---|
| `CIV-PIER-P010` | Soil/access-selected foundation catalogue interface | 1 set | Actual length/cost, site geotechnical and foundation test release |
| `CIV-PIER-P020` | 1.5 × 2.0 m RC pier column | Selected height, 5–12 vertical m | Reinforcement, seismic, and collision calculation |
| `CIV-PIER-P030` | 7 m hollow/precast-shell shared cap | 1 | 25–35 t target, lifting, reinforcement, connection and cap calculation |
| `CIV-PIER-P040` | Fixed/guided/free bearing | 4 at internal support; 8 at expansion-unit boundary | Supplier and bearing/movement schedule freeze |
| `CIV-PIER-P045` | Bearing-replacement jacking interface | 4 | Jacking and maintenance-load calculation |
| `CIV-PIER-P050` | Drainage, earthing, access, and identification | 1 kit | Site services and inspection release |

Assembly sequence: release survey/geotechnics and temporary works; construct
the foundation; cast or erect the column and cap; install and survey the
role-selected bearing line(s); lift the paired Pi-beams; then complete the
released diaphragm or link-slab connection after the erection hold point closes.

## Abutment EBOM

| ID | Product | Quantity | Release gate |
|---|---|---:|---|
| `CIV-ABT-P010` | Foundation interface | 1 set | Site geotechnical and foundation release |
| `CIV-ABT-P020` | Bearing shelf and backwall | 1 | Structural and drainage calculation |
| `CIV-ABT-P030` | RC wing wall | 2 | Earth-pressure/retaining-wall release |
| `CIV-ABT-P040` | Elastomeric/PTFE bearing | 4 | Supplier and bearing schedule freeze |
| `CIV-ABT-P050` | Replaceable expansion joint | 1 | Thermal movement and supplier freeze |
| `CIV-ABT-P060` | Approach slab, drainage, earthing, and access | 1 kit | Settlement, drainage, and inspection release |

Assembly sequence: release earthworks, geotechnics, drainage and thermal
movement; construct foundation, shelf, backwall, wing walls, and approach;
install bearings and expansion joint; then survey seats, clearances, settlement
datum, drainage, and access before girder erection.

Deployment-specific pile type/count/actual length and cost, scour protection, utility diversions,
reinforcement, seismic/collision design, soil pressure, and erection temporary
works remain outside the upstream catalogue.
