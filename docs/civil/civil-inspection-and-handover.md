# Civil inspection, testing and handover plan

Apply to the [master plan](civil-works-master-plan.md) and method-specific asset register. The [existing catalogue ITP](../../design/component-catalogue/catalog/buildable-civil/inspection-and-test-plan.md) retains reusable-product characteristics; this plan adds site/workface and full-line interfaces. Project designers define test methods, sampling, acceptance values and independent-review scope. Empty criteria or unsigned results cannot close a hold point.

H = stop until authorized release; W = notified witness opportunity under the agreed ITP; R = controlled record review. Each row must name responsible inspector, independent/owner role where required, current asset/design/method revision, actual test/result, acceptance basis, date and signed disposition. Proposed role titles are not approvals.

## Test planning and first repetition

Select representative foundation/ground zones and temporary-loading configurations, Pi20/Pi25/cap/column production and transport/lifts, complete first bay with connection/track/walkways, first station primary structure and access equipment, first at-grade slab/panel zone, special crossing and depot/pit/energy interfaces. Test both ordinary installation and credible degraded/recovery conditions. The representative unit does not qualify unlike ground zones, structural products or equipment configurations without a reviewed extension.

Record baseline calibration, test setup/instrumentation, operator/inspector competence, measured values, uncertainty, raw files, criteria and configuration. Review results before shortening cure cycles, increasing erection rates, reducing restraints/buffers or repeating a changed method. Failed results stay in the package and trigger investigation/rework/retest under a controlled NCR.

## Following-trade and final handover

Use a signed boundary packet for each run/station/site: allowed loads/work/access, residual temporary works, edge protection, drainage/earthing, penetrations, installed component IDs, inspection state and outstanding restrictions. Systems contractors accept the civil interface before mounting, pulling cables or energising. No shared folder or completed-task flag substitutes for that packet.

At final handover, reconcile every purchased/installed item to the asset and drawing revision; retain survey/model/IFC references, supplier certificates, material/installation/test records and NCR/BCF closure. Transfer inspection/maintenance intervals, bearing/joint replacement and recovery access, drainage cleaning, warranties/spares and monitoring baselines. Record owner/operator/independent and local-authority acceptance separately.

Store native ERP references for Project, Task, Asset, construction release and competence/reservations, with access-controlled personal records. Public planning artifacts contain proposed roles and unaccepted evidence templates; they do not publish invented inspector identities or customer approvals.
