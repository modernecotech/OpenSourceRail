# Other civil works construction methods

These methods complete the [civil master plan](civil-works-master-plan.md). Define project quantities and release criteria from survey, design and local approvals; route length is not an earthworks, pipe, pile or building take-off. Use the [inspection and handover plan](civil-inspection-and-handover.md) for concealed work and following-trade release.

## Surveys, enabling, demolition and utilities

Establish verified horizontal/vertical control and a maintained survey network. Survey ground, existing roads/drains, water levels, structures, property, trees and utility routes; combine records with detection and trial holes. Resolve clashes in the common model and issue utility-owner approved protection/diversion/isolation plans. Locate working platforms, stockpiles and access outside protected services.

Before demolition or site clearance, establish ownership, hazardous-material/waste findings, structural sequence, adjacent support, service isolation, segregated removal route and disposal/recovery destinations. Maintain traffic, pedestrians and emergency access; record vibration, dust/noise and settlement where relevant. Protect retained assets and survey adjoining property before intrusive work. Restore survey control disturbed by construction.

Utility diversions precede conflicting excavation/piling. Construct replacement ducts/pipes and chambers, test, switch service under owner control, verify the new route, then remove/protect the obsolete asset. Record actual depths/offsets and access in as-built data before concealment. A drawing clash marked resolved is not field clearance.

## Earthworks, formation and ground improvement

Strip and segregate reusable topsoil; test excavated material and decide reuse, treatment or disposal. Install temporary drainage and designed cut/excavation support before bulk works. Excavate/fill to surveyed profiles with inspected lifts, moisture/compaction control, test locations and proof/formation acceptance appropriate to the design. Track quantities using survey surfaces and truck/weighbridge records; account for swell, shrinkage and rejected material in haul planning.

Select rigid inclusions/load-transfer platforms, soil mixing, stone columns, stabilisation or lightweight fill by zone-specific settlement, drainage, cyclic loads, liquefaction and utilities/access constraints. Trial the method and verify equipment/material supply before production. Retain installation depths/geometry, mixing or compaction parameters, tests and settlement monitoring. The [foundation/production system](foundation-and-production-system.md) supplies selection interfaces, not site treatment depths.

Release formation only after drainage, bearing/settlement and survey acceptance. Protect it from delivery traffic and weather; repair and reverify damage before trackwork. Sequence treatment and trenches so later excavation does not remove or undermine accepted treatment.

## Retaining structures, ramps and transitions

Review reinforced-soil walls, embankments, cast/precast retaining walls and abutments using land width, flood/scour, bearing/settlement, seismic and construction access. Install foundations and drainage, then facing/reinforcement and controlled fill in the designer's sequence. Keep heavy compaction/plant away from faces according to the approved method. Inspect hidden reinforcement, filters and outlets before each closure.

At grade-to-viaduct transitions, coordinate stiffness/settlement, rail geometry, movement, approach slabs, drainage and maintenance access. Survey as-built levels and monitor settlement through the selected stabilization/acceptance period. The 4.5 m retained-approach selection envelope is a catalogue planning rule, not permission to retain an unsuitable flood or soft-ground site.

## At-grade and elevated track civil interfaces

For long accessible at-grade runs, compare the released continuous slipform method with constrained ST6 panel zones. Install tested formation, subbase and service/drainage crossings first. Survey line/level and reinforcement/inserts; cast and cure the selected slab or prepare bedding and erect panels on designed supports. Inspect bedding/grout, panel joints, anchors, drainage and stray-current interfaces. Use project-defined trial panels/slipform lengths before repetition.

Keep closure pours at station ends, turnouts, transitions and surveyed corrections separately designed. Record panel IDs and final coordinates. The [slab-trackform catalogue](slab-trackforms.md) has a 6 m single-track module; double track requires two rows. Panel mass is a bare study envelope; complete lifting/transport includes embeds, gear and configuration. Do not buy a rounded panel length that extends outside an identified method zone.

For elevated track, release deck geometry, movement, waterproofing and drainage before track supports. Fit local plinths/alignment layers and adjustable fasteners to the reviewed rail/structure design. Protect prestressing and waterproofing during fixing. Rail welding/stressing, alignment/cant and fastening acceptance follow the railway specification; the civil plan supplies foundations and interfaces, not invented rail neutral temperatures or tolerances.

## Drainage, water management and flood protection

Prepare catchments, storm basis, surveyed outfalls, receiving-system capacity, water-quality requirements and flood/scour scenarios. Design temporary drainage through all earthwork and foundation stages; avoid redirecting water into occupied streets or neighboring foundations. Sequence permanent culverts, cross-drains, attenuation and outfall protection ahead of dependent formation.

Construct foundations/bedding and pipes/channels, inspect joint levels, watertightness and access, then backfill in tested layers. Keep service separations and chamber access. Clean/CCTV or otherwise inspect the specified system; verify gravity falls and pumps/alarms/backups where required. Test before concealment and again before handover. Elevated deck drains, station roofs/shafts, abutment drainage and depot contaminated/washwater systems need separate catchments and controlled discharge.

Protect water crossings against construction runoff, debris, washout and accidental spills. Investigate erosion/scour, flood levels and safe construction windows. Temporary causeways/cofferdams, bank loading and navigational restrictions require hydrological/geotechnical design and permissions; no blanket dry-season assumption is adopted.

## Special bridges, closure spans and road conflicts

The bridge/water civil class and unresolved running closures are separate inventories. Survey each conflict and compare rail bridge, road relocation/grade separation, longer-span steel/composite or segmental solutions, short verified products and alignment alternatives. Include road clearance, navigation/flood, utilities/property, foundations, track geometry, temporary works, traffic disruption and whole installed cost.

Issue a site-specific staged design and supplier package before buying special members. Define lifting/segmental or launching sequence, supports, stability, post-tensioning/joints where selected, closure geometry and rail/structure interaction. Coordinate permissions and shutdowns with adjacent running/station packages. A residual 23.3 m interval does not become a Pi25 catalogue beam, and a short segment does not automatically satisfy shipping width.

## Depot, workshop and stabling civil

Freeze actual yard tracks, usable berth lengths, switches/leads, clearances, departure queues, charging positions and maintenance/recovery access. Reconcile declared storage against the [depot scope](../../cities/catalogue/west-asia/Iraq/Baghdad/engineering/depot-scope/summary.json). Baghdad's one declared heavy depot and 116 stalls do not establish physical overnight provision for all 772 trainsets; workshop bays and passenger platforms are not verified parking.

Sequence land/utility release, ground/drainage treatment, underground services, building/pit and equipment foundations, superstructure/enclosure, slab/pavements, yard track/turnouts, equipment installation, MEP/fire and commissioning. Inspect workshop pits for support, groundwater/waterproofing, ventilation/access/rescue and fall protection; coordinate jacking/bogie extraction and crane reactions with equipment suppliers. Do not load slabs or pits with plant before release.

Separate dirty washwater/oil drainage from stormwater; provide selected treatment and permitted discharge. Design stores, delivery/road turning, fire routes, staff access, waste, workshop extraction and equipment replacement. Fit charging/PV/battery bases, ducts and protected separation only after actual energy/fire/thermal requirements and usable layout are reviewed. Conduct yard-route and repeated-day commissioning with the final rail/charging graph.

## Energy, equipment-room and systems civil

Survey every station/depot/wayside energy site. Freeze supplier foundation reactions, equipment dimensions, access/replacement envelopes, electrical isolation, fire separation, earthing and flood levels. Construct foundations, plinths, retaining and drainage; install ducts/chambers/cable bridges, sealed penetrations and equipment-room structure/enclosure. Inspect concealed earthing/ducts, reinforcement/inserts, water/weather protection and pull routes before backfill/closure.

Provide civil handover by equipment site: dry clean access, foundations and anchors surveyed, ducts cleaned/proved, load capacities and penetration rules, drainage, barriers, lighting and isolation boundaries. Grid energisation, BMS commissioning, signalling proving and fire acceptance are separate system gates. Protect battery equipment against construction dust, water, impact and unauthorized storage; supplier-specific fire/thermal data determines the permanent layout.

## Roads, access, public realm and reinstatement

Create staged road/pedestrian plans for piling, deliveries and lifting, with emergency routes and local business/property access. Use booked off-peak windows only where approved; stop work if the approved segregation or access cannot be maintained. Plan diversions, crossing marshals and barrier/lighting inspections for every shift. Retain route access through future maintenance and recovery, not only construction.

After underground and structural completion, restore utility covers, pavements, drainage, kerbs, crossings, accessible paths, lighting, street furniture and landscape to local-owner standards. Survey and inspect reinstatement, remove temporary obstructions, repair monitored damage and close waste/land records. Hand back streets and property independently of railway opening; keep defect/maintenance responsibilities and warranties explicit.
