# Viaduct Bearing And Movement Schedule

The standard twin-track arrangement erects individual spans, then connects
four spans into a short semi-continuous unit. Bearings remain replaceable.

| Support | Tracks | Bearing lines | Bearings |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Abutment/end support | 2 | 1 | 4 |
| Internal semi-continuous pier | 2 | 1 | 4 |
| Expansion-unit boundary | 2 | 2 | 8 |

Internal bearings are in one longitudinal row; adjacent unit ends retain two
independent rows at an expansion boundary. The supplier schedule must
assign fixed, guided, and free directions for every span end and show design
reactions, translations, rotations, uplift restraint if required, replacement
loads, installation temperature, and identification marks. Permanent jacking
shelves and a checked load path are mandatory.

The 25 m reference places a deck gap every four spans, nominally 100 m; the
permitted four/five-span family stays within 80–125 m. Internal connections use
a released diaphragm or link slab. The project schedule must close thermal,
creep, shrinkage, seismic, braking, bearing-friction, connection fatigue and
differential-settlement actions; provide covers over walkways and cable
routes; and complete continuous-welded-rail/bridge interaction. Rail expansion
devices are installed only where that analysis requires them.
