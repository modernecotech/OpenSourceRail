# Viaduct Bearing And Movement Schedule

The simply supported twin-track arrangement has two web bearings under every
girder end.

| Support | Tracks | Adjoining ends | Bearings |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Abutment/end support | 2 | 1 | 4 |
| Interior pier | 2 | 2 | 8 |

Interior bearings are in two longitudinal rows. The supplier schedule must
assign fixed, guided, and free directions for every span end and show design
reactions, translations, rotations, uplift restraint if required, replacement
loads, installation temperature, and identification marks. Permanent jacking
shelves and a checked load path are mandatory.

There is a deck gap at every simply supported span end. The former generic
"expansion joint every 60 m" rule is withdrawn. The project schedule must
instead close thermal, creep, shrinkage, seismic, braking, bearing-friction,
and differential-settlement movements at every gap; provide cover plates over
walkways and cable routes; and complete continuous-welded-rail/bridge
interaction. Rail expansion devices are installed only where that analysis
requires them.
