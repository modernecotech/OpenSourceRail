# Baghdad cost and demand review — 5 October 2026

**Controlled planning basis; construction prices, surveyed demand and committed financing remain unresolved.**

The monetary model now excludes route-search curve penalties. Local curvature changes determine construction-product boundaries, while tangent length cannot spread a remote bend's penalty. This correction preserves Baghdad's controlled 479.0 km alignment, 186 station records, 772 six-car trainsets and nine full-fleet depots.

The lower allowance removes a pricing artifact. It is not a proven saving or a complete installed budget. Special structures, actual foundations, installed grid and charging upgrades, land, utilities and supplier qualification still require quantities and quotations. Maintenance is partly a capital-percentage proxy; its reduction does not demonstrate fewer physical maintenance tasks. Staffing, payroll and traction duty are retained. The [source-bound reconciliation](../cities/catalogue/west-asia/Iraq/Baghdad/engineering/cost-reconciliation/README.md) retains the original reviewed files for offline reproduction.

The reference allocates USD 1.983bn to modelled imported invoices. This includes origin shares inherited from benchmark allowances, rather than verified import quantities or supplier commitments. Government USD cash and Chinese USD credit each cover half, approximately USD 0.991bn. Other credit, bonds and cash are denominated in Iraqi dinars. Government's total capital contribution remains 25%. Imported invoices represent 23.85% of the corrected base allowance: the share rises when an inflated local-cost denominator is removed, even though modelled absolute imported exposure falls. These are uncommitted model allocations. Neither exchange-rate risk nor an outstanding IQD liability disappears through currency labelling.

Month 101, annualised from one nominal model month after the revenue ramp, gives USD 652.6m of fares and existing commercial receipts, USD 25.0m of additional assumed receipts, and USD 519.1m OPEX. The reference operating balance before debt service is USD 158.6m. This is not a calendar-year forecast or evidence of debt repayment: terminal debt remains, no debt-clearance date is recorded, and the financing case is not closed.

Integrated fares must charge a passenger journey once. The service-capacity reference assumes one boarding per paid journey and is a zero-transfer upper bound. Keeping trains, operating costs and non-fare receipts unchanged gives:

The [demand handoff](../cities/catalogue/west-asia/Iraq/Baghdad/engineering/demand-bridge/README.md) validates unique origin–destination cohorts, declared transfers and explicit tariffs, but adopts no surveyed forecast without evidence. Jobs, station entrances, pedestrian routes, route choice and peak section loads remain open. The retained road export is insufficient to claim verified entrance-based walking catchments.

The [monthly financing ledger](../cities/catalogue/west-asia/Iraq/Baghdad/engineering/programme-recalculation/README.md) includes escalated fares and OPEX, commercial receipts, construction phasing, debt service and surplus sweeps. Mezzanine funding changes the liability profile; it does not establish sustainable repayment. Unknown construction increments must be priced before selecting a funded case.

Clearance results distinguish provisional support-to-support terrain gradients from a designed rail profile. DEM noise attribution, surveyed vertical alignment, building heights, foundation access and property rights remain unresolved. Event counts are not unique buildings, confirmed defects or procurement quantities. Depot launch and charging capacity remain separate acceptance gates. See the [clearance screen](../cities/catalogue/west-asia/Iraq/Baghdad/engineering/clearance/README.md) and [civil allowance boundary](finance/civil-allowance-boundary.md).

Across the catalogue, disconnected line networks receive candidate transfer links for survey, without invented walking paths or new accepted connections. Current city simulations and package checks bind their actual inputs and executable evidence; planning completeness does not release a physical design.
