# Choosing a railway for a corridor

Start with the busiest hour in each direction, the journey time and waiting time passengers need, the accessible boarding arrangement and the streets available for construction. Compare trains, power and civil works against that same brief. A comparison should identify the cheapest offer that meets those requirements, or tell the buyer which missing price or test could change the decision.

The current Airport case traces a 17.46 km horizontal alignment through demand, terminal capacity, moving power, unequal axle loads, civil quantities and purchaser cash. The linked paired-supply budgetary enquiry carries the evaluated duty and remaining price gaps. [Audit results and budgetary enquiry](../cities/Baghdad/engineering/simulation/AUDIT-RESULTS.md) · [Evidence needed for each decision](../cities/Baghdad/engineering/simulation/ASSUMPTION-AUDIT.md)

**What went wrong in the earlier comparisons.** The network estimator fixed a span and pier arrangement, picked the lowest priced supply case, then ran the civil comparisons using that supply. Its published summary called the associated equipment “selected”. The separate 15-case integrated catalogue assessed every longer span with a six-car steel train and radial supply, and every three-car material option with a 10 m span and paired supply. Zero of its fifteen cases cleared all decision holds. These calculations answered narrower questions than their labels suggested. [Network comparison at the reviewed revision](https://github.com/modernecotech/OpenSourceRail/blob/7b047241d4/engineering/outline/baghdad_system.py#L855-L865) · [Published comparisons](../cities/Baghdad/engineering/simulation/SIMULATION.md)

**Keep three kinds of decision visible.** Safety, accessible use and passenger protection govern every option. Local manufacture and repair, low lifetime cost, elevated construction and catenary-free supply guide the design. Formation, capacity policy, speed, equipment, station arrangement and construction method need corridor-specific choices. The development vehicle has six 17 m wheel-supported cars, 44 fixed seats and two wheelchair places per car, one boarding side and an independently sized service battery. Its passive aluminium propulsion interface leaves the installed drive open. The synthetic corridor provides a repeatable software comparison. The city opening provides drawings and supplier schedules. Both examples remain accessible through their own guides. ## Does fixing one choice change the apparent winner?

An independent replay of the original **148.2 km first programme** tested all four supply options against each of its four priced spans and both pier arrangements. It held seven lines, 64 station allowances, 24.67 million annual train-km and 242 trainsets fixed. Protected conductor has the lowest modelled 30-year present cost across all eight civil layouts. Its lead over overhead is only **$51.62m–$52.54m** on a railway with about $12bn of priced lifecycle costs. Overhead serves as a conventional comparator. A change in the price or lifetime of a line-wide asset could reverse the order. All 32 cases retain open physical acceptance.

| Span and piers | Protected conductor | Overhead | Partial conductor and battery | Station charging and battery |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 10 m separate | 12,021 | 12,073 | 12,080 | 12,703 |
| 10 m shared | 12,099 | 12,152 | 12,159 | 12,783 |
| 20 m separate | 11,864 | 11,916 | 11,921 | 12,543 |
| 20 m shared | 11,903 | 11,955 | 11,960 | 12,583 |
| 25 m separate | 11,833 | 11,884 | 11,889 | 12,511 |
| 25 m shared | 11,864 | 11,916 | 11,921 | 12,543 |
| 30 m separate | 11,812 | 11,864 | 11,868 | 12,490 |
| 30 m shared | 11,838 | 11,890 | 11,895 | 12,517 |

Values are USD millions, rounded, on the original common planning basis. At 20 m the priced shared crosshead increases the protected-conductor total by $39.22m over separate piers. Site-specific avoided utility moves and foundations would have to cover that difference after pricing the wider head and revised footings. Longer-span sizing, crane duty and local site works can also change the order. The 12 km moving-load simulation has a different service, supply topology and pricing scope.

## Which assumptions could change the purchase?

The seven-line comparison carries a fixed passenger offer through 32 combinations. Its input file sets the route, peak frequency and fleet. Its power and civil models then calculate equipment requests from that brief. The JSON output labels each reviewed input by its TOML key, separates calculated limits from procurement assumptions, and returns the reasons all 32 cases remain outside selection. [Scenario inputs](../cities/Baghdad/system.toml) · [Decision evidence and sensitivities](../cities/Baghdad/engineering/network_option_audit.py)

| Decision | What the seven-line model actually uses | What would change the decision |
|---|---|---|
| Service and fleet | 700 places at 24 trains/h yield 16,800 offered places per hour in each peak direction per group. At 38 km/h commercial speed and three minutes at each terminus, rounding the cycle on each of seven lines needs at least 208 active sets. The input allows 210 active and 242 total. | Rerun the cycle with observed peak loads, dwell distributions, terminal occupation, route-specific speeds and workshop outages. Two sets above this lower bound say little about whether peak service can recover from delays. [Hourly offer and fleet calculation](../engineering/outline/baghdad_system.py) |
| Train and power | The four supply cases produce 201.09–205.09 t loaded, 5.00–5.10 MW requested traction rating per train and 5.05–5.95 MW provisional capacity per grid site. The last figure assumes 30 sites. Under equal axle loads, 2% grade, 0.6 m/s² acceleration and the stipulated 15% adhesion margin require friction of at least 0.0957 with all axles driven. The planning trial uses 0.10. | Test wheel loads and local wet adhesion, then change acceleration, driven axles, grade or supply design together. The 1.5 kV current calculation, including a 90% voltage trial, has no moving feeder, return or fault-clearance solution for these seven lines. Reevaluate the timetable after limiting current. [Traction, storage and current calculation](../engineering/outline/baghdad_power.py) |
| Factory and erection | At eight beams per yard and eight lifts per front each workday, the monthly schedule needs six yards and six erection fronts at 10 m, three of each at 20 or 25 m and two of each at 30 m. It uses 250 workdays/year, a one-month release lag and a three-year civil window. Extra yard and front costs are unknown. | Bid machine and mould cycle, release strength, crane capacity at radius, deliveries and traffic shifts per member. A longer girder changes the eight-lift assumption itself. Rerun the resource schedule and price the resulting sites and shifts. [Production and delivery model](../engineering/outline/baghdad_viaduct.py) |
| Finance and renewals | Stationary buffers have a 12-year life and traction batteries a 10-year life in the input. At 10 m with separate supports, partial conductor costs $6.62m more than overhead at a 4% real discount rate. At 8% it costs $34.51m less. Both calculations use identical quantities and renewal dates. | Ask for warranted life at the stated duty, energy tariffs and replacement prices. Recalculate the year-by-year cash requirement with the buyer's finance terms, service opening dates and demand. These discount trials hold capital at full-service start and exclude borrowing and fare income. [Renewal and full-service cost calculation](../engineering/outline/baghdad_system.py) · [Phased service finance](../cities/Baghdad/phased_finance.py) |

The measured or independently supported limits needed to select equipment include protected stopping, safe boarding and egress, structural integrity, actual clearances and thermal capacity. These obligations follow the candidate through redesign. A buyer can change service targets, bid returns can change costs, and a different crane can change the erection method. Exceeding a trial crane chart identifies the need for a new lift study. The original 10/20/25/30 m list records the lengths that estimator can price. ## Run the comparison with your own assumptions

| What is known locally? | How to use it |
|---|---|
| Passenger brief and operating plan | In the [Workbench](workbench/current/index.html), set operating hours, target and assumed availability. Open **Change service assumptions and search values** to enter trial speeds, stopping rates, dwell and headways. A batch reports how many combinations remain. Keep the same brief and resume at the indicated index to test those combinations. |
| Crane, soil and alignment | `./osr design railway spans --route-km 18 --soil-kpa 175` prices the trial sections. Enter a contractor’s working radius with `--lift-radius-m` and its chart capacity there with `--crane-limit-t`. The 12 m cost bands cover reference hire and haul. Use `--erection-offer offer.json` for each contractor’s actual rates and productivity on a particular design. Until a matching offer arrives, the buyer can still see the priced parts of the viaduct. [Manufacturer example of configuration-dependent capacity](https://www.liebherr.com/en-int/mobile-and-crawler-cranes/mobile-cranes/mobile-crane-technology/variobase-5387323) · [Viaduct comparison](../cities/Baghdad/engineering/mechanical/civil/SPAN-FAMILIES.md) |
| Traction supply and grid connection | `./osr design railway feeder --help` gives the modeled layouts and editable electrical inputs. A full train duty and a spacing calculation answer different questions. Protection coordination, safe isolation and installed ratings need evidence from the specified equipment. [Feeder assumptions and examples](../cities/Baghdad/engineering/simulation/FEEDER-OPTIONS.md) |
| Precaster's offered capacity | `./osr design railway yard --beams 80 --moulds 8 --cycle-hours 24` schedules the issued box casting. Enter the supplier’s actual staffing, output, yield, cycle and price. The finish date includes the last mould release. [Workcell offers](../cities/Baghdad/engineering/prototype/YARD-OFFERS.md) |

## When to select

Keep a design in the engineering comparison while it has an unresolved mandatory function. Publish its known cost and failed cases so a designer can see the value of correcting it. For each surviving combination, collect the same delivered scope from suppliers, compare installed and discounted lifecycle cost under the same passenger offer, and repeat the affected checks when an input changes. A cheap indicative total, a familiar precedent or a passed software test gives a reason to investigate. Selection requires an identified configuration and evidence for its governing limits. Fabrication and passenger operation have separate acceptance decisions.
