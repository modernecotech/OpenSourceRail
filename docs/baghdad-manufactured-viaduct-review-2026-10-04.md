# Baghdad manufactured viaduct and cost review

The two supplied reviews cover a manufactured-viaduct proposal and the older
`135e249e` cost baseline. The latter predates the [integrated delivery and
financing follow-up](baghdad-ci-controls-review-2026-10-04.md). Its USD 7.88bn
is retained as a historical planning comparator; it is not a complete budget.

The [new comparison package](../cities/catalogue/west-asia/Iraq/Baghdad/engineering/viaduct-comparison/README.md)
provides Pi20 and Pi25 with both structural-continuity and simple-span/link-slab
schemes, plus an OSR-US constrained-access alternative. All use the same
complete double-track comparison scope. The longest current Pi25 run is an
investigation candidate; the separate 1 km study is not a surveyed alignment
or selected project. No 30 m product or literature-based saving is introduced.

The civil load seed now distinguishes the 12-axle LM3 from Baghdad's six-car,
24-axle consist. Its 111 m length and tare/passenger masses reconcile with the
controlled rolling-stock profile. Supplier axle positions and loaded axle
distribution remain absent; repeating LM3 spacing would invent geometry.
The 16 t infrastructure allowance must be applied with the complete loading
pattern and adopted dynamic/combination rules.

The LM3 seed's passenger masses now use the same explicit 75 kg/person planning
assumption: its previous AW2/AW3 masses silently used 60 kg. Reference axle
geometry and both passenger-mass assumptions remain subject to deployment
design acceptance; no physical load case is released by this correction.

Catalogue code now distinguishes a structural continuity connection from a
link slab. A link slab retains eight bearings at internal twin-track piers;
four shared bearings require the structural connection's staged load path.
The finite support register includes both ends and reconciles with the shared
catalogue function. Connections, CWR, thermal/braking/seismic/foundation forces,
fatigue, replacement access and prices remain design/check releases. The
existing lower civil rate is explicitly conditional on the unaccepted
structural arrangement.

The comparison separates the standard allowance and routing penalty for every
elevated segment, with an individual form for each exceptional alignment.
Alternatives retain survey, wider curves, station movements, rights-of-way,
segmental, utility/land, traffic and whole-life costs as unresolved fields.
Removing a routing penalty is not an achieved saving. Existing capital and
financing scenarios are preserved. A separate bearing-index cashflow sensitivity
prices only existing standard Pi25 length, adds incremental EPC once and
recalculates monthly/six-month funding, reserves and repayments. Government
remains 25%; assumed imports retain 50% government USD / 50% Chinese USD credit,
and other funding remains IQD. Invoice dates and origin shares are inherited
assumptions; segmental/special designs receive no Pi25 bearing quantity or price
credit. Connection and consequential costs remain unpriced.

Installed-price forms include quantities, invoice currency, price dates,
origin, exclusions, supplier references and credit eligibility. Foundations
need actual support ground zones and deep-element lengths. Unknown costs
cannot produce a total. Ground cranes/portals and overhead launchers require
configured proposals, complete member/rigging masses, route and support checks,
temporary/deck loads and recovery plans. Pi25 and Pi20 have almost no bare-mass
margin within their respective product targets.

The production model uses all eight fronts through survey/correction and final
acceptance to calculate the slowest complete double-track bay rate. Missing
cycles or resources keep that rate unknown. The existing casting cycle and
10–15-bay buffer remain planning assumptions pending measured trials. First
article beam, pier/cap/connection, transport, erection and finished bay hold
points provide the required measurement scope; no trial has been performed.
UHPC connections and special composite crossings remain bounded alternatives,
without carbon percentages being converted to cost savings.

The older review's depot/storage, hourly/site energy, six-car allocations,
workforce, demand/fare sensitivities, rental alternatives and bootstrap work
already have [controlled outputs](../cities/catalogue/west-asia/Iraq/Baghdad/engineering/delivery-closure/README.md).
Supplier-backed prices, site design, demand counts and financing commitments
remain open. The artifact policy now names Baghdad's proposal/archive and
distinguishes current, historical and diagnostic evidence. The front-page
README includes **nobody should be priced out of dignity**.
