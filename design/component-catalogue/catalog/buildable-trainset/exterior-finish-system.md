# LM3 simplified exterior finish system

Generated by `tools/automation/buildable-trainset.sh`. This controls the
separation between mandatory base protection, replaceable livery film and
the trial-only calcium-carbonate radiative roof coating.

- Design ID: `LM3-FIN-260`
- Status: `design-reference; CaCO3 roof route is trial-only`
- Principle: film simplifies livery but never replaces corrosion, composite-surface or fire protection
- Film procurement boundary: rail-application product selected by current supplier bulletin and project fire/adhesion evidence
- CaCO3 research screen: initial solar reflectance `0.955`; sky-window emissivity `0.94`. research screening targets only; measure the selected LM3 system new and aged

## Controlled route

| Seq. | Operation | Hold point |
|---:|---|---|
| 10 | release corrosion, fire and composite base finish before decorative work | base finish records accepted |
| 20 | survey, repair, fully cure and clean each film/coating substrate | signed pre-installation inspection |
| 30 | cut film by module/bay and make film plus roof-coating witness coupons | coupon adhesion and appearance accepted |
| 40 | dry-apply livery film; mask roof keep-outs and apply qualified candidate coating | edges, overlaps, mask map and wet/dry film record accepted |
| 50 | inspect, water/wash test, retain coupons and complete removal/repair demonstration | new-build release or trial-only disposition signed |
| 60 | age coupons and one-car trial through UV, wash, abrasion and service exposure | aged optical/adhesion and maintenance evidence reviewed |

The CaCO3 values come from the Purdue research paper linked in
[`exterior-finish-process.md`](../../../../docs/rolling-stock/light-metro-3car/exterior-finish-process.md).
They are screening targets, not LM3 performance claims.
