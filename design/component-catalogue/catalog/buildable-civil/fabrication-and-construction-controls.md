# Civil Fabrication and Construction Reference Controls

> Status: **reference defaults — not issued for fabrication or construction**.

This workface handbook fills repeatable moulding, inspection, handling, erection and
handover gaps around the reusable civil kit. It deliberately leaves site actions,
ground, reinforcement/prestress, temporary works and statutory release with the
competent project organisations.

## Workface release rule

No work starts from coordination geometry alone: released drawings/method, accepted inputs, competent people/plant and open-hold-point authorization must all identify the same asset and revision.

Planning/RFQ definition only. Fabrication or construction release requires project survey, geotechnical model, checked calculations, reinforcement/prestress design, supplier data, temporary-works design and signed engineering acceptance.

## CIV-MFG-010 — Survey, Datums And Set-Out

Default sequence:

1. establish protected project control outside the work zone.
2. transfer primary control independently to factory bed or work front.
3. set mould, foundation, bearing and rail-seat datums from primary control.
4. record as-built coordinates before the work is concealed or loaded.

Planning default: Use one project grid and vertical datum; close each set-out from two independent control points. Survey all first-article interfaces and every foundation, bearing seat, rail seat, platform edge and movement joint.

Typical first-shift crew/plant: two-person survey crew, total station, digital level and traceable check artefact. This is a resource-planning allowance, not authorisation or a minimum safe crew.

Hold the work when: control closure fails, benchmark disturbed, design/survey revision mismatch, or required accuracy is absent from the released survey plan.

Required handback: accepted survey control report, set-out sheets and signed as-built survey.

## CIV-MFG-020 — Precast Mould, Reinforcement And Inserts

Default sequence:

1. clean and survey mould/bed datums.
2. apply release system and fit cages, ducts, drains, lifting anchors and inserts.
3. independently check cover, restraint and insert templates.
4. photograph and sign the pre-pour hold point.

Planning default: Use rigid reusable steel moulds with replaceable end boxes and positive insert templates. First mould and every repaired/reset mould receive a full dimensional survey; subsequent pours receive datum, closure and insert checks before each pour.

Typical first-shift crew/plant: mould/carpentry 2–3, reinforcement 4–6, one independent inspector, mould survey kit and cover/insert gauges. This is a resource-planning allowance, not authorisation or a minimum safe crew.

Hold the work when: unreleased reinforcement/prestress drawing, unidentified steel/insert, cover failure, dirty duct, loose insert, mould outside drawing tolerance, or unsigned pre-pour checklist.

Required handback: mould acceptance, material lots, cage/insert/cover report and signed pre-pour record.

## CIV-MFG-030 — Concrete Placement, Curing And Prestress Transfer

Default sequence:

1. accept batch and fresh-concrete tests.
2. place in the released sequence without displacing cages or ducts.
3. finish and cure with temperature/maturity records.
4. confirm specified release/transfer strength before demoulding or prestress transfer.

Planning default: Reject site water addition unless the approved mix procedure permits and records it. Concrete class, exposure, w/c ratio, workability window, test frequency, curing cycle, release strength and prestress sequence remain calculation/specification values—not catalogue defaults.

Typical first-shift crew/plant: placement 6–8 plus pump/crane operator, finishing 2–3, laboratory technician and independent inspector at hold points. This is a resource-planning allowance, not authorisation or a minimum safe crew.

Hold the work when: batch ticket mismatch, workability/temperature outside specification, interrupted placement beyond permitted joint time, curing excursion, failed strength result, or prestress anomaly.

Required handback: batch/test records, curing history, transfer/release strength, prestress elongation/force record and approved deviation/NCR closure.

## CIV-MFG-040 — Demould, Dimensional Acceptance And Storage

Default sequence:

1. confirm release strength and approved lifting arrangement.
2. demould without prying at unapproved points.
3. inspect surfaces, cracks, geometry, inserts, ducts and bearing/rail interfaces.
4. mark unique ID, orientation, mass and approved support points before storage.

Planning default: Inspect 100% of the first three units of each mould/type and every safety/interface feature thereafter. Routine non-interface dimensions may move to first/last plus 10% minimum three only through an approved control plan. Support units at the drawing-defined points on level, verified bearers.

Typical first-shift crew/plant: lift supervisor, certified crane operator, four riggers, two survey/inspection staff and rated lifting frame. This is a resource-planning allowance, not authorisation or a minimum safe crew.

Hold the work when: strength not accepted, crack/damage beyond acceptance limits, interface out of tolerance, blocked duct/drain, unidentified unit, or storage support not verified.

Required handback: dimensional/crack report, concrete and lift records, traceability mark and storage inspection.

## CIV-MFG-050 — Transport And Delivery Acceptance

Default sequence:

1. match unit, mass and centre of gravity to transport schedule.
2. inspect route, trailer, temporary supports and restraints.
3. load only from approved points and record restraint installation.
4. inspect and survey interfaces again after delivery.

Planning default: Use the approved transport orientation and support spacing; do not support on bearing seats, walkway edges, drains or rail plinths. The route survey, permits, dynamic allowances and temporary-state calculation are deployment-specific.

Typical first-shift crew/plant: transport supervisor, certified operator, four riggers, escort/traffic staff as permitted, inspected trailer and rated lifting equipment. This is a resource-planning allowance, not authorisation or a minimum safe crew.

Hold the work when: route/permit absent, actual unit mass exceeds schedule, support/restraint mismatch, weather beyond method limits, transport damage, or delivery survey failure.

Required handback: route/permit pack, checked transport calculation, pre/post condition records and delivery acceptance.

## CIV-MFG-060 — Foundation, Pier And Cap Construction

Default sequence:

1. verify utilities, ground horizon and formation.
2. construct/test foundation and survey starter interface.
3. erect or cast column with temporary stability maintained.
4. install cap, closure/grout and bearing-seat survey.

Planning default: Release one work front at a time from accepted survey, utility and geotechnical zone data. Never extrapolate a foundation selection across an uninvestigated ground boundary. Keep column/cap temporary stability independent of incomplete permanent connections.

Typical first-shift crew/plant: site engineer, supervisor, survey pair, foundation crew sized to selected method, 4–6 erection riggers and independent quality hold-point inspector. This is a resource-planning allowance, not authorisation or a minimum safe crew.

Hold the work when: unexpected ground/water/utility, foundation test failure, starter or bearing-seat survey failure, uncontrolled temporary state, grout/material mismatch, or open structural NCR.

Required handback: ground log, foundation tests, concrete/grout records, temporary-works inspection and pier/cap as-built survey.

## CIV-MFG-070 — Girder Erection, Bearings And Closures

Default sequence:

1. accept bearing seats, bearings, unit and erection plant.
2. set bearing orientation/temperature and install temporary restraints.
3. lift or launch to the released sequence and survey before de-rigging.
4. complete diaphragms/closures and remove temporary restraints only at the authorised stage.

Planning default: Plan one lift controller, one command channel and a controlled exclusion zone. Set each bearing by ID and orientation; protect movement surfaces and retain access for inspection/replacement. No person enters beneath a suspended or incompletely restrained unit.

Typical first-shift crew/plant: lift supervisor, crane/launcher operator(s), four riggers, survey pair, two closure/grout workers and safety lead; plant capacity follows the checked lift study. This is a resource-planning allowance, not authorisation or a minimum safe crew.

Hold the work when: seat/bearing/unit not accepted, wind or visibility beyond method limits, plant configuration mismatch, loss of communication, unplanned movement, survey failure, or incomplete restraint.

Required handback: checked erection/lift plan, plant and personnel certificates, bearing schedule, lift record, closure strength and final survey.

## CIV-MFG-080 — At-Grade Formation, Slab And Transition

Default sequence:

1. prove utilities, drainage outfall and formation level.
2. place and test ground treatment/subbase by lot.
3. set rail-seat/duct/drain templates and sign pre-pour hold.
4. cast/cure, survey and trial the first complete transition panel.

Planning default: Build a full-width trial panel including drainage, ducts and rail-seat fixings before repetitive work. Divide earthworks and pavement testing into traceable lots no larger than one shift or one material/source change; the project specification sets test values and lot size if smaller.

Typical first-shift crew/plant: site engineer, survey pair, earthworks 5–8 plus selected plant, concrete 6–8, laboratory technician and track-interface inspector. This is a resource-planning allowance, not authorisation or a minimum safe crew.

Hold the work when: soft spot or groundwater differs from model, drainage unavailable, compaction/plate test failure, template movement, concrete/cure failure, or transition survey outside released limits.

Required handback: formation lot tests, drainage proof, pre-pour record, concrete tests, trial-panel acceptance and as-built survey.

## CIV-MFG-090 — Track, Platform And Systems Interface Handover

Default sequence:

1. clean and survey rail-seat/plinth, platform and equipment interfaces.
2. install accepted fastening/rail/turnout products to supplier procedures.
3. measure gauge, alignment, cant, stepping, clearances, drainage and electrical continuity.
4. hand over an as-built interface model with all NCRs and temporary conditions visible.

Planning default: Use a common chainage and asset ID across survey, IFC, inspection and maintenance records. Perform 100% measurement at turnouts, movement joints, stations, transitions and first article; routine interval and acceptance values come from the released track/vehicle/interface plan.

Typical first-shift crew/plant: permanent-way supervisor, 6–10 track workers, survey pair, signalling/power representatives, calibrated track gauge and clearance gauge. This is a resource-planning allowance, not authorisation or a minimum safe crew.

Hold the work when: supplier revision mismatch, missing vehicle envelope, failed gauge/cant/stepping/continuity, obstructed drainage/access, or interface NCR not accepted by both owners.

Required handback: supplier installation records, track/clearance survey, signed civil-track-station-vehicle ICD and configuration handover.

## CIV-MFG-100 — Nonconformance, Repair And Work-Front Handback

Default sequence:

1. identify, contain and preserve the original evidence.
2. assess structural, durability, geometry and downstream interface effects.
3. obtain authorised use-as-is, rework, repair, return/scrap or design-change disposition.
4. perform repair and repeat every invalidated inspection before handback.

Planning default: No field cutting, drilling, heat straightening, reinforcement/strand exposure, concrete repair, bearing adjustment, grout substitution or tolerance concession without a written disposition by the accountable designer and checker where required.

Typical first-shift crew/plant: responsible construction engineer, quality lead, accountable designer and independent checker according to consequence; repair team only after disposition. This is a resource-planning allowance, not authorisation or a minimum safe crew.

Hold the work when: unapproved repair, repeat defect trend, hidden defect extent, missing retest, configuration mismatch, or any open safety/structural NCR.

Required handback: NCR, engineering assessment, approved repair method, repeat-test/survey result, as-built update and signed handback.
