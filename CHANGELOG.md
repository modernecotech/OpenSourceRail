# OpenSourceRail changelog

## 0.5.0 development — 20 October 2026

- Select ferrite PM-biased active maglev with fixed onboard magnetic modules,
  powered linear propulsion, passive reaction iron and one narrow concrete
  guidebeam per direction. Keep passenger, cooling, protection and recovery functions.
- Remove obsolete physical design generators, configurations, cost dependencies,
  outputs and publication paths from the current checkout. Earlier repository
  revisions remain available through Git history.
- Define 45 current system responsibilities and 11 work packages with manufacturing
  and verification routes, 72 core requirements and seven evacuation requirements.
  Detailed part breakdowns and all engineering acceptance gates remain open.
- Price formations from per-car quantities, coupling interfaces and one shared
  control/protection set per train. Calculate ordinary guideway allowances from
  concrete, reinforcement, reaction steel, supports, transport, lifts and site work.
  Rates are unquoted project assumptions; complete city programmes remain unpriced.
- Compile city geography and service targets into maglev formations, route duty,
  fleet, charging and manufacturing quantities. Baghdad uses six nominal 17 m cars.
  Site, force-map, supplier and operating acceptance remain required.
- Retain source-bound native CAD/STEP, numerical studies, Rust control and egress
  tests, renders and assembly visibility videos. Visibility sequences are not
  collision or motion simulations; offline control tests do not enable physical outputs.
- Provide current architecture CLI, workbench, reproducible dependency runner and
  a source-change background timer. Unavailable evidence holds dependent outputs.

This version is an architectural development milestone. It does not claim a
complete railway design, manufacturing release or operational authorisation.
