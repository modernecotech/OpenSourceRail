# City railway designs and maps

Explore **266 city network outlines**. Each city dossier brings together its visible network map, line and station tables, service and fleet, civil and factory quantities, workforce, energy accounting, capital and whole-life costs, finance scenarios, site requirements and downloads.

The figures use the exact selected calculated outline and explicit Class C estimates. A complete outline supports comparison and development; engineering verification and manufacturing or operating approval remain separate.
