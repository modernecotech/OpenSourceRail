# Task-derived maintenance and reserve cash

[Task workload](maintenance-tasks.csv) covers daily/weekly/monthly inspections, every-roundtrip safety/diagnostic checks, wheel reprofile at 150,000km, bogie overhaul at 600,000km and body overhaul at ten years. Six-car quantities are 24 wheelsets and 12 bogies/train. Reference workload is 350,192 mechanical and 187,369 electrical person-hours/year, compared with existing workload allowances 370,560 / 247,040. Durations, defects, access queues, equipment, parts and non-fleet tasks remain unmeasured; lower reference hours do not authorise staff reductions.

Battery replacement stays on the existing **12-year** cycle. Reference annual reserve is USD 17.370m within the existing USD 51.878m rolling maintenance envelope. [Monthly reserve ledger](battery-reserve-monthly.csv) separates indexed contributions, replacement withdrawals, required top-ups and closing restricted cash. At 5% inflation/zero reserve interest, reference top-ups total USD 309.273m nominal; terminal restricted cash is USD 618.979m. It cannot repay loans/bonds or become terminal sale income. The replacement bill is not added to annual reserve spending a second time.

Initial 2% battery-spare stock is USD 4.169m reference; existing allowance inclusion is unknown. Serial-specific service/mileage/cell-health dates must replace line-opening proxies. Native Asset Maintenance/Asset Repair, calibration/parts and independent handback records remain required. No actual asset, spare, Work Order or accepted Job Card is invented.

[All 26 controlled interval records](maintenance-interval-register.json) also retain station, civil/track/structures, energy, wayside, tooling, finish/joint/wash and soiling work. Condition-based tasks have no invented frequency or labour cost. [Opening-fleet reserve cash](opening-battery-reserve-monthly.csv) uses 425 trainsets and adds its own indexed shortfalls to the reduced-supply financing case.
