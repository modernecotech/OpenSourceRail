Airport opening

Three six-car trains provide a departure every 15 minutes in each direction. With three spares the fleet totals six sets. The central construction allowance is $1,122.8m. The largest design change is the protected-conductor supply needed during a site outage.

Operating plan

144 passenger trips, 2 empty mainline trips and 6 depot moves run each day. One working platform face and one exclusive throat per terminus accommodate the booked movements. The tightest resource has 150 seconds clear. The timetable provides 2,800 places per hour per direction. The common demand profile implies 1,158 boardings across both directions in the busiest hour. Urban opening comparison

Yellow is the urban survey comparator because its named stops connect with the most other proposed corridors. At four trains an hour on both lines, the common-rate urban scope is $1,139.0m against $624.5m for Airport, before corridor-specific feeder correction and site work. At the same net fare it would need 50,065 additional paid journeys a day to match Airport operating support under those common assumptions.
