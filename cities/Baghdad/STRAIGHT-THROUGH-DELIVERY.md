## Service, electricity and workers

The fixed 6-minute offer over 18 hours/day uses 505 running trainsets and 76 reserve, or 3,486 cars across 8 depot allowances. The model produces 103.17 million train-km/year, 2,192.9 GWh/year and 241 nominal grid supply sites. Each line offers 7,000.0 nominal places per hour in each direction.

The standard-stock comparison uses 3 active paired launcher crews, 2 road teams and 8 concurrent yards. Each nominal yard uses 3.54 ha and 16 mould positions. There are 128 concurrent moulds on 28.3 ha. Rail-fed jobs reserve a yard and a mobile front at each end.
