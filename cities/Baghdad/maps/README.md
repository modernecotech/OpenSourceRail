# Baghdad railway maps

View the [seven-corridor alternative](Baghdad-148km.png) and [independent citywide alternative](Baghdad-606km.png) maps, or open either GeoJSON file below in a GIS viewer. The maps place the routes over the retained Baghdad [city background](../../catalogue/west-asia/Iraq/Baghdad/map-context.json.gz): streets, water, land use and place names from OpenStreetMap, Natural Earth and Protomaps. The routes show where the railway is being planned; they are not surveyed track centre lines. Both concepts use elevated double track, no tunnels and a protected conductor rail for traction power.

| Map | Declared route length | Station allowance | What it shows |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| [First programme](network-148km.geojson) | 148.2 km | 64 | Green, Red, Blue, Purple, Yellow, White and Airport lines; named places and priority access points |
| [Independent citywide network](network-606km.geojson) | 605.652 km | 280 | Nine principal corridors, five reserved links, six infill corridors, six access branches and stop screens |

The two route layers show independent concepts. The citywide model assigns the airport approach to M05, Sadr City approaches to M04 and M09, and Jadriya approaches to M01 and M02. The [junction screen](../system-results.json) identifies gaps at both M04 approaches. The other approaches touch their drawn main lines within the map tolerance. Track junctions, stop locations, clearances and pier positions remain design work. The six pale priority areas show a 1 km straight-line radius around the airport terminal, three Sadr City points and two Jadriya points. These circles help compare geographic proximity. Walking access depends on streets, river crossings and property boundaries.

The airport terminal point is the centroid of [OpenStreetMap way 28647537](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/28647537), not the terminal entrance. Other named places retain their feature-level OpenStreetMap links. Sadr City and Jadriya points are the existing WorldPop radial population-screen anchors recorded in the map data. The [waypoint source](rfi-waypoints.json) retains first-programme names and nominal line lengths. The larger map retains its source geometry identity in the GeoJSON header.

Map distances and station allowances are programme inputs. The first map draws 138.395 km of schematic line against a 148.2 km declared programme; the larger map draws 608.460 km across principal lines and planning connections against a 605.652 km declared network. These drawn lengths must not be substituted for the programme controls or added to the cost model. The drawn polylines do not establish road clearances, grades, pier locations, river crossing designs, platform sites or construction permissions. The city background is from the retained 24 October 2026 Protomaps build with OpenStreetMap and Natural Earth data. [OpenStreetMap attribution and licence](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) is retained.
