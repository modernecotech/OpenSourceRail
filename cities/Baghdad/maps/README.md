# Baghdad railway maps

The active map shows three rings, four cross-Tigris trunks, the Airport and the urban gap line. Basmaya, Nahrawan and Tajiyat are within a 1.3 km street walk of through trains. The route and station GIS supplies the same geometry as the delivery plan. [Map](baghdad-metropolitan-network.png) · [Editable GIS](baghdad-metropolitan-network.geojson) · [Passenger evidence](../METROPOLITAN-NETWORK.md) · [Delivery estimate](../METROPOLITAN-DELIVERY.md)

Squares show the ends of through services and diamonds mark former termini. Orange dots are suburban spacing points. The dotted contour lies 2.5 km outside the outer ring and has no passenger service.

The layout comparison measures access, transfers and construction scope against the earlier plans. The Airport opening study uses a separate candidate and public GIS screening layers. [Layout comparison](../THREE-RING-STUDY.md) · [Airport site study](../engineering/simulation/AUDIT-RESULTS.md)

Retained maps and early route estimates supply geometry and benchmarks for the engineering tools. [Earlier map catalogue and RFI sources at the reviewed revision](https://github.com/modernecotech/OpenSourceRail/blob/f2462e112648e7dd357f3f9f20e384414458c898/cities/Baghdad/maps/README.md)
