# Baghdad metropolitan procurement

Use a single route, train formation, quantity takeoff and lifecycle boundary for all bidder returns. The live metropolitan estimate prices a six-minute offer and 30 years of operation and renewals in constant 2026 USD. Ask bidders to identify unavailable scope, route-specific constraints and price departures. [Current network and alignment](METROPOLITAN-NETWORK.md) · [Planning quantities, installed and lifecycle estimates](METROPOLITAN-DELIVERY.md)

For the current civil enquiry use the [20 m shared-pier delivery programme](METROPOLITAN-DELIVERY.md). Ask for matched deck-fed and road erection offers using the same permanent stock and support geometry. Price temporary track, starter spans, gantries, yards, flatbeds, booked shift resources and road occupation separately. Obtain separate returns for curved forms, closure pieces, bridge approaches and any foundation alternatives. [Viaduct quantities and interfaces](engineering/mechanical/civil/LIVE-STOCK.md) · [Curved beam work orders](engineering/mechanical/civil/CURVED-BEAM-PROGRAMME.md)

Request turnkey, vendor-led with technology transfer, and locally integrated offers against the same route, service, accessibility, summer duty, installation schedule, inspection points and maintenance horizon. Require a priced departures schedule and name the party responsible for each shared interface through commissioning and warranty.

| Return | Information to request |
|---|---|
| Installed and through-life price | Quantities, unit rates, installation and commissioning, replacements, software and inspection. Identify currency, price date, tax, freight, escalation and exclusions. |
| Financing and cash | Untied cash price, draw schedule, fees, currency, borrowing cost, grace, security and guarantees. Reconcile the cash schedule to installed and operating costs. |
| Iraqi production | Contract awards, domestic labour and materials, imports and untraced inputs by package and year. Calculate verified Iraqi value added on the same ex-tax, ex-contingency and ex-financing denominator. |
| Repair and design rights | Editable drawings, interface specifications, configurations, build tools, diagnostics, inspection fixtures, repair instructions, replacement lead times and licence terms. |
| Transfer and acceptance | First article, coached assembly, independent repeat build and witnessed local repair. Name the reviewer, evidence and acceptance conditions for each milestone. |

Reconcile fabrication and installation hours to the resource plan. Keep asset configuration, revision, inspection and supplier responsibility traceable from order to maintenance.
