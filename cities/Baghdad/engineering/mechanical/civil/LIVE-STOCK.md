# Baghdad metropolitan viaduct stock and delivery

The recurring main-line bay uses two **20 m precast concrete U-girders**, one per track, at **4.50 m track centres** on shared pier crossheads. The straight-through metropolitan proposal uses this family for its standard-stock budget and inward deck-fed erection on eligible open lines. The chainage register adds 2,478 straight closure pieces, 862 curved pieces and 698 short curved pieces. Curved bays use an inside and an outside girder with a shared centre of curvature and different arc lengths. The mapped fit uses a 120 m minimum inside radius, a preferred route radius of 400 m and up to 200 mm of sweep from the chord to the arc. These dimensions guide preliminary set-out. Survey, cant, transitions, rolling-stock clearance and bearing movement govern the final geometry. River spans, station transitions, junctions, tight curves and shared-alignment interfaces carry separate design and price holds. [Straight and curved work orders](CURVED-BEAM-PROGRAMME.md) · [Detailed register and section inputs](curved-beam-register.json.gz) · [Editable plan and cross-section](curved-beam-families.dxf)

At each end of an eligible open line, a terminal yard supplies one inward launching front. A flatbed carries one girder at a time on temporary rail over released deck. The gantry places the two girders onto the next shared support. The metropolitan planning calendar allows eight concurrent yards at 3.54 ha and 16 mould positions each, with 48 hours per planning mould cycle. Loops and sites without a proven launch path use a road-fed allowance. The programme still requires site-specific ground works, starter and closure bays, and evidence for loaded transport axle forces, deck reactions, transfer, rigging and temporary stability.
