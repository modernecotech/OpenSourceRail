# Baghdad Airport simulation programme

The 12 km opening has a powered-wheel, protected-conductor train and independently supported double track. Its prototype supplies six 17 m cars, 700 places, 24 axles, eight doors per car and a straight 125 m island. The study also evaluates three cars and 350 places. Car spacing, bogie pivots and doorway centres use the Airport article. Every experiment keeps its vehicle, infrastructure, service and equipment revisions together through loading, energy, installation, manufacturing and cash flow. The city-wide network and the repository's passive-wheel reference retain their own design records.

## Vehicle and civil findings

Three deterministic load allocations vary installed group weights, equipment location assumptions and passenger mass simultaneously. They bound the three scenarios examined. Weighing each equipped vehicle remains necessary. The six-car steel set gives **165.84, 198.64 and 246.04 tonnes loaded**. The aluminium-body series gives 155.14, 192.75 and 245.23 t. The hybrid gives 153.80, 191.41 and 246.84 t. A hybrid can become the heaviest option after adding joint and attachment stock. Body stock uses explicit mass multipliers over the actual cut register. Each option keeps steel bogie frames and a separate 500 kg service pack per train. The central steel car has a loaded CG 0.333 m ahead of its midpoint and 1.400 m above rail. The 201.551 t earlier crowd sensitivity is available separately. The steel sidewall's linear 1.3 gravity screen reaches 152 MPa and 14.3 mm displacement at the highest allocation. The current planar screen omits connection slip, torsion, fatigue and end loads. Alternative body stiffnesses, fire behaviour and repair joints need their own drawings.

For each selected formation the study moves point axles across both overhangs and bearing lines. It evaluates the four central-load cases and repeats the selected span at low and high loading. Stock dimensions, support inset and relative crane pick locations follow the released comparison. The frozen 12-strand cage permits direct comparison of positive bending at exactly the same prestress. The following are central six-car steel results. The native comparison applies one 85.39 kN axle at midspan to a prismatic 9.50 m simply supported gross box. CalculiX B32R meshes of 8, 16 and 32 elements give 0.1794, 0.1804 and 0.1814 mm centre displacement. The last change is 0.59%. The 32-element solution exceeds the Euler bending calculation by 14.4%. These discretisations agree within a 1% refinement target and exceed a 5% Euler cross-model target. Resolve shear flexibility, section formulation and support modelling before using displacement for clearances. Local pads, slabs, cracking, end blocks and embedded hardware require more detailed models and load trials. [CalculiX box-section definition](https://www.dhondt.de/ccx_2.20.pdf) · [Input decks, native outputs, solver hashes and logs](span-benchmark.zip)

The 156 mm secondary-seat space leaves 11, 1 or −9 mm for 145, 155 or 165 mm trial stacks before shims. The 0.6 kN/mm spring case uses 40.2 mm of a trial 40 mm payload stroke. The drawing's doorway width has 10 mm in hand centrally and 2 mm when the allocated loaded distortion doubles. The straight 75 mm platform gap needs actual sway, wheel wear, boarding equipment and platform surveying. At the specified two-hour outage and 20% energy reserve, the 65 kWh pack has 1.6 kWh available above demand when new. At 80% remaining capacity it is short by 8.8 kWh. A 1.0 m/s² stop on a 2% descent requires adhesion of 0.122 even with every axle braking. At trial adhesion 0.08, the deficit is 0.042. Heat rejection, rail-brake contribution, wheel-slide performance and protection response require measurement.
