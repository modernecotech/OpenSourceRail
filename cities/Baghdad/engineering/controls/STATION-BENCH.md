# Baghdad station visit and fault trials

Use one representative train door, a car I/O node, the protection and dispatch controllers, a brake actuator under representative load, a restrained drive and an independent position transducer. Simulate the other 47 openings at their installed addresses. Record which interfaces are real. The six-car Baghdad protected-conductor interface has four door openings on each side of each car. Repeat the trials in both train orientations and record the physical opening ID and side.

## Set up the return

Identify the candidate, station visit, article serials, wiring revision, as-tested binary digest, controller startup identity, recorder inputs and calibration. Agree timing, movement, force, allowable current and isolation limits with the responsible engineer before applying stimuli. Use the [station handover intake](README.md#review-a-recorded-brake-handover) for the ordinary departure. Record each fault trial separately with its stimulus time, expected response, measured response and disposition. Use an independent logger that continues through power interruptions.

| Trial and stimulus | Independent observations required | Conditions before recovery |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arrival and boarding. Approach under service braking, stop at the designated side, then send the bound door release. | Measure signed speed, berth displacement, parking switch, brake force, inverter inhibit and torque. Record the physical opening moving and the opposite lock contact staying closed. | Retain the hold through boarding. Verify each of the 48 lock slots after closure. |
| Departure. Request dispatch, build directed starting effort against the retained hold, release the brakes and depart. | Record closure, lock, dispatch, directed effort, release request, parking release, independent force decay and first motion on a common time axis. Measure rollback and timing against approved limits. | Exercise both departure directions. Investigate wrong-sign effort, lost effort and brake drag individually. |
| Lose a holding-brake feedback signal during boarding and during effort take-up. Drop each feedback independently. | Record the feedback loss, inhibit output, actual effort, independent brake request, parking contact, brake force and displacement. Verify cancellation of dispatch throughout the fault. | Restore genuine feedback, observe a healthy held sample, then operate the local reset key. Verify a new station cycle is required. |
| Wrong-side and stale commands. Request the other installed side, change train or visit, replay a used sequence, delay a command beyond its allowed age and remove commands entirely. | Observe both motor circuits, all relevant lock contacts, torque removal and brake demand. Measure withdrawal latency even while dispatch messages are silent. | Re-establish the bound visit, healthy status and a fresh local reset. Cross-check the addressed opening against its physical side after reversing the consist. |
| Platform evacuation. Operate a single emergency handle at a secured stop, first with the egress path unproved, then with independent authority for that opening. Repeat with authority for another opening, a blocked path and indicated motion. | Observe the nominated and opposite door motors, contact states, actual effort, emergency-brake output and retained hold. A request must suppress traction before the first lock changes. A powered exit requires a proved path for that opening, zero speed and retained hold. Record physical accessibility and assisted egress separately. | Clear the emergency request and confirm physical closure and all locks. Test that the handle or a held reset key cannot initiate departure. |
| Service-power interruption. Remove control power during boarding and again during effort take-up. After restoration, replay pre-outage messages with plausible timestamps and sequence numbers. Hold the reset key across restart. | Log downstream rail voltages, brake force, lock state, actual torque and wheel position through the blackout. Record the new startup identity, rejection of prior messages and the state of the drive before the first fresh command. Determine de-energised hardware behaviour directly. | Restore independently proved supplies, secure the train, observe healthy locks and holds with key released, then turn the local key. Confirm the recovered state retains the hold and rejects direct dispatch. |

The Rust replay exercises the station, door, ATP and TCMS logic using synthetic inputs. Its example startup identifiers are supplied by the fixture. The equipment supplier must demonstrate fresh authenticated identities across restarts, independent safe-egress authorisation and real actuator behaviour through a blackout. The powered-egress case covers a nominated exit at a platform. Loss of all door power, manual door release and evacuation onto the guideway require separate equipment and site trials, including conductor isolation and accessible escape routes.

cargo run --locked --manifest-path cities/Baghdad/engineering/controls/Cargo.toml

To replay the opening dossier, append `-- --opening PATH/TO/opening.json` to the Cargo command. This binds the replay to the exported opening bundle and checks the protected-conductor parent, six-car formation and fleet-wide equipment counts. The output identifies the opening, its evaluation and the station replay. Keep the opening JSON byte-for-byte with the result, as its digest enters the replay identity. For an opening trial, pass the same `--opening PATH/TO/opening.json` to `bench_trace.py` when creating each template and when assessing the completed manifest. The source hash, opening candidate and evaluation travel with the signed trial record.
