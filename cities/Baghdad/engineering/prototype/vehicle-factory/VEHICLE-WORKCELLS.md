# Airport vehicle workcells for the 36-car opening

Fabricate the 36 steel bodies and 72 bogie frames locally using long-bed saw handling, reusable jigs and manual gas-shielded welding. Buy nested plate blanks, contract bogie datum machining and full-body coating, and buy approved wheelset, traction, friction brake, suspension, door and HVAC assemblies. Integrate, inspect and repair the vehicles locally. Use the same production routes for the representative car when released for series reuse. Reserve a separate repair road so a failed item can leave the line without occupying its production position. [Factory route and acceptance responsibilities](factory-routing.md)

The opening has 36 cars in six six-car sets. The sealed cut schedule yields 5,184 gross pieces, of which 4,320 belong to the bodies and 864 to the bogie frames. The saw and liner route covers 2,592 RHS and 576 flat liner blanks. The plate route covers 1,440 450 × 450 × 8 mm right-triangle gussets and 576 12 mm pads. Nest by actual delivered stock lengths with trim, kerf and clamp allowance. A nominal 12 m finished cut needs incoming length sufficient for both end preparation and kerf. Separate the 5,184 gross pieces from purchased plate sheets, offcuts, machining scrap, repair stock and the as-installed BOM. The 5.273 t/car source figure is a stock subtotal. Hold the hole pattern, final seat dimensions and process capability for signed drawings. |

## Build and survey the fixtures

Use the car coordinate system with x forward, y left, z up from railhead. For the fixture, establish a level plane with three datum pads, a centreline from two pins or monuments and a single longitudinal datum. Immobilise parts with separate clamps close to the supports. Keep positive location on the primary reference and let non-locating supports adjust for fabrication and thermal movement. Use rigid transform translation to the installed bogie coordinate frames at x = ±6,400 mm. Bogie primary locators land at local x = ±1,050 mm, y = ±1,040 mm, and secondary locators at x = 0, y = ±850 mm. Assign removable gauge blocks for the design seat faces at z = +602 and +758 mm for a nominal 156 mm fitted secondary stack.

Specify a concept 18 × 3.2 m body bed and a 3.2 × 2.5 m bogie bed. These are fixture footprints. Construct welded steel bed modules with machined pick-up faces on levelling feet and bolt-on locator towers, replaceable wear pads, clamp slots and unobstructed inspection windows. Include positive module indexing, surveyed jig monuments outside heated zones and removable bridges at splice and door points so both faces can be welded or checked. Carry empty and loaded fixture survey, thermal drift and support-settlement checks. Final member sections, anchorage, loads, restraint gaps, lifting attachments and the clamp schedule need a fixture engineer's release before tooling quotation becomes a build order. Reserve adjacent laydown and working aisles in addition to the bed footprints.

Use a removable rectangular portal gauge, threshold datum bar and a sliding surrogate for the 700 × 30 × 1,990 mm leaf stock reservation. A 680 mm translation produces a nominal 1,380 mm long swept box per leaf in its travel direction. Check the installed aperture at threshold, mid-height and header. Compare with the 1,310 mm budgeted clear width and 1,300 mm requirement. A nominal 21 mm stock clearance awaits hardware, tolerance, loaded twist and positive residual. Include the guide/lock/retention stack, build stack, removal direction, end approach, fastener extraction stroke and hand/tool swing before releasing the fixture insert or its access window. ## Shift and changeover basis

The source schedule allocates 250 effective production days, one eight-hour shift per day and 80% reservable workcell utilisation, or 1,600 occupied hours per unit. The average demand is one car every 6.94 working days and two bogies per car. The 60 occupied hours per body and 48 campaign setup hours require two indexed beds. One bogie bed carries 1,036 hours against 1,600 hours on the assumed calendar. The two body beds and bogie bed can weld concurrently, so the schedule reserves three sets with extraction. Bed hours span layout, tacking, joining, cooling, inspection pauses and release. Welding sets are booked only when a task occupies welding equipment. Shared cranes and the tracker have their own booked task hours across bays. Quote complete crews and prove simultaneous access to shared equipment with a dated shop calendar.

The model assumes repeat families in six-car material and assembly releases. The annual saw setup provision is 36 hours, an average six hours per release for profile, length, blade, stop and first-off changes. Fixture setup provisions are 48 hours across the body beds and 28 hours at the bogie bed for initial referencing and setup/verification changes. Car and bogie times include ordinary locating and clamp/unclamp. Electrical setup includes drawings, formboard pins, adapters, reference check and changeover. Quote an explicit launch and pilot calendar with first-piece holds, offsite queue and transport, module delivery, absenteeism, rejects, test release and finished-set dispatch. The busiest single local fitout bay carries 1,338 hours, about 84% of its assumed reservable capacity. Measure queues, the range of repair durations and peak labour requirements in the first batch. Final train integration books six set tests and needs about 102 m nominal car length in series, plus actual coupler gaps, buffers, clearances and access. Reserve a staged full-set test track separately from the car test road and confirm the installed clearances before scheduling.

### When would an automated cutting cell pay?

Request two bids for the same 2,016 plate gussets and seat blanks. One uses a local subcontractor's existing nesting and cutting capacity. The other includes ownership, extraction, software, training, service, tooling, scrap recovery and resale for an in-house cell. Divide the extra fixed cost over this order by the accepted blanks. Every additional $10,000 of fixed cost requires about $4.96 saving per accepted blank to break even on these parts alone. This is a threshold for reading bids. Actual rates and yield remain for suppliers to return. If another factory programme will share the machine, state its committed volume, availability and share of setup cost separately.
