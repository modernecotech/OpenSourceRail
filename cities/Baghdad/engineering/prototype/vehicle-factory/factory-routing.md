# Vehicle factory and depot planning

Ask Iraqi workshops to price each operation against one equipped Airport car and the six-set order. The planned stock takeoff covers 144 gross pieces per car and 5,184 for the 36-car fleet, including two bare bogie frames and the jamb liner blanks per car. Obtain controlled consumptions for joints, inserts, coatings, bolting, hoses, wiring, fluids and finished fitout. Request local capacity, qualified processes and source evidence for each step. Imported safety-critical equipment carries its own engineering, traceability and overhaul offer. [Piece lists, draft orders and stock scope](../manufacturing-guide.md)

## Route to quote and time

| Stage and work centre | Inputs and operations to plan | Hold point and accountable review |
|---|---|---|
| Receiving and controlled stores | Reconcile purchase lines to supplier sites, grades, mill heats, wheelset and equipment serials, certificates, packing list and damage check. Keep restricted and quarantine stock identifiable. | Goods receipt and incoming Quality Inspection against approved criteria. QA releases or quarantines the batch. Purchasing closes missing supplier evidence. |
| Cut shop and preparation | Nest by actual rolled lengths. Track saw kerf, offcuts, scrap and trace transferred heat marks to part IDs. Qualify preparation and dimensional checks for braces, plate gussets and seats. | Compare dated cut and consumption records with the matching cut revision and approved profile and part tolerances before issuing parts to a jig. |
| Body jig and structural joining | Set surveyed sill, bolster and doorway datums. Plan weld sequence and distortion control for splices, posts, floor and roof ties and brace connections. Record measured jig condition and tooling calibration. | Demonstrate qualified personnel and procedures. QA checks weld and NDT records and before/after dimensional survey, including twist, spring-seat coplanarity and all eight portal openings. Engineering reviews fatigue-critical details and any repairs. |
| Bogie jig, machining and assembly | Establish axle planes and seat faces. Weld and inspect frame before machining. Assemble wheelsets, bearings, springs, retention, drives, brake and removable services using the approved interface schedule. | Review fitted geometry, bearing fit and endplay, alignment, torque retention, dimensional records and restrained drive/brake proof. Preserve individual frame and bogie serial records. |
| Coating, systems and fitout | Provide corrosion protection, film checks and drainage. Use controlled child BOMs for doors, roof equipment, passenger modules, wiring, collectors and power controls. Engineering reviews fire and accessibility interfaces. |
| Marriage, static integration and factory tests | Log each bogie-to-car installation and axle orientation. Record fluids and weigh the equipped car. Check supplied release limits, traceability and all interfaces before grouping cars into a train. | Supplier provides factory results, serial BOM and open-defect list. Buyer reviews approved test plan and witness records. Failed or incomplete gates route to repair and reinspection. |
| Independent first-article qualification and series decision | Demonstrate loaded structure, doors and platform interface, brake and hold performance, repeated heat duty and supported bogie exchange. | Delivery and depot handover | Ship protected, serialised, complete sets. Provide tooling, parts, limits, schematics and maintainer training. Demonstrate repaired unit handback before an operating roster assumes the capability. | Operator accepts manuals, supplies, facilities and assessed competence. Passenger operation follows separate route, recovery and authority approvals. |

Return setup and cycle time separately for each operation, with observed sample size, time range and evidence. State shift pattern, crew/skills, work centre and jig count, planned and unplanned downtime, first-pass yield, repair demand and stock buffers. Show batch sizes and the monthly ramp, fitted output, repairs and dispatch dependencies through six completed trains. Identify shared fixtures, inspection capacity and long-lead imports. Include a separate repair road and a dated timing trial that measures both the off-line repair and the next car advancing. Flag provisional inputs. The wider Baghdad production layouts and workforce cases can inform an offer once adapted to this quantity and geometry. ## ERPNext and Frappe HR preparation

1. Prepare draft Items with stock units for raw stock, service units for contracted operations, and revisions or separately controlled identities for interchangeable versus superseded parts. Define which serials and batches are required for raw material, assemblies, replaceable equipment and final cars. Prepare the company, Project, work centres, accept/inspect/quarantine/WIP/finished goods and spare warehouses, cost centres and controlled drawing attachments. Preserve the physical candidate, frame revision, source hash and engineering change reference wherever a supersession changes what may be built.
2. Expand the source draft routing into reviewable ERPNext BOMs, Operations and Routing by work centre. Build separate subassembly BOMs for each powered bogie and each complete door. Keep stock units distinct from RFQ price units. Resolve pilot allocation before creating series demand. Bind draft Work Orders to reviewed BOMs, use Job Cards for labour and stage checks, and link material movements and measured scrap to the consumed batch and produced serial. Supply as-planned versus as-built consumption and time reports with each completed car.
3. Establish Quality Inspection Templates and required attachments by operation and part revision. Capture approved limit source, units, method, calibration, measured values, uncertainty where needed, signed margin and disposition. Identify witness or hold points in the work instructions. Record quarantines, NCRs, concessions, rework and reinspections against affected serials. Route release to named quality and engineering owners. Assess the ERP configuration and workflow in a buyer-authorised test instance before relying on it for a gate.
4. Frappe HR records role, crew, shift and competence evidence with controlled access. Technical assessors confirm task and equipment authorisations, restrictions and expiry. Work assignments and duty release require valid competence, including during relief, rework, commissioning and training. Price bilingual work instructions, trainer/mentor hours, apprenticeships and assessor checks. Publish aggregate Iraqi workforce and training measures while keeping individual records controlled.
5. Hand the operator serial-linked asset and maintenance records with location, meter baselines, job intervals, warranty, accepted drawings and available spares. Log exchanges against originating and replacement serials. A finished Work Order or booked asset is an administrative milestone. The signed evidence review governs technical acceptance. ## Repair and maintainability demonstration

The bidder should include a depot service-lane drawing with the complete car, installed bogie, wheelsets, brakes, motor, collector, removable fittings, pallet and stands. The stock proposal allows a 3.820 m longitudinal extraction and a 3.10 m lane for a 2.50 m pallet and side allowances. Return the actual swept clearance and governing loaded pose for each end, lift capacities, support and isolation procedure, handling and tool access, stand loads and parking loads. Quote both detachment and restoration trials under a reviewed lift plan. Record leaks, electrical continuity, brake/hold results, bonded connections, geometry and fault indications before handback. [Mechanical exchange sequence](../mechanical.md) · [Equipped bogie and HVAC checks](../fitout-guide.md)

Price inspections and work instructions for wheel wear and reprofiling, bearings, suspension shims, brake wear and parking tests, drive cooling, collectors, door seals and liner replacement, HVAC filters, wiring and bonding, corrosion and rescue coupling. Give change time and crew, specialist fixtures, life limits and triggers, parts lead times, interchangeability restrictions, spare pool and local repair level. Provide illustrated removal/reinstallation access, inspection limits, supplier overhaul or repair turnaround, obsolete-part substitution process and data/reproduction rights. Report which maintenance cases have actually been demonstrated and which remain a proposed procedure. Assign authority for maintenance release, and keep owner cost, supplier warranty and future renewals reconciled to the commercial schedule.
