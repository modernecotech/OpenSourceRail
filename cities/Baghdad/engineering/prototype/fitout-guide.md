# Fit the powered bogie and body equipment

Use the bogie sheet and the piece IDs in `fitout-interfaces.json` to set out the pair of bogie jigs for one Airport car. Dimensions describe nominal steel. Survey the frame after welding and again after loading. Keep both surveys with the article serial and frame revision.

## Check the suspension stack

Use the bogie centre as the longitudinal origin and rail head as the vertical origin. Both bogies use the same axis directions. The four underside primary plates are shown in ochre. The two upper secondary plates are green. The 156 mm vertical gap between the bogie and car plates is the full nominal space for the secondary spring, isolator, shims and assembly tolerances in the stock model. Ask the supplier to return the installed stack, yaw and roll limits, jounce and rebound, and required retained travel. Compare the adverse stack with each measured seat gap for the loaded and empty car. Record the signed margin and the governing pose.

## Fit and exchange equipment

Each row of `returns/fitout.csv` identifies the mating datum, required supplier information and inspection or exchange trial. Record supplier-proposed limits with units and drawing revisions. Identify measured values by serial, calibration record, date and witness. Record limits, measured values and margins in separate columns. Keep the as-built and reviewer columns open until evidence exists.

Restrain and isolate the car under the reviewed lifting procedure. Independently support the body, secure the bogie on its pallet, account for stored pressure, cap hoses and identify electrical plugs before releasing retention. Prove access to fasteners, tooling, slings and connectors at each release step. Overlay the equipped drive, axleboxes, wheelsets, brakes, collector, hoses, hoist and pallet on both bogie removal strokes, including lateral transfer past the stands. Observe the closest approach and repeat the sequence for reinstallation. Confirm feedback, brake response and leak checks after reconnection.

Return HVAC pack and filter envelopes in the **car** frame, with mounting-hole positions, connections, mass, centre of gravity and lift orientation. Check roof structure, ducts, drain slopes, door pockets, passenger clearances, finished ceiling and the full filter slide. The steel roof ties alone establish no equipment load rating or maintenance aperture. Resolve conflicts with the supplier before fixing bracket geometry and ordering material.
