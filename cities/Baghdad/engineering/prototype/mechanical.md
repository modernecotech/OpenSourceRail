The workshop enquiry is for one 17 m steel body and two exchangeable bogie frames. The revision moves each doorway jamb 30 mm outward along the car and fits a replaceable liner on each jamb. The bogie centres, door centres, body envelope and straight platform alignment retain the dimensions of the Airport parent. This is a proposed prototype with a separate physical identity. Its drawings support fabrication review and quotation.

## Door, frame and fitout

Each of the eight steel openings is 1,360 mm wide before finishing. Allow 8 mm liner, 8 mm projecting seal, 3 mm fabrication error, 4 mm loaded distortion and 2 mm fit and wear **per edge**. The resulting 1,310 mm width has 10 mm in hand against the required 1,300 mm. These dimensions allocate space for a supplier design. Obtain the proposed seal profile, guide package and deflected aperture before freezing the jamb jig.

Cut the ten braces to the new lengths. Check the forty gussets by hand and orientation against the mesh.
2. Inspect the four chord splices, forty post-end joints, forty-four crossmember-end connections, twenty brace-end connections and the four body spring seats. 3. Fit sixteen 2,000 × 40 × 8 mm liner blanks. The proposal uses six flush screws and six replaceable threaded receivers per strip. The returned detail must show access, blind side grip, hole edge distances, corrosion isolation, residual jamb section, tightening method and identification of a damaged receiver. 4. Check each pair of sliding leaves over its full stroke of 680 mm per leaf. The space reservation for one leaf is 700 × 30 × 1,990 mm. The primary and secondary seat centres lie at y = ±1.040 m and ±0.850 m. Establish the bearing seat fits, axle squareness, drive-mount torque arms, rotor and caliper clearances, spring retainers and brake-hose routing from the chosen equipment. Quote a restrained drive/brake proof before rolling trials.

Isolate power, establish the car holding arrangement, segregate the work zone and verify pressure and electrical isolation. With the car separated, independently supported and a reviewed lifting and transfer plan in force, identify and disconnect hoses, bonds and wiring. The proposed 2.50 m pallet plus 300 mm on each side requires a **3.10 m lane**. The installed wheelsets, drive, brakes, overhead obstructions, lift points, jacks and pallet deck need physical data. On reinstallation check alignment, torque marks, leak tightness, continuity, brake force, travel and holding feedback before enabling traction.
