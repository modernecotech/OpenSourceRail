Welded steel and aluminium/GFRP comparison

If a hybrid supplier can remove 500 kg per car after accounting for inserts, fasteners, fire protection and finishes, the six-car train would weigh 198.55 t at the same passenger load. The model then requests 4.941 MW and draws 13.356 GWh/year on the same timetable. The central 30-year calculation leaves a whole-fleet allowance of $33,821,542 in present-value terms for any additional tooling, purchase, inspection, maintenance and repair costs before reaching the steel case’s cost. This result includes a change from 30 to 29 priced supply sites when the model resizes the radial layout. The saving from electricity alone is $102,214 at the same tariff. The mass saving is an enquiry assumption. Both installed weights and comparable prices need supplier returns.

Beam cage, bearings and platform

The 10 m beam enquiry includes 1,169.1 kg of scheduled bar and strand. The drawing locates twelve strands and shows the bar marks used by the bending schedule. The civil guide gives the bearing and lifting loads, platform drainage and boarding datums. The concrete price already allows for reinforced stock. Addition of the cage detail requires a replacement quotation.

Independent citywide planning option

The separate citywide plan assigns 552.562 km to fourteen connected services. Its 30-minute clockface option passes the checked interstation, terminal and shared crossing screens. Five access sections remain deferred. The 150-second peak service and its larger capacity require further signalling and terminal design.

Power and controls

The revised planning load requests 5.015 MW per accelerating train. The combined sensitivity reduces source voltage 1%, increases peak demand 5% and increases hot loop resistance a further 5%. The planning floor is 1.35 kV.

The package identifies the movements exposed in each affected section, including train acceleration and depot movements. Restoring passenger service or permitting evacuation requires verified isolation and an approved operating plan. The six-site options need their own installed price, hot measurements, protective grading and independent utility review.

Use the bench procedure for stopped boarding, effort take-up against the parking hold and departure. Record door side, torque, brake force, position, emergency egress and behaviour through a power interruption. Each trial must identify the article and controller build.
