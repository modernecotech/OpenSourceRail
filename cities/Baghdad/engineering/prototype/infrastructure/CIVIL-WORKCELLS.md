# Airport precast yard and erection workcells

The 36-car Airport opening carries 2,400 separate-track 10 m box beams and 2,400 corresponding site lifts over 12 route-km. The same takeoff carries 2,402 separate pier and foundation positions and 9,600 bearing allowances. Six passenger sites require their own platform schedules. The two open terminal faces cover the ends only, and the opening panel quantity remains open. The pilot bay supplies four trial panels and an uninterrupted 125 m island template uses 100. Allocate any accepted pilot to a series position before crediting it against the quantities. [Opening takeoff and article drawings](../airport-prototype.zip)

## Workdays and release

The opening delivery scenario allows 250 nominal days per year for two years. It models 200 effective days in year 1, with a ramp, and 225 in year 2. That gives 425 effective days for each yard or front. The target for 2,400 beams is 5.65 per effective day before dispatch lag. With one month's lag and 18.75 effective days lost from the available final month, the simplified whole-window rate rises to 5.91 per day. The opening's declared eight casts and eight lifts per productive day is the planning case. Its original monthly model finishes casting in year 2 month 6 and erection in year 2 month 7. That result assumes supply and site readiness and should be revisited once closures, utilities and front handovers are scheduled.

Keep three concrete gates in the work order. Measured strength and an approved transfer procedure release the prestress bed. A separate handling gate releases the element to the yard. Dispatch requires the approved age and strength, final dimensional and camber survey and closed product checks. The opening model's one-month lag is a dispatch allowance. Retaining beams in the stress bed for that lag would materially change mould demand. Agree the mix, hot-weather controls, moisture retention, temperature ramp and gradients, maturity or companion specimens, and lifting and transport strength with the responsible engineer. Record the age and strength decision against every beam serial number. ## Beam cells and common services

Use a wet-cast box mould with removable or collapsible internal void forms and removable solid-end bulkheads. Restrain the core against buoyancy and movement, demonstrate extraction past the solid end zones, and survey the 180 mm webs, 200 mm slabs, reinforcement, cover and insert locations after a first-article pour. The cell release sequence must account for forms that could restrain shortening. Arrange the cage jig for the slab bars and opposing U bars with their laps. Trial-fit the dense end-zone reinforcement, two six-strand banks, lift inserts, drains and rail fixings before approving the mould and the effective cycle. [Beam enquiry and tolerances](VIADUCT.md) · [Civil article guide](../civil-guide.md)

The costing hypothesis tensions twelve 15.2 mm straight strands to 182.7 kN each, giving 2,192.4 kN per aligned strand group at each end. Trace that load through bearing plates, anchorage heads, abutments and their foundations or through an engineered self-reacting frame. Check the two banks at y = ±0.910 m, mean strand level 0.400 m above the soffit, unbalanced stressing and release, end-head eccentricity and frame stability. A continuous two-position bed could share one aligned strand group in series and needs a common release decision for both castings. Parallel strand groups acting on a common abutment accumulate force. Obtain approved force limits and verified bed and head geometry, working tails, jack stroke, fitted anchorages, load–elongation reconciliation and staged detensioning. Provide calibrated jacks and gauges, protected stressing and release areas, spill response and certified lifting equipment. ## Support handover and erection

Set out stations, utilities and closures by chainage. Group the 1,201 support nodes into contiguous work areas and sequence two pier positions per node. Establish access and foundations, allow concrete and grout to reach the approved strengths, survey the two seats and bearings, then release the contiguous bay for delivery. A pace of eight beams/day requires roughly eight pier positions/day in steady state and 32 bearing provisions per eight-beam dispatch lot. Stop, regroup or shift the front where pier, possession or delivery readiness drops below that rate. The 2.50 m square section in the model is a trial footing. Return boreholes, service relocations, flood/scour and groundwater, seismic detail, eccentricity and settlement before assigning production forms, rigs or crew counts. At eight positions/day every day of site-stage occupancy adds eight simultaneously occupied positions. Count separate temporary excavations, footing and shaft/cap forms and cure holds using the surveyed construction sequence.

For an eight-hour possession, a trial front budgets 1.5 h for establishment, crane repositioning and closeout, 0.75 h each for eight trailer-to-seat cycles and 0.5 h for interruption. Count truck spotting, rig and trial pick, controlled slew, seat and guide confirmation, temporary stability and hook release in the per-beam cycle. Sweep both pick and set radii under the surveyed obstructions. The crane return must cover boom, counterweight, outrigger extension and slew sectors, manufacturer chart deductions, ground bearing and mats, weather and wind, and dynamic loads. With pick lines 2 m from each end, the article's four equal legs at 60° screen about 97 kN/leg, or around 213 kN on each of two loaded legs with the diagnostic dynamic allowance. The figures inform rigging review. The contractor must check insert loads, roll stability, load transfer, restraints and wind at the complete supported weight. Verify cap and soil for outrigger/support loads, including temporary cases. A seven-hour usable possession with the same 2 h fixed allowances fits six complete lifts. A six-hour possession fits five, which misses the whole-window pace unless more workdays or fronts become available. ## Station panels and tooling enquiries

A trial panel weighs about 69.0 kN and uses 2.761 m³ of stock and 243 kg of straight bar. Four flat tables at one panel per table per 24 h can supply one standard bay on a productive day, giving 25 productive days per uninterrupted island template. At a 48-hour occupied cycle those tables give two panels/day and 50 productive days per template. Use interchangeable edge and opening inserts, captive drainage formers, checked crossfall and texture, cage/insert templates, labelled panel stands and a separate panel lifting plan. Reserve dates for special panels, drainage returns, seams, shaft interfaces, tactile edge and accessible route inspections. Station heads, foundations, lifts, structures, egress and commissioning each need their own quantities and dates. Keep the boarding tolerance check for the actual train, rail and platform under load and wear. [Station enquiry](STATION.md) · [Boarding sweep](PLATFORM-SWEEP.md)

Enquire locally for welded shutters, bulkheads, replaceable wear plates and gauges, cage jigs, stock stands and maintenance access. Require straightness and dimensional survey through the curing cycle, trial-fit the extraction path and quote inspection and spares for seals, fixings and actuators. For stressed hardware establish traceability, fabrication and proof-testing responsibility and jack calibration before crediting local availability. Enquire for a site-adjacent self-reacting wet-cast system, external-abutment lines in an established yard and existing supplier capacity with route dispatch. Compare bed foundations and installation, interchangeable form sets, cranes and crew cover, curing consistency, logistics, clear build and maintenance space, verified release cycles and cost for identical sections. For panels, stationary flat tables and circulation pallets can both support this family. Circulation shares cleaning, casting, compaction and curing stations and needs enough pallets and controlled buffers through the cure hold. Ask existing plants for timed evidence and changeover costs around the station variants. A vendor's extrusion/slipform product list establishes equipment availability. Any proposal to use that process for the beam needs evidence for closed box geometry, solid ends, developed bars, embeds and the same analysis interfaces, or an explicit candidate revision.
