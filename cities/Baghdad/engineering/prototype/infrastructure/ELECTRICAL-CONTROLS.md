Quote one integrated vehicle traction and control module. The prototype allowance covers one axle drive module, a shoe allocation, a TCMS node, an inverter controller, one service-power unit, two shoe and two brake channels and eight door controllers. Price station dispatch and protection bench equipment, wayside source equipment, route communications, other drives or cabinets and independent integrated witnessing as separate scopes. Reconcile the module against `pilot-car`, `fleet`, `signalling` and `test-traction-controls`. ## Terminals, connectors and cables

Complete the [connector return](connector-return.csv) from the proposed schematics. The listed interfaces are enquiry groups. Expand each into one row per physical connection or terminal/pin. The supplier may propose hardwired terminals where appropriate. Add bonds, shields, grounds, current transformers and maintenance/test points where applicable. For each row return both device references, cable and mating connector/terminal part numbers, pinout with polarity and return reference, ratings, assembly quantities and as-built serial/terminal identifiers. Identify isolation boundaries, signal states on loss of supply, interlocks, retention, strain relief, bend radius and clearances. Confirm derating, environment, contamination protection, mating cycles, separation of incompatible circuits and safe unmating conditions in the installation return. No supplier part number, pin number, voltage rating or combination of independent channels has been selected by the schedules.

Bind each physical terminal to one distinct independent recorder input in the relevant version 2 `returns/*-logger.json` form, with the device serial, terminal or decoded field, units and test mode. Record door addresses as installed. Slots 0–3 face left and 4–7 right per car, offset by eight per successive car. The real door, opposite lock and any aggregate exit signals require wiring and witness evidence. Keep the raw common-clock CSV, signed mapping, calibration, limit revisions, test stimulus, build digest and article, visit and start-up IDs. ## Isolation, inspection and acceptance

| Inspection point | Evidence and release condition |
|---|---|
| Before build | Engineer reviews single-lines, connector and cable schedules, protective bonding drawings and isolation-zone map. Map both tracks, adjacent-source and cross-tie paths, vehicle regeneration and backfeed, local energy stores and service supply. Show which retained circuits keep brakes, emergency egress and independent instruments available. |
| Before cabinet and harness energisation | Return conductor identification, point-to-point and polarity checks, bonding continuity, protection settings and approved insulation test results. Check connector coding and retention, terminal torque, covers, cable separation, drip and thermal paths, safe access and reinstatement. Account for loaded outputs and simulated channels in the test method. |
| Before isolation trial or site energisation | Asset owner approves site-specific switching, locking, proving dead, discharge, earth placement, accessible test points, barriers, rescue arrangements and independent restoration checks. Demonstrate authorised isolation with both track and adjacent-source paths considered and with the selected feeder topology, including the return conductors and paired branches where fitted. Record voltage presence and absence, no backfeed and egress-path status. |
| Traction and protection trials | Witness conductor/return hot bounds and contact sharing, actual thermal duty and cooling, fault-current and breaker interruption envelopes, primary-backup selectivity, touch and stray voltage, regeneration and independent stopping with zero network receptivity. Use study limits for fault duties and utility supply. The 1.35 kV collector floor is a planning screen. |
| Station visit and recovery | Witness arrival, hold and departure in both directions, brake feedback loss, wrong-side and stale commands, communications silence, evacuation with proved and blocked paths and loss/restoration of service power. Log measured force, effort, displacement and door/lock states through blackout with independent instrumentation. Verify fresh start-up identity and deliberate reset before a new station cycle. Obtain separate evidence for manual release, loss of all door power and guideway evacuation. |

Record the engineer-approved limits, instruments, serials, as-built wiring and software revisions, observed results, faults and disposition before progressing each hold point. Independently authenticate the raw logs and signed witness records in the qualification workflow. The software replay and blank logger forms support the inspection.
