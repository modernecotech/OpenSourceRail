# Accessible station bay supplier schedule

Quote one 5 m straight bay using four 4.98 × 1.98 × 0.28 m panels. Opening quantities depend on the design around station plant, lift shafts and accesses. The uninterrupted 125 m island template uses 100 panels. Price passenger lifts, the structure below the bay, other station works and witnessed boarding trials separately from the bay allowance. Reconcile with `stations` and `test-civil`.

Use the top-surface datum in `platform-bay.ifc` and the boarding information in `portal-detail.svg`, `workshop-guide.md` and `civil-article.json`. The trial straight-body horizontal gap is 75 mm, and the nominal car floor is 1.000 m above rail head. The site survey must connect that rail datum to the bay. Neither quantity defines an approved installed gap or step. | Quote item | Enquiry basis | Design and inspection return |
|---|---|---|
| Precast bay deck, connections and edge | Four panels, about 2.761 m³ and 243 kg scheduled straight bar per panel. Quote seats, inserts, joints, anchors, edge finishes and seals. | Return bar grades, laps, openings, bearing and connection details, lift insert and inspection access, finish tolerances, as-built level and material evidence. Confirm platform actions, local loads and the final clear width at fittings and joints. |
| Drainage and wet surface | 1.25% crossfall toward centre, nominal 50 mm fall across half the island. Runoff screens about 0.53 and 1.06 L/s per bay at 50 and 100 mm/h. | Return design rainfall, tributary area, grates, outlets, overflow, downpipe and clean-out access. Survey falls and observe ponding, slip performance and blocked-outlet paths under the agreed method. |
| Door and accessible boarding | Interface with the same six-car train and physical door addresses, four openings per car on each side and 24 on the boarding side. | Return edge and tactile details, swept envelopes, stopping tolerance, gap and step across wear, load, suspension movement, temperature and curvature. Provide measured level and wheelchair circulation on the bay, and a plan to survey each of the 24 boarding zones on the installed platform. |
| Access, emergency movement and maintenance | Return temporary trial access and price installed approach interfaces separately, including accessible route and lift-outage arrangements. | Submit egress and crowd calculations, measured pinch points, alternate paths and equipment removal routes. Identify fire and rescue interfaces and prove the authorised egress path for the nominated physical exit. Coordinate signage, barriers, isolation controls and lockable maintenance access. |

Hold before casting for edge, connection and drainage approval. Survey as-built rail and edge datums before boarding trials. Agree accessible gap, step, wheelchair movement, wet-surface performance and egress limits with the responsible engineer. Document which zones and operating conditions were witnessed, including route availability during loss of power and how a separated, isolated guideway escape would be verified.
