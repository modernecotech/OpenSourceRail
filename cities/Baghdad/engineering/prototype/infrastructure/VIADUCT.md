# Viaduct beam supplier schedule

Quote one straight single-track 10 m box on a 9.50 m bearing span and one delivery and erection. The opening takeoff has 2,400 beams, 2,400 lifts, 2,402 pier/foundation positions and a 9,600-bearing allowance. Price repeat production and factory yard costs separately. Reconcile the pilot against `elevated-civil`, `tooling` and `test-civil`. The planning proxy includes cage and strand, with new piers, geotechnics, extraordinary access and independent load trials priced separately.

Use `track-span.ifc` at the soffit datum, `pier.ifc` at the footing underside and the archive's `beam-cage-section.svg`, `beam-bar-strand-schedule.csv`, `civil-article.json` and `mechanical.json`.
