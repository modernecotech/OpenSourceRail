# First precast beam and station bay

The Airport first-article enquiry uses a straight, separate-track 10 m box on a 9.50 m bearing span. Price one casting and one station panel before quoting a series. The box has 180 mm webs, 200 mm slabs and two 550 mm solid end regions. It uses 13.404 m³ of reinforced stock. The parent lift study takes a 335.1 kN concrete-and-steel self-weight. Adding the bar weight to that allowance would count the steel twice.

## Reinforcement and prestress

Use `beam-bar-strand-schedule.csv` for lengths and counts, and `civil-article.json` for individual transverse stations, bar coordinates, bend centreline radii and the strand pattern. The cage and strand arrangement has its own physical identity linked to the unchanged concrete stock. The added steel detail is for prices, congestion review and first-article mould planning.

- **U-L and U-R** are two 12 mm opposing U bars around the outside perimeter, with nominal 45 mm cover. Place their centres 15 mm apart along x. Each has two 90° bends at 36 mm centreline radius. The two free legs overlap the opposite U by 400 mm at top and bottom. The cut length follows the bend centreline and includes both straight lap tails. The assumed lap and mandrel require a bar grade, bond and development design.
- **B1, B2, T1 and T2** identify straight longitudinal slab bars at two elevations in each slab. **BX and TX** cross these on the inner slab faces. **W** runs along the outer side of both webs. End stations use closer transverse spacing. The enquiry excludes splice losses, extra bursting steel, diaphragms around fittings, local rail seats, lift inserts and anchor plates pending the supplier detail. Check the cage at end blocks, ducts and inserts with the actual bar and strand bundles in the mould.
- **PS** is straight seven-wire pretensioning strand. Each web receives a vertical bank of six strands. The banks are centred at y = ±0.910 m above a soffit datum, with a mean strand height of 0.400 m. The equipment supplier must return its bed length, tails, abutment rating, release method and measured strength and losses. Quoted embedded stock excludes working tails. The representative product sheet lists 140 mm² nominal area, 1.10 kg/m and 261 kN minimum breaking force for the 15.2 mm strand. [Bekaert strand product sheet](https://www.bekaert.com/content/dam/corporate/en/product-catalog/pdfs/Bekaert-Prestressing-Strand-for-Concrete-Reinforcement-Productsheet.pdf)

The section study asks what effective compression offsets a gross-section positive bending envelope of about 894 kNm. At 300 mm eccentricity the demand is about 1,286 kN. The costing case assumes an initial 70% of the representative catalogue force and compares 15%, 20% and 30% losses. These are planning hypotheses. Use the full-transfer and pick-hogging stress rows to challenge the first casting procedure. Release needs verified concrete at transfer and service, strand transfer length, actual sequence and losses, permitted stresses, local and ultimate resistance, fatigue and water control. A positive midspan compression margin does not establish those properties.

## Bearings, lifts and site foundation

Four 350 mm square support footprints lie at x = 0.250 and 9.750 m and y = ±0.650 m. The nominal distance from pad edge to concrete edge is only 75 mm longitudinally and 175 mm transversely. Allocate installation error, pad rotation and sliding travel along with actual required edge distance. A 45 K temperature change across the 9.50 m span gives 4.28 mm free movement under the study expansion coefficient. Shrinkage, creep, bearings and rail restraint need separate movements. Return bearing thickness, anchors, guides, positive restraint, pad-access width and jack saddle detail.

For a closed and unloaded track, the calculated installed dead end reaction is about 183 kN, or about 91 kN per jack if two jacks share it equally. Supply the actual lifting points, staged reaction transfer, stroke, eccentric and asymmetric load cases, blocking and temporary restraint before a bearing change. A single jack may attract a much greater share. Reinstate the rail, sections and drainage against surveyed datums before reopening.

The lifting calculation uses pick lines 2.0 m in from the ends. Price rigging for the complete supported assembly and the surveyed crane radius. The static four-leg equal-share case at 60° gives about 97 kN per leg. A two-loaded-leg case with 10% added vertical load gives about 213 kN per loaded leg. The supplier must check out-of-plane stability, local inserts, transfer strength, strand development, transport reactions, wind, lifting gear weight and allowable ground pressure. Survey the 2.50 m trial footing against the actual site and revise for eccentricity, settlement, groundwater, seismic, utilities, road clearances and scour.

## Platform, access and drainage

The 5 m station bay has four 4.98 × 1.98 m panels. The panel register provides straight stock and bar mass per panel and per uninterrupted 125 m island. Check openings, edges, lift shafts, fire compartments and reinforcement laps separately. The drain fall of 1.25% gives 50 mm drop across half the island. For the 40 m² tributary area, 50 and 100 mm/hour trial rainfall produce approximately 0.53 and 1.06 litres/s per bay at a 0.95 runoff coefficient. Return site rainfall, downpipe positions, outlet levels, blocked-outlet overflow paths and accessible grate locations.

The straight train body leaves a nominal 75 mm horizontal platform gap. The car floor is 1.000 m above rail head. Survey the actual threshold height, straight and curved edge positions, suspension movement, stopping accuracy, door transfer step and lift access. Confirm each of the 24 door zones on the boarding side. An illustrative 3 m effective exit width at 0.8 people/m/s would clear 700 people in about 4.86 minutes and 1,400 in about 9.72 minutes at sustained flow. Measure the pinch points, opposing flows, queue limits, lift-outage route, fire and accessible egress demand and safe power isolation before setting station capacity.
