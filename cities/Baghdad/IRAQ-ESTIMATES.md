# What Baghdad would pay to run the railway

The central planning case assumes a flat IQD 750 ticket. For the seven corridors it puts the first full year at about 516,000 paid journeys a day. The funding table uses annual average traffic, Iraqi pay and electricity rates, and 50% borrowing at 4% real over 20 years. Each network is a separate investment. Amounts are millions of constant 2026 US dollars.

Over 30 years the central present cost is **$12.33bn** for the seven corridors and **$48.65bn** for full citywide service. This comparison takes construction at face value and discounts annual operations and the original scheduled renewal allowances by 4% real from service opening. Interest and fares are accounted for in the buyer cash comparison above.

## How to read the estimates

Iraq’s 2024 land transport statistics report an average IQD 500 ticket for internal Baghdad government bus services (table 7). The downside, central and upside posted rail tickets are IQD 500, IQD 750, IQD 1,000. After paid discounts and collection costs the operator retains 80%, 85%, 90% respectively. At IQD 1,510 per US dollar the central net receipt is IQD 637.5, or $0.422. [Iraqi transport statistics](https://cosit.gov.iq/documents/transportation%20and%20communication/transportation/fullreports/landtrans/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D9%82%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%B1.pdf) · [Central Bank selling rate to banks, October 2026](https://cbi.iq/news/view/3355)

Assumed all-day occupancy is 8%, 15%, 24% of the scheduled place-kilometres. The seven-corridor estimate assumes 11 km per rail leg and 1.15 legs per linked journey. Citywide assumes 16 km and 1.25 legs. A passenger changing trains counts once towards fare revenue. An assumed 92% of linked journeys pay. These are opening-year planning judgements. Baghdad origin–destination research on earlier proposed lines gives local demand context. Station catchment counts, measured trip lengths and a fare survey would allow the operator to refine the figures. [Baghdad transport study](https://jeasd.uomustansiriyah.edu.iq/index.php/jeasd/article/view/2032)

Operations include the estimated unattended-service wage bill once. The model replaces the payroll already allowed in train, station and power maintenance costs and replaces the original electricity allowance for the same mileage. The downside combines higher construction, recruitment and power costs with weaker fare income. The upside combines lower costs with stronger income. These are joint sensitivities, with no assigned probabilities. The main-route service defers five access sections while retaining the cost of the full citywide infrastructure. [Pay and staffing](../../docs/operator-review/delivery/README.md) · [Power prices and local supply](IRAQ-POWER.md)

The public funding column covers operations and scheduled debt payments less collected fares in the first full service year. The loan share applies to each construction estimate, using the original timing of annual draws. The buyer also funds its construction share and later renewals. Iraq’s $930m World Bank national railway project is a precedent for seeking multilateral finance. The metro rates and terms here are assumptions awaiting lender offers.
