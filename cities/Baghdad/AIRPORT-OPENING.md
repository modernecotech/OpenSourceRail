# Opening the Baghdad Airport corridor

The Airport service is a 12 km, six-stop opening option within the seven-corridor proposal. The priced planning scope totals **$813.7m**, with low and high cases of **$782.9m and $879.5m** in 2026 USD. This dedicated opening includes a depot, a control centre, three traction supply sites, terminal trackwork and Iraqi site allowances. Surveyed quantities, supplier prices, committed financing and a funded opening date remain open. The published reference quantities and generic rates total $793.177m. The railway authority still needs to agree the sites and opening date.

Baghdad Airport terminal → Al Amiriya → Hay Al Aamil → Al Bayaa → Um Al Tubol → Al Qadisiya

## Iraq site works and opening budget

The three cases use the same six stations, 21 trains, depot, control centre and three traction supply sites. The low case assumes accessible public rights of way, short extra haulage and limited ground treatment. **Land and utilities.** The Ministry of Planning reported about IQD 96bn for the 7 km Baghdad–Wasit entrance contract, including service roads, bridges and widening, and identified water, electricity and property conflicts. We use 10%, 22% and 42% of its historical cost per kilometre as rounded 2026 allowances for the narrower corridor land, diversions and reinstatement scope. Those fractions are engineering judgements. The road figure is carried at face value in IQD. A cadastral schedule and utility owners' estimates would replace it. [Ministry of Planning project report, in Arabic](https://mop.gov.iq/archives/10965)

**Railway and airport.** The World Bank's May 2025 Iraq railway plan estimated USD 33m for 32 km of Al Yussufia–Baghdad track renewal. We use the published USD per kilometre directly with a 1.6–4.0 multiplier for short new track, formation, basic ramp, traction and controls. The low/base/high cases buy 0.40/0.85/1.50 extra track-km beyond the depot fence. Separately allow 5/6/8 point ends, including four for the two terminal crossovers. The turnout premium covers special trackwork, machines and local route proving above plain track and ordinary signalling. Landside works cover the connector to the existing passenger forecourt, managed drop-off and security interface beyond the station entrance. External grid works cover tie-ins upstream of the three priced railway substations. The track length, multipliers and premiums need a surveyed layout and utility connection offers. Allow two 1 m diameter shafts below each of 25/97/241 standard support caps, 18/24/32 m deep. Boreholes must establish the actual founding solution. The reference beam and lift rates cover ordinary production and installation. Add only temporary yard premises, extra loaded-equivalent haul and exceptional road-crossing premiums. The base case carries 12 extra km per beam dispatch, including return-trip cost in the km rate. The ranges assume a conventional landside terminal and a depot connectable by a short surface lead and ramp. ## Annual operations, passengers and fares

In the central case this service costs **$16.25m/year** to operate. About **10,121 paid one-way journeys per day** bring in **$1.56m/year**. The operator needs **$14.69m/year** in operating support before capital repayments and renewals. These first-full-year cases combine demand, fare, wage and power-price assumptions. **Who rides.** Iraq's civil aviation figures count 4,199,253 arrivals and departures at Baghdad International Airport in 2024. Holding that volume constant, assume 5%, 12% or 22% use rail for one ground journey. Add 150, 500 or 1,100 one-way trips per day by airport workers and visitors. For residents travelling solely between the five other stops, assume 2, 5 or 9 boardings per eligible train call. Each of the 456 daily trains makes four calls where a local passenger can board and alight at another non-airport stop. Airline passengers, airport workers and local-only journeys are counted once each. [Iraq air transport statistics, tables 4–5](https://www.cosit.gov.iq/documents/transportation%20and%20communication/transportation/fullreports/eartrans/%D8%A7%D8%AD%D8%B5%D8%A7%D8%A1%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%86%D9%82%D9%84%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D9%88%D9%8A%20%D9%81%D9%8A%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%82%D8%B7%D8%A7%D8%B9%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AD%D9%83%D9%88%D9%85%D9%8A%20%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%A7%D9%85%20%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9%202024.pdf)

**Trip length and fare.** Assume equal choice among the downstream local stops and equal airport traffic to each of the five other stops. The diagram's planning chainages give mean distances of 4.2 km locally and 7.2 km for airport trips. The central mix averages 4.7 km and uses 1.35% of all scheduled place-km. This low average calls for a pilot count of hourly loads before committing to the full service. With one train per journey, assume 92% pay. At IQD 750 the operator receives IQD 637.5 per paying journey after discounts and collection costs. Count actual airport access, worker shifts and neighbourhood boardings to update the forecast. [Baghdad fare assumptions and local bus comparator](IRAQ-ESTIMATES.md)

**Who operates it.** The dedicated unattended service requires 197 ordinary posts, including station assistance and cleaning, the control room, depot, civil and power response, and administration. The central case adds 8 FTE for two airport-interface posts over the operating day. The wage calculation credits $1.674m of estimated payroll already in the train, station and line allowances, then adds the local roster once. It prices the airport liaison duties separately from ordinary station duties and construction labour. [Iraqi wage assumptions and roster method](../../docs/operator-review/delivery/README.md)

Ordinary station running costs stay in the existing station envelope. The six named stops each receive one generic accessible station allowance. Each terminus needs at least two simultaneous turnback positions at peak. Keeping all trains at their arrival platforms throughout booked waits would occupy up to 8 and 7 berths. Ask bidders to price both terminal crossovers, the platform and stabling layout, empty moves, rescue access and depot connection against this service. A procurement drawing must show clearances for the full 104.5 m train. The published between-station block screen needs up to 2 blocks on the most restrictive pair before braking overlaps or degraded operation.

For a dedicated opening, quote one permanent depot and one permanent control centre for the 21-set fleet. The reference direct price is $99.0m for the depot, control centre and three grid sites together. A buyer comparing another arrangement starts with $661.903m of other priced work and adds 1.326 times the matched replacement direct price in millions. For a localised comparison, start with the dedicated Iraq scenario above, deduct the dedicated reference price times the same multiplier, then add the matched host return times that multiplier. Credit any overlapping depot lead or external feeder in the local site rows. Host capacity and annual service fees need separate returns. A shared-site return should identify the host, spare capacity, access tracks, incremental works and service fee. A temporary return should price the interim workshop, stabling, dispatch room, backup supervision, temporary connections, later transfer and removal. Keep each prospective credit conditional on available capacity and the host's agreement. The opening layout tests two end feeds and an intermediate feed. Its uniform-share grid request is lower per site than the request from one train. Check local feed sharing, losses, outages and coincident acceleration before sizing transformers or assigning a supply section. Use the local site-works rows to scope surveys and tenders for access, land rights, feeders, special foundations, yard access and haulage. Financing follows the construction cash schedule.

## Opening sequence

| Release decision | Evidence requested |
|---|---|
| Secure route and sites | Airport operator agrees terminal entrance, locate Um Al Tubol, survey route and station chainage, utilities, street crossings, geotechnics, platform access, depot and grid parcels. |
| Release structures for production | Approved site and load data, beam and pier drawings, accessible station layouts, casting trials, storage and transport permits. In an illustrative two-year civil window, the casting and erection model needs 1 yard(s) and 1 front(s). Ground works, stations and utility approvals can govern progress. |
| Energise and move test trains | Utility agreement and protection settings, isolation and return-current tests, controlled terminal routes, depot access, accepted braking, evacuation and recovery trials. |
| Start passenger service | Commission all six stations and protected routes, train staff, prove timed vehicle availability and emergency cover, demonstrate accessible passenger journeys, secure independent operating acceptance. |
