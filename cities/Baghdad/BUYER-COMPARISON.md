# Buying a railway using an open reference

Amanat Baghdad can use OpenSourceRail to write a specification, estimate quantities and negotiate the right to build and repair locally, whether an Iraqi consortium, an international supplier or a combination delivers the railway. The working estimate is a negotiating reference. A credible fallback to a vendor offer requires priced local packages, a capable integrator, financed tooling and an acceptance programme.

The earlier investment process did not secure a contractor to deliver the metro. Amanat Baghdad’s spokesperson told Al-Mada that the investment offers fell short of the government’s requirements. Two days later, the National Investment Commission’s deputy chair told NINA that a new study and updated plans were being prepared for fresh investment opportunities. [Al-Mada interview with Amanat Baghdad, 25 October 2026](https://almadapaper.net/458226/) · [NINA interview with the National Investment Commission, 27 October 2026](https://ninanews.com/website/News/Details?key=1322248)

OpenSourceRail is preparing for new ideas in **late 2026 to early 2027**. The authority has yet to publish a submission date in the sources reviewed. The seven corridors provide a historical comparison while Baghdad revisits its routes, service and financing. Use this period to prepare quantities, specify local repair rights and gather indicative prices. When the new brief is issued, reconcile route, service and financing requirements before asking for comparable firm offers.

## Issue the same scope to every bidder

For the current civil enquiry use the [20 m shared-pier delivery programme](engineering/mechanical/civil/rail-fed/network-delivery.md)
and ask for both deck-fed and road erection against identical stock and site
conditions. Request separate prices for yard works, launchers, deck haul,
night shifts and special crossings. Use the 10 m quantities and local-content
examples below to reproduce the earlier common estimate.

Request three returns for one network at a time. Invite a turnkey offer, a vendor-led offer with specified transfers of production and repair, and a locally integrated offer using open interfaces. Allow vendors to quote all three. Provide the same train capacity, accessibility, hourly service, route assumptions, construction dates, excavation exclusions, summer duty, spares and 30-year maintenance horizon. Require a priced departures register against the open bill. Require each supplier to identify interface responsibility through commissioning and warranty. The sponsor carries any scope it retains in a split award.

For a package priced at 100 units with 35 imported, 10 untraced and 55 documented domestic value, report **55% verified** and **10% untraced**, even if an Iraqi prime receives the entire contract. Aggregate by value across lots. Report skills separately in qualified staff, shop hours, independently accepted articles and turnaround time. A local address alone establishes none of these measures.

**Reading the figures.** The target award is the budget for work assumed to be placed with Iraqi firms. Retained value is the portion of those awards expected to pay for domestic production, labour and services, including local subcontract work once. The columns reconcile to the original installed price. A larger local order does not reduce capital automatically. Domestic work under foreign-awarded packages is unestimated. The balance of the Iraqi award can include imports, overseas services and land transfers. Each case holds the award split fixed while changing how much work an Iraqi contractor performs locally. These buyer estimates are negotiating positions pending origin schedules and priced offers. [Reproduce the comparison or inspect every scope](localisation.py)

**Civil works and stations.** The central assumptions retain 75% of Iraqi-awarded elevated civil work and 65% for stations. Iraq already produces cement and local crews can cast, erect and finish concrete. Allow for imported stressing systems, bearings, lifting machinery and specialist supervision. Stations also need lifts, doors, power and emergency systems. Track and depot packages include imported rail, fasteners and workshop machinery. The land and utilities account assumes 25% / 45% / 65% of an Iraqi award buys diversions or reinstatement, with 55% / 75% / 90% of those works retained. The remainder covers provisional land or compensation transfers and receives no production credit. A parcel and utilities schedule can replace that split. [Central Bank of Iraq, cement production from Ministry of Industry data, table 7](https://cbi.iq/static/uploads/up/file-173563991524858.pdf)

**Electrical and vehicle work.** For the grid package, use the estimated Iraqi share of each complete traction site as a proxy for the mix within Iraqi-awarded lots. The grid table in the electricity guide covers *all* sites regardless of award. Those totals overlap this comparison. Line-side cable laying and installation offer more local work than producing traction converters, safety processors and brake electronics. For trains, retain 20% / 35% / 50% of Iraqi awards for progressively more assembly, frame fabrication, harness installation and test. Imported wheelsets, drives, brakes, doors and foreign commissioning consume part of the award. Iraq's railway modernisation project includes workshop refurbishment and procurement of equipment and spares. Metro production at the planned rate still requires first articles, tooling and witnessed tests. [Iraqi traction supply prices and factory evidence](IRAQ-POWER.md) · [World Bank project description for Iraqi Republic Railways](https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2025/06/25/iraq-new-us-930-million-project-to-extend-and-modernize-railways-promote-regional-connectivity-and-boost-growth)

**Tooling, engineering and acceptance.** Local jig fabrication and maintenance coexist with imported machine tools. Design and field testing can pay local professional wages, while specialist software, overseas factory tests and independent reviewers draw on external services. The low and high percentages model different sourcing agreements across the same packages. Bidders should replace them with labour hours, tier-level materials, origin and independently witnessed transfer milestones. The cost of tooling, licences, training or a warranty change must appear in the corresponding cash offer. [Factory and supplier pricing instructions](#take-a-package-to-a-supplier)

## Negotiate the price and the capability together

1. Publish the service specification, cost basis and evaluation method to all bidders. Make safety, accessibility and demonstrable delivery capability conditions for progression. Have the sponsor approve procurement rules and applicable lender conditions before issuing weighted criteria.
2. Obtain confidential, itemised first-round offers and independent local quotations. Keep bidder-proprietary details confidential. For each package compare cost per delivered unit, accessible capacity, performance guarantee and verified domestic content. Identify missing scope and price uncertainty explicitly.
3. Ask each bidder for a revised offer on the same baseline and for incremental prices to move named work into Iraq. Ask, for example, for the unit price and defect warranty for locally machined bogie frames after first-article acceptance, or locally tested control cabinets with the same protection equipment. Record tooling cost, minimum order, schedule, rights and five-year spares price alongside each change.
4. Report the lowest evaluated whole-life cost at each achievable domestic value-added level. Show the incremental cost or saving per additional unit of verified domestic value. Retain a cheaper turnkey return and a higher-local-content return as distinct options where neither dominates. A shortlist requires compliant performance, a matched delivery boundary and a funded integration owner.
5. Compare first and final offers on identical scope, currency and date. Claim a negotiated saving only for the like-for-like difference. Track *contracted* local content at award and *realised* value from audited invoices, factory inspections and acceptance records. The buyer may award to another vendor while retaining the reference, interface records and documented negotiating history.

Rated criteria and life-cycle cost are established procurement tools. Actual criteria and weightings require procurement-authority approval for the chosen financing. [World Bank guidance on rated criteria](https://www.worldbank.org/en/about/rated-criteria)

Use the [station pricing register](maps/STATION-PRICING.md) to record site, access class and the unique budget slot for a surveyed station before including its offer in the complete scope comparison. A vehicle unit is a complete six-car trainset. A station unit is one complete station including access. The civil unit covers the whole first-network elevated civil account, including residual works and site-specific crossings. The buyer first reconciles samples, repeat quantities, exclusions and integration cost in the underlying scope review. Isolated frame, deck, cabinet and crossing quotations stay on that review until the matching scope is complete. Specify the total cash price for the returned units. Keep financing, programme design/assurance, testing and the project allowance outside the direct return. An empty lot carries the original estimate.

For example, one complete matched station at a hypothetical **$14m** displaces one **$13m** allowance. The other 63 station allowances stay in the ledger. The $1m direct difference becomes **$1.326m** of installed capital after the existing 8% design, 2.5% testing and 20% project allowances. The comparison reports the fourteen revised scopes for discussion. A commercial comparison can change the estimated price. A change in physical design also requires re-evaluating the affected vehicle, civil, power and service interfaces before selecting it.
