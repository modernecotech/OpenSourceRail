## Resources and calendar

The standard-deck programme uses 3 pairs of opposed launching gantries and 2 road teams. It needs 6 simultaneous construction flatbeds and up to 8 concurrently staffed yards with 128 moulds and 28.3 hectares of hardstand and yard area. Open routes use deck-fed construction where assumed. The road teams and shared yards cover the isolated packages.

The permanent railway uses 1,486.55 track-km. The construction method also carries 640.56 km of temporary feed track, which is recovered. It models 15,618 loaded public-road beam trips and 64,240 erection lane-hours. 32 residual bays still need design.

Relative calendar days with independent road teams and two-end launcher pairs. Each road team reserves one casting yard and each deck job reserves two terminal yards. Terminal yards are relocated by work package and the model retains mobilisation and site setup for every package. ## Open work before adopting a programme

- Identify approved land, utilities, gradients, site drainage, foundations and local construction access.
- Measure bridge, grade separation, station transition, river and special span quantities and quote whole packages.
- Resolve station deck/foundation boundaries and temporary running continuity from measured station footprints.
- Approve capacity and junctions for any genuinely shared guideway. Coincidence here is identical mapped vertices with no proximity merge.
- Resolve physical gaps created by shared-edge allocation. A road work package uses its net length-equivalent.
- Check terminal and shared yard land, crane chart at working radius, carrier/gantry deck loading, traffic windows and shifts. The retained loaded-flatbed mean axle exceeds the high operating maximum.
- Obtain schedules for the stations, utility diversions, track, depot, power, systems, checks and passenger opening.
- Timetable conflicts, fleet rotations, depot connections and capacity
- Utility connections, electrical protection and grid failure cases
- Station and special civil designs, property access and coordinated systems labour
